# India campaign authoring (T293.4 / #1840)

Brief and owner decisions (2026-11-16, approved): [docs/design/india-campaigns.md](../../docs/design/india-campaigns.md). The campaigns use a new 272x320 authored map (60E-94E, 5N-37N at 8x10 tiles per degree) built from this folder's committed subsets; the shared and per-opening terrain sidecars, worlds, rulesets and (listed) scenarios are generated by `build_campaigns.py`.

## What exists

- `sources/geography.json` — the clipped Natural Earth v5.1.2 land/lake/river vectors for the new frame, byte-verified against the pinned Near East snapshot's recorded hashes (the Near East pipeline). At the 50m scale the Punjab's five rivers reduce to the Chenab and Sutlej lines; the provenance labels that approximation. Central Asia beyond the pass crests stays inside the rectangular frame as empty land: no seat, city or claim is authored there.
- `targets.py` lists the centre hints; `resolve_sites.py` is an authoring aid for centres new to the committed subsets and needs the ignored `pleiades_gis_data.zip` described in [tools/NearEast/README.md](../NearEast/README.md). It is not part of the reproducible build.
- `sources/sites.json` (75 curated records) and `sources/chronology.json` — regenerated by `prepare_sites.py` from `cities.csv` plus the archive snapshot. The archive's South Asia coverage is sparse: about half the centres are **author geographic hints** (regional=true, coordinates from `targets.py`, dated context from `sources/chronology.json` — the Madaktu pattern), each range labelled "author-interpreted contextual range".
- `build_campaigns.py` — the reproducible build: the authored relief bands (Himalaya front, Hindu Kush edge, Sulaimans, Aravalli, Vindhya-Satpura, both Ghats), plain passes (Khyber and Bolan corridors, the Narmada gap, the Naneghat and Kanara crossings, the Palghat gap), the Thar desert box, and the shared terrain sidecar `data/worlds/india.terrain.b64` plus per-opening derived sidecars `india-{325,185}bc.terrain.b64` (shared map plus city tiles and landing aprons — the Persia pattern, bounded diff).

## Curated city list

`cities.csv` carries 75 centres with columns `id, pleiades, caption325, owner325, caption185, owner185, population, regional`; an empty caption/owner pair means the centre is not in that opening. Owners and populations are **designed abstractions** (the two approved 15-seat lists; seat ids as in the brief).

- **Founded after an opening is excluded, not relabelled:** the Tamil ports' excavated phases (Arikamedu, Muziris, Kanchipuram) begin after 325 BC and enter at 185 only; Kotalingala's coin evidence begins after 325; the later Indo-Greek and Kushan capitals (Purushapura, Nalanda, Sanchi-as-a-city) are absent.
- **Alexander's foundations** (Bucephala, Nicaea on the Hydaspes, Alexandria on the Indus) exist at 325 by construction, inside the seats the brief grants them.
- **Sites that changed their town:** the Greek-named Sangala (Euthymedeia) presents as Sagala; Korkai/Kolchoi and Dharanikota/Pitoura carry the period's own names.
- **No Aetolia-style gaps:** every approved seat opens with at least one centre — Abhisara, Andhra (325), Audumbara, Kuninda, Kuntala (185) and the Punjab republics are deliberately one-to-three-centre seats, labelled as the sources' thin documentation.
- **The delta is a labelled abstraction:** the Gangaridai country's towns carry Magadha at 325 and Sunga at 185 by design; the sources' Gangaridai distinction is noted in the brief.

## Build

`python tools/India/prepare_sites.py` then `python tools/India/build_campaigns.py` writes the shared sidecar, the two derived sidecars and `data/worlds/india-{325,185}bc.json`, `data/rulesets/india-*.json`, `data/scenarios/india-*.json` (twice-run byte-identical). `--terrain-only` prepares just the shared map and PNG. Scenarios are `listed: true` with guide entries in `data/campaign-guides/` and sections in `docs/campaign-guides.md`. Period unit labels (approved): Longbowmen, Spearmen, Skirmishers, Light horse, Heavy horse.

Tests: `IndiaCampaignTests` (load, validation, native round trip, 200 real AI seat turns on two seeds per opening, fleet orders, barbarian arrival, embark/landing, free approaches, dated evidence with the exclusions above, approved opening wars, per-opening derived-sidecar diff against the shared map, pinned per-seat counts, default seats agreeing with the guides) and `IndiaRoutesTests` (the cross-range route check — the Khyber and Bolan corridors, the Ganges-Vindhya routes, the Deccan crossings — plus every authored pass line carrying a cheap continuous corridor). Godot: `Checks/IndiaCampaignCheck.tscn` (New Game flow, capital framing, period captions, native load path), and `MapFitCheck` gained the 272x320 world.
