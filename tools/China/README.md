# China campaign authoring (T293.5 / #1841)

Brief and owner decisions (2026-11-17, approved as drafted): [docs/design/china-campaigns.md](../../docs/design/china-campaigns.md). The campaigns use a new 208x220 authored map (98E-124E, 22N-44N at 8x10 tiles per degree) built from this folder's committed subsets; the shared and per-opening terrain sidecars, worlds, rulesets and (listed) scenarios are generated by `build_campaigns.py`.

## What exists

- `sources/geography.json` — the clipped Natural Earth v5.1.2 land/lake/river vectors for the new frame, byte-verified against the pinned Near East snapshot's recorded hashes (the Near East pipeline). At the 50m scale the Wei, Fen and Huai lines are absent — the Huang He line and the Huai basin's lakes carry those valleys, and the provenance labels that approximation. Tibet's highlands, the deep steppe and Lingnan stay inside the rectangular frame as empty land: no seat, city or claim is authored there (Taiwan's coast likewise).
- `targets.py` lists the centre hints; `resolve_sites.py` is an authoring aid for centres new to the committed subsets and needs the ignored `pleiades_gis_data.zip` described in [tools/NearEast/README.md](../NearEast/README.md). It is not part of the reproducible build. The archive carries no Warring States settlement records, so every centre stays an author geographic hint.
- `sources/sites.json` (79 curated records) and `sources/chronology.json` — regenerated by `prepare_sites.py` from `cities.csv` plus the archive snapshot. Every centre is an **author geographic hint** (regional=true, coordinates from `targets.py`, dated context from `sources/chronology.json` — the Madaktu pattern India used for its sparse South Asia coverage), each range labelled "author-interpreted contextual range".
- `build_campaigns.py` — the reproducible build: the authored relief bands (the Qinling-Daba front, the Taihang wall, the Nan Ling, the Wushan and Funiu belts, the Zhongtiao line, the Shandong and Liaodong hills, the western highland edge), plain passes (the Hangu-Xiao corridor, Wu Pass route, the Zhongtiao crossing, Jingxing and the Taihang south crossing, the Jinniu road, the Yangtze gorges, the Fangcheng gap, the Huai corridor, the two Nan Ling gaps — the *Lüshi chunqiu*'s nine *sai* informing the pass plan), the Ordos desert box, and the shared terrain sidecar `data/worlds/china.terrain.b64` plus per-opening derived sidecars `china-{350,230}bc.terrain.b64` (shared map plus city tiles and landing aprons — the Persia pattern, bounded diff).

## Curated city list

`cities.csv` carries 79 centres with columns `id, pleiades, caption350, owner350, caption230, owner230, population, regional`; an empty caption/owner pair means the centre is not in that opening (Didao, Daliang and the three steppe courts are 230-only). Owners and populations are **designed abstractions** (the two approved seat lists — 15 regular at c.350, 11 at 230; seat ids as in the brief; Wey of Puyang is distinct from Wei of Liang).

- **Founded after an opening is excluded, not relabelled:** Daliang enters at 230 only (Wei's court move is the labelled Anyi/Daliang question); Didao's Longxi seat is a later-Warring-States phenomenon; the steppe court seats exist in the 230 roster alone.
- **Sites that changed their town:** Ying presents as Jiangling after 278 BC; Chen falls to Qin with the court's move to Shouchun; Lingshou and Quyang pass to Zhao with Zhongshan's 296 BC end; Luoyang, Gong, Chenggao and Mengjin pass to Qin with the Zhou end in 249 BC.
- **No seat opens empty:** every approved seat holds at least one centre — Lu, Wey, Yue and the three steppe courts are deliberately one-to-three-centre seats, labelled as the sources' thin documentation.
- **The steppe courts are designed abstractions:** Longcheng, the Donghu court and the Yuezhi court rest on the *Shiji*'s retrospective account of Modu's rise, written about a century later — edge seats at the frame's margin, labelled.

## Build

`python tools/China/prepare_sites.py` then `python tools/China/build_campaigns.py` writes the shared sidecar, the two derived sidecars and `data/worlds/china-{350,230}bc.json`, `data/rulesets/china-*.json`, `data/scenarios/china-*.json` (twice-run byte-identical). `--terrain-only` prepares just the shared map and PNG. Scenarios are `listed: true` with guide entries in `data/campaign-guides/` and sections in `docs/campaign-guides.md`. Period unit labels (approved): Crossbowmen, Halberdiers, Skirmishers, Light horse, Chariots.

Tests: `ChinaCampaignTests` (load, validation, native round trip, 200 real AI seat turns on two seeds per opening, fleet orders, barbarian arrival, embark/landing, free approaches, dated evidence with the exclusions above, approved opening wars, per-opening derived-sidecar diff against the shared map, pinned per-seat counts, default seats agreeing with the guides) and `ChinaRoutesTests` (the cross-range route check — the Hangu/Xiao corridor, Wu Pass, the Zhongtiao fords, the Taihang crossings, the Jinniu road and the gorges, the Fangcheng gap and the Huai corridor — plus every authored pass line carrying a cheap continuous corridor). Godot: `Checks/ChinaCampaignCheck.tscn` (New Game flow, capital framing, period captions, native load path), and `MapFitCheck` carries the new 208x220 world.
