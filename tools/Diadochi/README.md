# Diadochi campaign authoring (T293.1 / #1837)

Brief and owner decisions: [docs/design/diadochi-campaigns.md](../../docs/design/diadochi-campaigns.md). The campaigns reuse the Near East revision-2 terrain; this folder will hold the authoring script for the 323 BC and 301 BC worlds. The worlds, rulesets and (unlisted) scenarios are generated by `build_campaigns.py`.

## What exists

`targets.py` lists about 125 hoped-for centres with approximate coordinates (my own geographic knowledge, labelled as hints, not evidence). `resolve_sites.py` matches each to a Pleiades place that carries a matching name **and** lies within 0.6 degrees of the hint, writing `rendered/near-east-sources/diadochi-resolved.json` (ignored). It needs the ignored `pleiades_gis_data.zip` described in [tools/NearEast/README.md](../NearEast/README.md).

## Curated city list (frozen draft)

`cities.csv` has 146 centres with columns `id, pleiades, caption323, owner323, caption301, owner301, population, regional`. Owners and populations are **designed abstractions** (15 seats per opening, seat ids as in the brief); an empty caption/owner means the centre does not exist at that opening.

- **City records, not monuments:** the temple/monument stand-ins (Damascus, Jerusalem, Aleppo, Petra, Athens, Corinth, Thebes, Olympia, Eretria, Messene, Delos, Troy, Assur, Miletus, Pergamon, Priene, Thessalonica) now use the settlement record found by title and type.
- **Absent at the opening:** Thebes in Greece (destroyed 335, refounded 316) and Thessalonica (315) only appear in 301; Seleucia on the Tigris (about 305) only in 301; Edessa only in 301. Lysimacheia is "Kardia" and Cassandreia "Potidaia" (regional) in 323. Antioch (300) is not included in either opening.
- **No Pleiades record:** Thapsacus, Tushpa and Armavir have a blank `pleiades` and `regional=true`; `prepare_sites.py` takes their coordinates from `targets.py` and labels them as hints (only Thapsacus and Armavir remain).
- **Thin seats and dating:** `prepare_sites.py` rejects any centre without dated evidence at an opening it is used in. The Bronze/Iron Age sites first added for the inland seats (Giyan, Godin, Sialk, Qalaichi, Haftavan, Marlik, Bozkurt, Kanesh, Alisar) had none at 323 or 301 BC and were removed; they are replaced by centres with Pleiades date bounds covering both years (Taoke, Konkobar, Maragha, Zanjan, Kybistra, Kassope, Bouthroton, Kalydon, Oiniadai, Piraeus, Eleusis, Lindos). Pydna, Colophon, Thapsacus and Armavir use labelled contextual ranges in `sources/chronology.json` (Wikipedia overviews, author-interpreted). Tushpa uses Pleiades 874771. Still small: Armenia 3, Rhodes 3, Epirus 4. Viability is judged in the acceptance runs.
- **Contested holdings at 301:** Coele-Syria (Gaza, Jerusalem, Samaria, Ascalon, Joppa, Akko) is given to Egypt; Lycia and Pamphylia are given to Egypt as a disputed coast; Caria and Cilicia to Pleistarchus ("Cilicia" seat). One consistent snapshot, labelled in the scenario brief.
- **Dating is not done.** The Near East `sources/chronology.json` pattern (labelled contextual ranges with a cited source) is the model for the Hellenistic centres.

## Build

`python tools/Diadochi/prepare_sites.py` then `python tools/Diadochi/build_campaigns.py` writes `data/worlds/diadochi-{323,301}bc.json`, `data/rulesets/diadochi-*.json`, `data/scenarios/diadochi-*.json` and **`data/worlds/diadochi.terrain.b64`**. The sidecar is the shared `near-east-r2` map plus 35 cells (city tiles and landing aprons at the new coastal centres), so it is a separate derived file; the shared r2 sidecar is not touched, and no relief changes. Scenarios are `listed: false` until the guide entries exist. Tests: `DiadochiCampaignTests` (load, validation, seat/capital checks, native round trip, 200 real AI seat turns on two seeds, fleet orders, barbarian arrival, embark/landing, free approaches, dated evidence, derived-sidecar diff).
