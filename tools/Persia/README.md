# Persia campaign authoring (T293.2 / #1838)

Brief and owner decisions (2026-11-15): [docs/design/persia-campaigns.md](../../docs/design/persia-campaigns.md). The campaigns reuse the Near East revision-2 terrain and the Diadochi authoring pattern; this folder holds the authoring script for the c.550 BC and 480 BC worlds. The worlds, rulesets and (listed) scenarios are generated by `build_campaigns.py`.

## What exists

`targets.py` lists about 150 centres with approximate coordinates (hints, not evidence); the Diadochi subset's committed records carry most Pleiades ids, so `resolve_sites.py` is an authoring aid for centres new to the committed subsets (Sais, the Thracian coast, the small Peloponnesian and Ionian towns, Thermopylae), not part of the reproducible build. It needs the ignored `pleiades_gis_data.zip` described in [tools/NearEast/README.md](../NearEast/README.md).

## Curated city list (frozen draft)

`cities.csv` has 134 centres with columns `id, pleiades, caption550, owner550, caption480, owner480, population, regional`. Owners and populations are **designed abstractions** (15 seats per opening, seat ids as in the brief); an empty caption/owner pair means the centre is not in that opening.

- **Smyrna is absent from both openings**: Alyattes destroyed it around 600 BC and Antigonus refounded it after 320 BC — the archive's coarse interval spans the openings, the occupation does not (the Near East rule against inserting abandoned phases). Hasanlu (destroyed about 800 BC), Konkobar and Nisibis are likewise not used; Amisos is 480 only (founded about 560 BC), Cybistra 550 only (its dated rows do not reach 480).
- **Founded after the opening:** Persepolis (begun about 515 BC) is present only at 480; Pella (Archelaus, about 400 BC) and Piraeus (Themistocles, 493 BC) only at 480; Messene (371 BC), Alexandria and Antioch at neither opening. Pasargadae (begun about 546 BC) is Persis' capital at 550 with the royal court as a labelled designed abstraction.
- **Per-capia records, not monuments:** the temple/monument stand-ins the Diadochi subset warned about (Corinth, Thebes, Jerusalem, Damascus, Miletus, Priene, Pergamon) use the settlement records the committed subsets already carry. Cnidus uses the archaic Burgaz location (599576), not the Hellenistic Tekir record; Klazomenai and Paphos use the later-site records (their archaic sites are a few kilometres off, noted here as a location imprecision, not a dating one).
- **No Pleiades record:** Madaktu has a record without coordinates or dated rows; it is an author hint (`regional=true`, coordinates in `targets.py`) used only at 550, with a labelled contextual range in `sources/chronology.json`. Mycenae (the Argolid town Herodotus lists at Thermopylae) and Pydna carry labelled contextual ranges from overviews.
- **Seats and dating:** the contested openings follow the brief: at 480 the Mesopotamian and Levantine towns are Persia's own holdings (no Babylon seat); the Greek central towns (Thebes, Plataia, Chalcis, Eretria) appear only in 480, where their seats exist; Corinth and the Isthmus towns carry the Spartan league abstraction at 550 and the Corinth seat at 480; the Argolid appears only at 480. Naxos and Delos are Peisistratid clients at 550 (labelled) and Persian-subjected islands at 480.

## Build

`python tools/Persia/prepare_sites.py` then `python tools/Persia/build_campaigns.py` writes `data/worlds/persia-{550,480}bc.json`, `data/rulesets/persia-*.json`, `data/scenarios/persia-*.json` and **`data/worlds/persia.terrain.b64`**. The sidecar is the shared `near-east-r2` map plus 30 cells (city tiles and landing aprons at the new coastal centres), so it is a separate derived file; the shared r2 sidecar is not touched, and no relief changes. Scenarios are `listed: true` with guide entries in `data/campaign-guides/`.

**Source snapshot note:** the Near East and Diadochi subsets were extracted from a pinned archive snapshot (entries dated 2026-10-30, SHA-256 in their `sites.json`). That snapshot is not re-downloadable, so this subset was extracted from a current snapshot (date and SHA-256 in `sources/sites.json`); the shared records' coordinates and bounds were diffed against the committed subsets during review.

Tests: `PersiaCampaignTests` (load, validation, seat/capital checks, native round trip, 200 real AI seat turns on two seeds per year, fleet orders, barbarian arrival, embark/landing, free approaches, dated evidence, approved opening wars, derived-sidecar diff, pinned per-seat counts, default seats agreeing with the guides) and `PersiaRoutesTests` (the cross-range route check: Zagros, Elburz, Taurus and the 480 Greek theatre, production costs). Godot: `Checks/PersiaCampaignCheck.tscn` (New Game flow, capital framing, period captions, native load path).
