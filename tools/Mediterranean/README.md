# Mediterranean campaign authoring (T293.3 / #1839)

Brief and owner decisions (2026-11-16): [docs/design/mediterranean-campaigns.md](../../docs/design/mediterranean-campaigns.md). The campaigns use a new 400x220 authored map (10W-40E, 24N-46N at 8x10 tiles per degree) built from this folder's committed subsets; the shared and per-opening terrain sidecars, worlds, rulesets and (listed) scenarios are generated by `build_campaigns.py`.

## What exists

- `sources/geography.json` — the clipped Natural Earth v5.1.2 land/lake/river vectors for the new frame, byte-verified against the pinned Near East snapshot's recorded hashes (the Near East pipeline).
- `targets.py` lists the centre hints; `resolve_sites.py` is an authoring aid for centres new to the committed subsets and needs the ignored `pleiades_gis_data.zip` described in [tools/NearEast/README.md](../NearEast/README.md). It is not part of the reproducible build.
- `sources/sites.json` (178 curated records) and `sources/chronology.json` — regenerated by `prepare_sites.py` from `cities.csv` plus the archive snapshot (date and SHA-256 in `sites.json`; the Near East and Diadochi subsets predate it, and the shared records' coordinates and bounds were diffed against them during review).
- `build_campaigns.py` — the reproducible build: the authored relief bands, plain passes and open straits (`RIDGES`/`PASSES`/`STRAITS`, per the terrain-authoring rule), the shared sidecar `data/worlds/mediterranean.terrain.b64`, and per-opening derived sidecars `mediterranean-{431,280,218}bc.terrain.b64` (shared map plus city tiles and landing aprons — the Persia pattern, bounded diff; 54/51/49 cells).

## Curated city list

`cities.csv` carries 178 centres with columns `id, pleiades, caption431, owner431, caption280, owner280, caption218, owner218, population, regional`; an empty caption/owner pair means the centre is not in that opening. Owners and populations are **designed abstractions** (the three approved 15-seat lists; seat ids as in the brief).

- **Founded after an opening is excluded, not relabelled:** Alexandria (331 BC) and Carthago Nova (Hasdrubal's Qart Hadasht, 227 BC — Iberia enters Carthage's seat only at 218) carry no 431/280 era; the Roman colonies founded in 218 (Placentia, Cremona) and Aquileia are absent.
- **The synoecistic city of Rhodes** (about 408 BC) exists only at 280 and 218 as the seat's capital; the island's older towns (Lindos, Kamiros, Ialysos) carry the 431 opening under the Delian League abstraction.
- **Sites that changed their town:** the Pleiades records spanning two towns present the period's own town — Potidaia at 431 and Kassandreia later; Kardia throughout, on the record whose later name is Lysimacheia.
- **No Aetolian seat at 431:** Thermon, Kalydon and Oiniadai carry no 431 era; the region's coastal town Naupaktos belongs to Corinth's seat that year.
- **By 218 the Samnite and Lucanian centres stand inside Rome's alliance system** (Bovianum, Saepinum, Compsa, Aesernia, Grumentum, Petelia with `rome` as owner); the approved 218 seat list has neither highland seat, and both exist only at 280.
- **Passes stay in frame:** the Brenner line (about 46.6N-47.0N) lies north of the frame edge and carved nothing, so it is not in the data; the in-frame Alpine crossing is Mont Genevre. The Sicilian interior line ends on the Katane plain (its earlier endpoint fell in the sea).

## Build

`python tools/Mediterranean/prepare_sites.py` then `python tools/Mediterranean/build_campaigns.py` writes the shared sidecar, the three derived sidecars and `data/worlds/mediterranean-{431,280,218}bc.json`, `data/rulesets/mediterranean-*.json`, `data/scenarios/mediterranean-*.json` (twice-run byte-identical). `--terrain-only` prepares just the shared map and PNG. Scenarios are `listed: true` with guide entries in `data/campaign-guides/` and sections in `docs/campaign-guides.md`. Period unit labels: 431 names the era's identities (Hoplites, Peltasts); 280/218 keep the neutral mixed-era labels the Persia campaigns shipped.

Tests: `MediterraneanCampaignTests` (load, validation, seat/capital checks, native round trip, 200 real AI seat turns on two seeds per year, fleet orders, barbarian arrival, embark/landing, free approaches, dated evidence with the exclusions above, approved opening wars, per-opening derived-sidecar diff against the shared map, pinned per-seat counts, default seats agreeing with the guides) and `MediterraneanRoutesTests` (the cross-range route check — Hannibal's 218 corridor, the Pyrenean crossings, the Greek corridors — plus the authored pass lines carrying a cheap continuous corridor). Godot: `Checks/MediterraneanCampaignCheck.tscn` (New Game flow, capital framing, period captions, native load path), and `MapFitCheck` gained the 400x220 world.
