# Original assets: what the next image slices need (T292b, #1850)

**Scope.** This page is input for the remaining T292b slices: rivers, city and capital markers, army and fleet markers, battle pieces, glyphs and the Area map. It lists the facts and the consumer changes each one needs. It does not decide them; the open questions are at the end. Sounds and the six terrain tiles are built already ([measurement](original-assets-measurement.md), release plan §2.8 C5). Every source is a component inside the user's own `Imperial Conquest 2.exe`, read locally into the user-data cache. Nothing original enters git, a release or Pages.

## Storage rules that apply to every image category

- **Image lists** (`TImageList` on `TUnitMap`, `TBattleMap`, `TAreaMap`) store `[u32 colour-BMP length][u32 count][8-bit colour BMP][1-bit mask BMP]`. Cells are read row-major from the top left, with columns = sheet width / cell width; do not assume four columns (S point 2). The T292b terrain reader already does this for `TerrainList`.
- **Masks**: their polarity and AND/XOR behaviour are **not established** (S point 4). `PointsList` and `MapsList` masks are fully opaque `[measured]` (G §D). Stored city images equal the Wine drawing (U: 80 of 80 inside the 2 px border, 74 of 80 on the whole tile). Until a synthetic truth-table test exists, draw a cell as an opaque 32×32 tile and do not derive alpha from its mask.
- **Speed-button glyphs** (`<Form>.Panel1.<component>.Glyph.Data`) are 4-bit BMPs with the standard 16-colour Windows palette and no mask. The transparent colour is the sheet's **bottom-left pixel**: alpha 0 exactly where the palette index equals that pixel's index (G §B, `[confirmed: code]`). The key is index 11 (yellow) on 43 sheets, 15 (white) on 19 and 13 (magenta) on one. White inside a white-keyed glyph is transparent too.
- **Nation recolour** (armies, fleets, battle units only): purple `#800080` → nation `+0x424`, white `#ffffff` → `+0x428`, blue `#0000ff` → `+0x42C`. Classify each pixel once, then substitute, and keep every other pixel (S point 5). The remake's `BattleIconTint.Recolour` already maps per pixel in one pass, and the classical world's `battleColorsHex` equals those three dwords (asset-specification §2).
- **City and capital art and nation glyphs are never recoloured** (S point 6; U).

## Open questions for the owner (not decided here)

1. **Rivers**: in Classic tiles with original tiles, draw the six original river tiles in place of the plain-plus-stroke, or keep the stroke over them?
2. **Towns and capitals in other worlds**: in non-classical worlds, should original town art be shown at all (S: "custom nations unavailable unless a supported mapping is evidenced")? If yes, which nation takes which original owner picture? The recommended default is authored art outside the classical world.
3. **Glyph size**: draw the 20×20 glyphs padded at 1:1, or scale them (nearest-neighbour) to the toolbar's 24/32 px?
4. **Raster-operation pieces** (battle cursor, strategic selection, Area-map points): leave them authored, or build raster-op consumers for them?
5. **Area map**: keep the remake's baked map, use `MapsList` for the classical world only, or apply the original's eight palette colours to every world, with or without a Toggle colour command (#723 A01)? The last option needs no original bytes.
6. **Scope**: release plan §2.8 lists "the remaining original-game images (#1798, #1850)" as **not in v0.11.0**. If they are to ship in v0.11, the plan and claim C5 need an owner-approved change.
