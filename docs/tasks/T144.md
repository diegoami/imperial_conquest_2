- **Hazards**:
  - **The flag is the session's, not the command's.** The human trigger lives in `GameSession`'s `move`, outside `MoveArmyCommandHandler` (T23 put it there). Don't move it into the handler: the AI's moves go through the handler too, and would start or stop resupplying.
  - **A human army can still resupply under `classical-faithful`**, through the Supply army dialog (T134) and buying supply, as in the original. Nothing here touches those.
  - **The exporter writes the one flag.** If it cannot do so without breaking `ExportScriptReproducibilityTests` or `ExportScriptToyGuardTests`, stop and report; don't change the toy's value to get round it.
