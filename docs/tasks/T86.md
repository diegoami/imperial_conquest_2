- **Scope**: The source for every rule below is research [`decompiled-elimination-cleanup.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/decompiled-elimination-cleanup.md) §4 (research `43a44a1`, **[confirmed: decompile]**), with the neighbour merge from [`dat-neighbour-mask.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/dat-neighbour-mask.md).
  - **When conquest fires.** The engine eliminates a nation only when it loses its last city. The original conquers it much earlier, in `FUN_0044BB18` (:50150):
    - When a **non-capital** capture leaves the loser with **fewer than 6 cities**, the loser is conquered.
    - When the **capital** falls and the loser has **unity > 400 and more than 6 cities**, it tries to move its capital (`FUN_0044BD2C`, :50234): unity −50, and the news line "*<X> have moved their capital to <city>.*" when a city more than 10 tiles away exists.
    - When the capital falls and is not moved, the loser is conquered.
  - **What conquest does**, in `FUN_0044C528` (:50602), in order:
    1. Every loser city goes to the winner. The loyalty formula applies (`min(80, 120 − L)` if the city's allegiance is the winner's, else `min(70, max(40, 100 − L))`, then `+ Random(6)`), and so do the winner's per-city credits.
    2. The winner's unity becomes `min(990, unity + 50)`.
    3. If the loser's treasury is positive, the winner gains it. The loser's own treasury is **not** zeroed.
    4. The relation reset (T69's helper) runs, and the neighbour merge: every nation that bordered the loser, except the winner, becomes a neighbour of the winner, both ways.
    5. The loser's forces are disposed of (T84's helper), with the winner receiving fleets under construction.
    6. The news banner is written: a dashed line, "*<winner> conquers <loser>.*", a dashed line.
    7. The loser's conquered-by is set to the winner, its unity to 0, and its capital to the no-capital sentinel.
    8. All 40 recruitment slots' troops are set to 0.
  - **Defection** (`FUN_0044BED8`'s elimination block) does less. It writes no conquest news, no treasury change and no capital sentinel. It sets conquered-by to the receiver. The engine's `Defect` path today publishes `NationConquered`, clears the capital and gives a human unity 0; correct it to the original (#368 item 7).
  - **Capture and defection loyalty are formulas, not constants** (research [`decompiled-quarterly-rebellion.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/decompiled-quarterly-rebellion.md) §5, research `235af11`, **[confirmed: decompile + 8 cascade defections and 5 captures in saves]**):
    - A **defection** sets loyalty to `min(90, 140 − L)` if the receiver is the city's allegiance nation, and `min(65, max(50, 100 − L))` otherwise. The engine's constants 90 and 65 are wrong for 6 of the 8 cascade defections in the saves: Aradus, Hemesa, Palmyra, Modena and Acroinon went to 50, and Synnada went from 62 to 78.
    - A **forced capture** sets `max(40, min(60, 100 − L′))`, or `min(90, 140 − L′)` for the allegiance nation, where `L′` is the loyalty after siege erosion. The engine's constant 40 is wrong for Mediolanum 43, Felsina 41, Brixia 44, Byblos 55 and Gordium 49.
    - A defection removes only the old owner's recruitment slots at the city that hold troops. Slots with 0 troops stay. The engine removes every slot at the city.
  - **Not here.** A human loser's seat handover, the "Your nation has been conquered" screen, and rebirth are T87's.

  7. **Seeded outcomes.** Every seeded test, measurement or golden that changes is listed with its reason. - **Hazards**:
  - **The model changes.** "Eliminated" now arrives while a nation still holds up to 5 cities, and every one of them moves in the same step. T69's relation reset, T84's force disposal and T83's lost-seat fallback all key on `JustEliminated`. Keep them firing exactly once, in the report's order, and probe with two nations.
  - **Quirks.**
    - The original copies the treasury instead of moving it, which creates money. Reproduce it, and name it in the remark.
    - The original never decrements the loser's city count, wealth or tax base, which leaves stale values. The engine derives counts from ownership, so that quirk has nothing to act on. Say so in the remark rather than inventing a stale field.
  - **Victory.** The original's victory screen fires when the winner holds more than 333 cities. Check that the engine's victory rule agrees. If it doesn't, report it rather than fixing it here.
  - **Tests built on last-city elimination** (T69's, T84's and T83's real-elimination tests) may need a setup where the loser holds a single city, which still conquers. Change setup only.
