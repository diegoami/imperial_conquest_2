# T269 — Historical Near East campaign source and mechanics brief

Issue [#1704](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/1704),
supporting #1308, #1681 and #1682. Own this contract,
`docs/design/near-east-campaigns.md` and the task-catalogue entry.

Turn the owner's historical c1350 BC and c750 BC choices into a cited campaign
brief before authoring data. Reuse existing movement, supply, combat and troop
archetypes; no new chariot combat or stronger Computer general. Distinguish
historical facts, approximate identifications and deliberate grouping/balance.
Reserve a roster slot for the approved barbarian mechanic, including sea arrivals.

Done when both periods have a source-backed roster and geography brief,
explicit standard-mechanics mapping, source/licence requirements for terrain
and cities, and implementation acceptance. Do not certify the playable
campaigns, close their delivery issues or invent precise borders and leaders
from a general period overview. No original-game data, manual or art is copied.
