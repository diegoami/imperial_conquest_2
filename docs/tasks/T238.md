# T238 — A human move order walks a second time

Triage of #1585, owner decision 2026-10-30. After a human army's or fleet's move order, the original calls its walker once. If the army or fleet is still short of the target with moves left, it calls the walker once more from the new position, on a fresh line (`TUnitMap_MoveHumanArmy` `0x00446D9C`, `TUnitMap_MoveHumanFleet` `0x00446E24`). The remake walks once, and its single walk equals the original's first call step for step. Evidence: [`2026-10-29-walk-stops-at-own-city-on-line230.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/f02d219/docs/reports/2026-10-29-walk-stops-at-own-city-on-line230.md) (armies, three replayed cases) and [`2026-10-30-paths-in-play-and-fleet-walks42.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/f02d219/docs/reports/2026-10-30-paths-in-play-and-fleet-walks42.md) (fleets: an emulator run of 75 targets and two live cases). These are Wine and emulator results; native Windows confirmation is still open.

## Contract

- **Human orders only.** The second walk applies to a move order from a human seat: the map click and the CLI's `move` and `move-fleet` for a human active seat. AI orders keep a single walk. The AI army's own continuation is #1592 (research#105 answered it; its correction task follows this one); the AI fleet's continuation is unestablished. The second walk must not reach the AI through the shared handlers. Carry it as an explicit walk policy on the command (`MoveWalkPolicy`, which #1592's AI continuation can extend), set where `GameSession` builds the human order, never inferred inside the handler from seat control.
- **Armies** (`MoveArmyCommandHandler`) and **fleets** (`MoveFleetCommandHandler`): when the first walk ends short of the destination with moves left, walk once more from the new position toward the same destination with the remaining moves. There are never more than two walks. A second walk that makes no progress changes nothing further.
- **The move is one order.** Report it once, with its total moves spent and its final cell. Strategic travel shows both walks as one route: the second walk's cells follow the first's, with no stop or duplicate travel record between them. Keep the end-of-walk contact rules, `MovementAbortRule` and T237's refusals unchanged; the refusals run once, before the first walk.
- **Saves, rules and UI** are otherwise unchanged. No ruleset flag: the original does this in every case.

## Owns and done when

The two move handlers, their command records, `GameSession`'s `move`/`move-fleet` builders, and focused tests. For armies and for fleets, prove:
- a first walk ending short at a marker, then a second walk that reaches the target (the Venusia case: (115,49), 1 move left);
- a first walk ending short on an unaffordable step;
- a second walk that is blocked as well;
- an AI order that still walks once;
- one travel record and one receipt per order.

Update the CLI goldens only where a move now walks twice, and say which ones. Strategic travel checks, builds and hosted CI must be green. Closes #1585.
