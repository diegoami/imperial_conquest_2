#### T136 Disbanding a regular unit lowers the nation's mobilisation

- **Design milestone**: M14 (army management; T107's per-unit disband). It is a correction for bug [#631](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/631), found by the targeted research pass of 2026-10-03. The user decided on 2026-10-05 that it blocks `v0.5.0` ([build-process.md §4.8](../build-process.md#48-the-playability-gate-until-v050)); the v0.5.0 triage of that day made it a correction task, because it changes a rule's outcome (a nation's mobilisation), so it is not the fix lane ([§4.10](../build-process.md#410-the-fix-lane)). **Labels**: `phase:3 lane:engine release:v0.5.0`
- **Branch**: `task/T136-disband-lowers-mobilisation`, from `origin/main` · **Model/effort**: Sonnet / Low ([§3.3](../build-process.md#33-model-selection): not architecture, so the OpenCode default, then Sonnet) · **Reviewer**: the complex tier ([§3.4](../build-process.md#34-why-the-reviewers-model-differs-from-the-implementers)): GPT-6 Sol at `low`
- **Start after**: none · **Merge after**: none. Its files are in no other open task's Owns. It changes no CLI golden: the success script's one `disband-unit` (~:70) is its last order before `quit`, and nothing after it prints mobilisation.
- **Owns**:
  - `src/IC2.Engine/Armies/Commands/DisbandUnitCommandHandler.cs`: the accepted path (~:46–64), which also writes the nation;
  - `src/IC2.Engine/Armies/Commands/DisbandUnitCommand.cs`: **only** the remarks (~:5–48), the `RemoveUnit` paragraph among them;
  - `tests/IC2.Engine.Tests/Armies/DisbandUnitMobilizationTests.cs` (new).
- **Scope**:
  - **Protects**: the mobilisation rule stays `MobilizationRate`'s, read and never edited: the decrease is `MobilizationRate.AfterOrderCancelled(mobilized, troops, wealth, ruleset.Recruitment)`, with no new constant and no ruleset key. `disband-unit`'s refusals (unknown army, not your army, invalid index, last unit) and their order are unchanged; a refused order changes nothing. A mercenary unit's disband changes no nation field. `disband-army`, recruitment, `mobilize` and the AI are unchanged. Forbidden: an edit under `src/IC2.Engine/Recruitment/**`; a change to `tests/fixtures/corpus.json`, a ruleset or a CLI golden.
  - **The rule** **[confirmed: code, [`2026-10-03-army-to-army-ok-supply-rebalancing.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-03-army-to-army-ok-supply-rebalancing.md), "A disband inside the dialog changes mobilisation", `RemoveUnit` `:44466–44507`]**: a **regular** unit disbanded lowers its nation's mobilisation (nation `+0x442`, `NationState.MobilizedPercent`) by `troops × 1000 div wealth(+0x430) + 1`, floored at 0, the inverse of mobilising. That is exactly `MobilizationRate.AfterOrderCancelled`, whose formula is the cancellation one, `max(0, mobilized − 1 − troops × 1000 / wealth)`, with its step (`MobilizationRateOrderStep`, 1) and scale (`MobilizationRateWealthScale`, 1,000) from the ruleset and a term of 0 at zero wealth **[confirmed: [`decompiled-mobilization-and-mercenary-restock.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/decompiled-mobilization-and-mercenary-restock.md) §5]**. `troops` is the disbanded unit's own troop count; `wealth` is the issuing nation's `Wealth` at the order.
  - **A regular unit** is one with `UnitSlot.IsMercenary` false **[derived: the report reads the decrease for a regular unit only; the clone's regular-versus-mercenary test is `IsMercenary`]**. A mercenary unit's disband changes nothing but the army, as today.
  - **Which dialog.** The confirmed reading is the *Army to army transfer* dialog's Disband (`TArmyToArmy`, through `RemoveUnit`, committed at its `OK` step 1). The clone has one verb for both that dialog's Disband and *Change units*' (`TChangeArmyUnits`), and T111 composes `disband-unit` for both. Whether `TChangeArmyUnits_Disband` also lowers mobilisation is **unread** (#631, "Not established"); this task applies the rule to the one verb **[designed]**, because the clone cannot tell the two dialogs apart and the only read path lowers it. The original commits the dialog's value at `OK`; the clone's per-unit order commits at once, which gives the same nation value once the dialog closes.
  - **The remarks** of `DisbandUnitCommand` stop calling `RemoveUnit` a list-display helper: they cite the 2026-10-03 report for the decrease, say that the *Change units* path is unread and the rule is applied to it by design, and keep the no-refund and last-unit paragraphs.
- **Done when**:
  1. `DisbandUnitMobilizationTests`, on a scripted state: a nation at mobilisation 40 and wealth 768,000 disbands a regular unit of 15,000 troops; its `MobilizedPercent` becomes `40 − 1 − 15,000 × 1,000 div 768,000` = **20**, the unit is gone, and nothing else in the nation changes. It fails on `main` (mobilisation stays 40) and passes after.
  2. **The floor.** The same disband from mobilisation 5 leaves 0, not a negative value.
  3. **Zero wealth.** At wealth 0 the decrease is the step alone (mobilisation 40 → 39), and no exception is thrown.
  4. **A mercenary unit.** Disbanding a unit with `IsMercenary` true leaves `MobilizedPercent` and every other nation field unchanged.
  5. **The rule is `MobilizationRate`'s.** For three (mobilisation, troops, wealth) cases, the nation's new value equals `MobilizationRate.AfterOrderCancelled` on the same inputs, and `git diff origin/main -- src/IC2.Engine/Recruitment` is empty.
  6. **Refusals unchanged.** The existing `ChangeUnitsCommandTests` pass unchanged, and a refused `disband-unit` (last unit, not your army) leaves the nation's mobilisation unchanged.
  7. **Remarks.** `git grep -n -i 'list helper\|display helper' src/IC2.Engine/Armies/Commands/DisbandUnitCommand.cs` finds nothing, and the remarks name `RemoveUnit` and the 2026-10-03 report.
  8. `dotnet build IC2.sln --nologo -v q` and `dotnet test IC2.sln --nologo -v q` are green, `git diff origin/main -- tests/fixtures` is empty, and `git diff --name-only origin/main...HEAD` lists only Owns paths.
- **Hazards**:
  - **The dialog's own refusal is not this task's.** The same report confirms that the transfer dialog refuses to disband a regular unit unless an own city lies in the 3 × 3 around the army (*"An army must be near its own city to disband a regular unit."*, `:44253–44336`). The clone's `disband-unit` has no such refusal. It is a separate rule change, filed on its own by the main session after this plan merges, and not added here.
  - **`disband-army` is not this task's.** Whether `TUnitMap_DisbandArmy` lowers mobilisation is unread. Don't touch `DisbandArmyCommandHandler`.
  - **If the desktop disagrees** for the *Change units* path, that is a follow-up, not a reason to hold the task.
