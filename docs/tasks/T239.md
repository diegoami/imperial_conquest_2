# T239 — A faithful AI army march keeps walking until next to its target or out of moves

Correction for #1592. The original's AI army move, `FUN_0044dba8`, walks toward its target, or toward an intermediate point when the direct line fails. If that walk left the moves unchanged, it walks toward a fallback point and spends 1 move whatever happened. Then, while the army is more than 1 tile from the target and has moves left, it calls itself again from the new position, with a fresh line and a fresh intermediate point. The remake's faithful AI marches once: one `MoveArmyCommand` toward the target or one `ClassicalArmyNavigation.Intermediate` point, after which the army is not offered a second march (`AiMilitaryPhase.cs:307`). Over 48 ten-turn `classical-faithful` runs of v0.8.4, 1,406 of 5,011 AI marches (28.1%) ended more than 1 tile short with moves left. Evidence: [`2026-10-30-ai-march-continuation-missing-in-remake.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/b1ea901/docs/reports/2026-10-30-ai-march-continuation-missing-in-remake.md) (the wrapper, decompile and x86 replay), [`2026-10-30-ai-move-stale-word-in-play97.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/87d67ef/docs/reports/2026-10-30-ai-move-stale-word-in-play97.md) (the direct path continues in play: the stack word was never −1; Wine candidate) and [`2026-10-30-ai-move-fallback-point105.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/b1ea901/docs/reports/2026-10-30-ai-move-fallback-point105.md) (the fallback point, and the original's line test, waypoint finder and fallback equal the remake's `DirectLine`, `Intermediate` and `LocalFallback`; emulation, not observed in play).

Depends on T238, which owns the same handler and command record and adds the human second walk. Build T239 after T238 merges.

## Contract

- **Faithful AI marches only.** The continuation applies to an army march the AI issues where `ClassicalArmyNavigation.Applies`. Human orders keep T238's at-most-two walks; the designed AI keeps one walk. Carry it as an explicit property of the AI's order, set where the AI builds the march, never inferred inside the handler from seat control. It takes the march's **target** (the army or city the tree chose), not only the point aimed at, because the distance test and each fresh route use the target.
- **One level** of the original's wrapper, from the army's current cell `P`, toward target `T`:
  1. If `DirectLine(P, T)`, walk toward `T`. Otherwise take `Intermediate(P, T)`'s point and walk toward it. If `Intermediate` returns no point or throws `NotSupportedException`, the order ends here (the original's point is −1: no fallback, no further level).
  2. **No progress:** if the walk left the moves unchanged and still positive, walk toward `LocalFallback(P)` when it returns a point, then subtract 1 move whether or not a point was found. Moves may end at −1, as in the original; the next refill restores them.
  3. **Again:** while the Chebyshev distance from the army to `T` is more than 1 and moves are above 0, run another level from the new cell.
  - Use the AI's own walk rules for every walk, including the unaffordable step that zeroes the AI's moves (`MovementAbortRule`). There is no fixed level limit; step 2's cost ends the loop.
- **The direct path continues too.** On the original's direct-line path the two tests read a stack word that path never writes; in play it was never −1, so treat both paths alike.
- **Out of scope, unchanged:** a march whose origin and target lie in different `Classify` regions (the original calls a ferry lookup, `FUN_0044d9a8`, that the remake does not have) keeps today's single walk; an army aboard a fleet; the AI fleet's move (#1409); which marches the tree proposes, including the proposal gate that declines a march whose first step is blocked; the rule that an army marches once per turn.
- **The march is one order.** Report it once, with its total moves spent and final cell. Strategic travel shows all its walks as one route, with no stop or duplicate travel record between them. End-of-walk contact rules and T237's refusals are unchanged; the refusals run once, before the first walk.
- **Saves, rules and UI** are otherwise unchanged. No new ruleset flag: the faithful navigation already selects it.
