#### T128 The battle screen's assets: 15 recolourable unit icons, the ground tile, the cursor and the toolbar glyphs

- **Design milestone**: M21 (the tactical battle, v0.6.0 *Battles*, moved from v0.5.0 by the user's decision of 2026-10-04, [#496](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/496)). It adds the asset keys the battle screen (T127) draws, to both shipped packs, and the specification rows for them. **Labels**: `phase:3 lane:ui`
- **Branch**: `task/T128-battle-assets` · **Model/effort**: Sonnet / Medium · **Reviewer**: **Opus**/Medium **+ human visual review** (the tier rule decides; complex)
- **Start after**: T122 (the world's `battleColorsHex`) · **Merge after**: T122. T127 starts after this task. It is **never in flight with** another task that edits either pack's `manifest.json` or the generator scripts.
- **Owns**:
  - `docs/asset-specification.md`: **only** §4.7's bullet "The original's tactical battle screen" (rewritten as the key table below) and §6's key list (the new keys appended);
  - `assets/packs/placeholder/manifest.json` and `assets/packs/authored/manifest.json`: **only** the new `battle.*` keys;

- **Scope**:
  - **Protects**: that every key the battle screen draws exists in both packs and the drift test, with the size thresholds and three recolourable regions the decompile gives; and that no existing key, file or the strategic §4.1 icons change. Forbidden: a file taken from the original installation (no bitmap, resource or screenshot of the original enters a pack), an existing key renamed or redrawn.
  - **The inventory** **[confirmed: code and the `TBATTLEMAP` resource, static, [`2026-10-04-decompiled-tactical-battle-rules.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-04-decompiled-tactical-battle-rules.md) §10]**: `battle.unit.<type>.<small|medium|large>` (15 keys, 32 × 32, the size by `min(2, troops div (standardBattalionSize div 3))`); `battle.ground` (one grass tile); `battle.cursor`; `battle.toolbar.<unit_moves|friendly_units|enemy_units|cancel_selection|end_turn|change_pauses|computer_general|surrender>` (eight 20 × 20 glyphs) [designed: the key names, following §1.5's convention]. The pictures are the clone's own: the report's descriptions (a spear and sword for light infantry, a shield with crossed swords for heavy infantry, …) are a guide, not a copy.
  - **What the running game confirms** **[Wine candidate: [`2026-10-04-battle-probe.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-04-battle-probe.md) item 1, research `dee8150`]**: the battle window is 448 × 414, and a tooltip scan of its toolbar found the eight buttons in the order above (*Computer general on* being the seventh). The rewritten §4.7 bullet cites this beside the static source and drops the current bullet's "No current DoD needs these" and "not yet compared with the running game", which this task and the probe make stale. **The icons' size rule** was since read from the running game **[Wine candidate: [`2026-10-04-tactical-battle-sweep.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-04-tactical-battle-sweep.md) B8, research `87d1571`]**: on both sides a unit's icon changes size at exactly `std div 3` and `2 × (std div 3)` troops (light infantry 5,000 / 10,000, heavy infantry 2,000 / 4,000, archers 1,166 / 2,332, light cavalry 2,333 / 4,666, heavy cavalry 833 / 1,666), and the 30 (type, side, size) combinations draw 30 distinct 26 × 26 tile interiors, one per icon code. The rewritten bullet cites this beside the static source for the `small|medium|large` choice. It is a reading of the original's pixels for the rule only: the clone's icons stay its own drawings (clean room), and the ground tile and the cursor were not compared with the running game, so their tag stays the static one.
  - **The recolour.** Each unit icon has three recolourable regions, as the original's purple `0x800080`, white and blue are replaced by the nation's three colours **[confirmed: code, `FUN_0044A6C8`]**. The colours are the original's own: each nation's `+0x424`, `+0x428` and `+0x42C`, which T122 reads and exports into the world data as `battleColorsHex` (the user's decisions of 2026-10-04 on [#496](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/496)). `BattleIconTint` maps purple to the first, white to the second and blue to the third, Godot-free; a world without the field (a custom world) falls back to the nation's `colorHex`, white and `glyphColorHex` [designed]. The unit map's army and fleet icons, drawn for all 16 owners, use the same triple in the same role order (background, then the two figure colours) for every nation, so the mapping purple to the first, white to the second and blue to the third agrees with what the original paints on the map (a Wine candidate that adds no Done-when item: [`2026-10-09-owner-colours-by-band.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-09-owner-colours-by-band.md), research `ba103a0`).
  - **The recolour, since confirmed end to end** (a scope note; it adds no Done-when item). The battle form recolours its 15 templates with `FUN_0044a6c8` once for the attacker's owner (images 0–14) and once for the defender's (15–29), and every sprite drawn in Wine for Rome, Gaul and an edited Numidian army equals its template recoloured, pixel for pixel **[confirmed: decompile + Wine, [`2026-10-09-battle-map-units-use-the-nation-recolour.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-09-battle-map-units-use-the-nation-recolour.md), research `ef19e2c`; [`2026-10-09-numidian-battle-drawn-in-wine.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-09-numidian-battle-drawn-in-wine.md), research `ca08a33`]**. The classical world's `battleColorsHex` equals the constants new-game setup writes for all 16 nations ([`2026-10-09-unit-icon-recolour-and-nation-glyphs.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-09-unit-icon-recolour-and-nation-glyphs.md), research `4ef90ec`), so `BattleIconTint` needs no other source. Two consequences for the pictures: Numidia's fill is grey `#808080`, not the teal of its city markers (Macedonia and Gaul fill grey too); and Numidia's background is lime `#00FF00`, which is the base colour of the original's ground tile (`BatMapList` image 15: lime, with green, olive, white and black), so in the original a Numidian unit is a flat lime square on lime grass. The clone's ground tile stays its own drawing. Also, the fallback for a world without the field uses `glyphColorHex` as the third colour, and that field is the fill for 13 classical nations but the outline for Macedonia, Gaul and Illyria (bug [#912](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/912)). This does not affect the classical world, which carries the field.
