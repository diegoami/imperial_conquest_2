- **Scope**: The source is the decompile, recorded on #424.
  - After its sweep, `FUN_0044BB18` (`all_app_functions.txt` :50211–50226) takes one of two branches, with **no liveness test**. **[confirmed: decompile]**
    - If the captured city is not a capital and the loser has fewer than 6 cities, it calls `FUN_0044C528`, the conquest. Fewer than 6 includes **0**, after a sweep that emptied the loser.
    - Otherwise it tries the capital move (`FUN_0044BD2C`), and conquers if the move fails.
  - The engine's `ConquestTrigger.Evaluate` returns early when the loser is already `Eliminated`. That happens when the sweep's defections took its last cities through `FUN_0044BED8`'s elimination block. The engine therefore skips everything `FUN_0044C528` does for that loser:
    - the capital sentinel;
    - the "<winner> conquers <loser>." banner;
    - the winner's +50 unity, capped at 990;
    - the treasury copy;
    - the neighbour merge;
    - conquered-by set to the **capturer**, not to the last defection's receiver;
    - the recruitment-slot wipe.
  - Stale capitals stay reachable in the original through defection by rebellion (`FUN_0044C204` → `FUN_0044BED8`, which writes no capital) and through rebirth. So T90's and T91's any-nation's-capital checks are unaffected.

- **Hazards**:
  - **Two elimination blocks in one capture.** Read `FUN_0044BED8`'s elimination block and `FUN_0044C528` side by side. The defection block runs first; decide what the conquest overwrites. Conquered-by ends as the capturer, and the capital goes to the sentinel. News order must follow the decompile.
