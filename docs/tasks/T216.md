### Covered correction

For an AI seat under `classical-faithful`, an over-capacity embark with a legal free land receiver and a free army-table slot moves the first unit whose cumulative troops would exceed `ships × transportTroopsPerShip`, plus every later unit, **whole** into a land army. Keep preceding slots aboard, even when unused fleet capacity remains. Conserve troops, unit order/type/quality/name, supply and money; tested mixed controls split 100 money as 67/33 and 90 supply as 60/30 for 4,000 kept/2,000 ashore. When no slot fits, the original removes the empty embarking-army record and the entire army remains ashore; repair carrier indices and map markers from the actual post-compaction record order, not a presumed stable index. Accepted commands must leave transport links and ownership consistent. Preserve the original capacity boundary: at-capacity does not enter this branch.

The free receiver's exact placement and resource rounding must be checked against the promoted source/control ledger before production editing. A copied `SplitArmy` placement or transfer rule is not proof that F8FC uses it. Tests must distinguish a true whole-slot transfer from a proportional trim and prove no troop loss on every accepted branch.

### Owner decision and research boundary

No-land, full-army-table and existing-receiver outcomes are not established by Research PR #74. The source has a no-receiver deletion branch, but its safe accepted-state contract has not been tested. **Proposed interim policy for review:** when this implementation cannot create a fully validated land receiver, refuse the over-capacity embark with a typed reason before changing any unit, army, fleet, resource or RNG state. Do not silently retain the current troop-scaling behavior. The owner must approve this deliberate safety fallback, or wait for the bounded original controls in conquest #174. Either choice leaves the known free-receiver correction in scope. Do not claim original fidelity for a proposed refusal.
