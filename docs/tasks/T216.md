### Covered correction

For an AI seat under `classical-faithful`, an over-capacity embark with a legal free land receiver and a free army-table slot moves the first unit whose cumulative troops would exceed `ships × transportTroopsPerShip`, plus every later unit, **whole** into a land army. Keep preceding slots aboard, even when unused fleet capacity remains. Conserve troops, unit order/type/quality/name, supply and money. Original F8FC splits money and supply by truncating **one third to the land receiver**, independently of troop proportions: tested controls split 100 money as 67/33 and 90 supply as 60/30. When no slot fits, the original removes the empty embarking-army record and the entire army remains ashore; repair carrier indices and map markers from the actual post-compaction record order, not a presumed stable index. Accepted commands must leave transport links and ownership consistent. Preserve the original capacity boundary: at-capacity does not enter this branch.

The source calls F8FC → `FUN_00449F08` → `FUN_004492C0` for a new land receiver, bounded by the original 198-army table; this is the same last-qualifying 3×3 land-cell scan used by the existing `SplitPlacement` helper. The receiver is initialized with its own covered cell, morale 59, and the original AI new-army move count before whole slots/resources are transferred. Verify this mapping against the promoted controls, including the observed receiver cell and record compaction; do not assume `SplitArmy`'s supply rebalance applies. Tests must distinguish whole-slot transfer from proportional trim and prove no troop loss on every accepted branch.
