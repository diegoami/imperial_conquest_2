- **Scope**:
  - **Protects**: everything under `src/**` and every CLI golden. The CLI keeps its `<kind> accepted.` lines and its "Type 'peace-yes'…" prompt, which the comment at `GameSession.Commands.cs:25–40` says are pinned. Also protected: the news log's content, every order's behaviour, every dialog's content, the side panel and its toggle, and the news panel's own visibility toggle. Forbidden: an edit under `src/**`, `data/**` or `tests/fixtures/**`; a ruleset key; a new command.
  - **Point 1, the map fills its area** **[designed: the user's remark on #781, "the map does not cover the whole screen"]**. Today `FitToView` zooms by `Min(w / mapW, h / mapH)`, which leaves background bars on one axis. It also fits only once, so a window resized after the first frame (the 1500 × 850 start size maximised to 2560 × 1351) keeps the first fit. After this task the default fit **covers** the control: zoom `Max(w / mapW, h / mapH)`, with the map's overflow reachable by the existing pan, clamped so that no background shows. The fit is redone on every resize until the player zooms or pans; after that, a resize keeps the player's view centre. The zoom-out limit is the cover zoom, so the background never shows.
  - **Point 2, windows open centred** **[designed: the user's remark on #781]**. Every overlay opened through `ShowOverlay` covers the whole game screen (its backdrop), and its panel is centred in it, whatever the window's size and whenever it opens, including on the first frame and after a resize. The likely cause, unconfirmed, is `ModalOverlay.Build`'s extra root, anchored before it has a parent. The implementer proves the cause with `ScreenLayoutCheck` before fixing it, and fixes it once (in `ModalOverlay` or `ShowOverlay`), not per screen.
  - **Point 3, no CLI prompt in the app** **[designed]**. When an order raises an offer of peace that the app answers through its window (`PendingPeaceOfferFor`), the output area leaves out the offer's lines (`PeaceTreatyOfferDialogLines`) and the "Type 'peace-yes' to accept or 'peace-no' to decline." prompt; the window shows them. The filter in `CommandOutcomeText` matches the engine's own line builders, not a copied string, so a reworded CLI line cannot slip through.
  - **Point 4, a readable reply and its news** **[designed wording, `CommandOutcomeWording`]**. For an accepted order, the output area shows a readable line for its command kind in place of `<kind> accepted.`: for example *"You accepted the peace treaty."* for `diplomacy.accept-peace-treaty`, and *"Unit recruited."* for `recruitment.recruit-standing-unit`. Below it, it shows the news lines the order added to `State.NewsLog` (the entries added since the order was submitted), so *"<Winner> and <Loser> have agreed to end their war."* is on screen after Yes. A refusal keeps the engine's refusal text, as today. Every kind of `GameCommandTable` that the app can issue has a wording. A kind without one falls back to today's line, and the test below lists none without one.
  - **Not this task's**: the CLI's own text; the news panel's layout; a refusal's wording; the map's art; the side panel's width (T140); the End turn box (T118).

- **Hazards**:
  - **The cover fit crops the map.** When the window's shape differs from the map's, part of the map is off screen until panned. If a check or the visual review shows a city that panning cannot reach, stop and report; do not go back to the letterbox.
  - **Two overlay patterns.** `ModalOverlay` screens and inline dialogs build their roots differently, so fixing one may leave the other off-centre. Done-when 2 covers both.
  - **The engine's text is pinned.** Changing a CLI line, even one only the app shows, breaks `success.golden.txt` and `PeaceTreatyOfferTests`. All of this task's wording lives in `godot/`.
