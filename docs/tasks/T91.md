- **Scope**: The source is the decompile (`all_app_functions.txt`), as recorded on #407, #415 and #416. The research report [`decompiled-defection-and-siege-attrition.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/decompiled-defection-and-siege-attrition.md) carries both corrections since research `58e35fb`.
  - **The cascade's unity gate reads the loser (#415).** `FUN_0044BB18` calls the sweep `FUN_0044BA1C` (:50196) **before** it writes the captured city's owner (:50199). The gate at :50133, `(&DAT_00474AB0)[candidate.owner * 0x24A] < 0x28A`, is therefore **the loser's unity < 650**, read after the capture's own −15 (:50182). **[confirmed: decompile]** The engine reads the new owner's unity.
  - **Every "is it a capital" test is any nation's capital.** `FUN_0044B8D0(city)` is true when **any** of the 16 nations' capital pointers (nation `+0x444`) names the city, whether that nation is alive or not. Three places in the original use it, and the engine reads each one as the owner's capital or as never:
    1. **After the sweep, the capital-move-or-conquer branch** (`FUN_0044BB18` :50211, #416). The engine checks only the loser's own capital (`wasCapital`).
    2. **The capital ×5/3 in defender strength** (`FUN_0044A98C`: `FUN_0044B8D0(city)` and loyalty > 59). The engine passes the owner's capital at `InstantBattleResolver.cs` ~:830, and `false` for every capital-move candidate at `ConquestTrigger.cs` ~:222 (#409 S4).
    3. The cascade's own gate, already corrected by T90 (`FUN_0044BA1C` :50125).

    The engine and the original differ only when a city is named by another nation's capital pointer. Since T86, a nation eliminated by defection keeps its capital field, so this is reachable. T90's review proved it with two real captures.
  - **The Galatia scenario and corpus (#415 R3).** In the save's news log (`galatia-elimination-and-city-resupply-confirmed.md`), only Synnada and Acroinon "defect from" Galatia, and the conquest takes the other five. Under the loser's-unity gate [derived], capturing Laranda leaves Galatia at 653 (no cascade), and capturing Gordium leaves it at 638 (two defections, 5 cities left, then conquest). `elimination.galatiaDefectsFromCount = 7` and the scenario's scripted defections overstate this.
  - **#417.** The stale comments in `GalatiaEliminationScenarioTests` (the "seven" wording, the "only Gordium" remark, a second `<remarks>`). The stale `Defect` remark. The dead `isCandidateCapital`. Optionally, a two-capture test of the stale-capital path.
