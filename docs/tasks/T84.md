  - `tests/IC2.Engine.Tests/Cities/Capture/**` and `tests/IC2.Engine.Tests/Ai/**`: **new files only**
- **Scope**: When a nation is eliminated, the engine leaves its armies and fleets on the map. T69 resets the nation's relations and rejects war on it, so those forces would become permanent, untouchable blockers. The original leaves nothing behind. Research [`decompiled-elimination-cleanup.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/decompiled-elimination-cleanup.md) (`43a44a1`, §2, §4 and §6) is the source for every rule below:
  - **Armies first.** Every army of the eliminated nation is deleted: on land, in a city, or aboard a fleet. An army aboard a fleet clears that fleet's carried-army link. (`FUN_0044AB90`, :49372, **[confirmed: decompile]**.)
  - **Then fleets.** Every *launched* fleet of the eliminated nation is deleted, together with any army still aboard it, whoever owns that army. (`FUN_0044AD38`, :49493, **[confirmed]**. Whether another nation's army can board it is **[hypothesis]** in the report.)
  - **Fleets under construction change owner** to the nation that received the last city, keeping their countdown and build city. They are not deleted. (**[confirmed]**, the fleet loop's `+10 == -1` test in both paths.)
  - **Nothing is refunded.** The deleted records' units, supplies and money are destroyed by rule, not credited to anyone (**[confirmed]**).
  - **The AI never targets an eliminated nation.** Every targeting path in the original requires the target's unity to be above 0 (**[confirmed]** for diplomacy, report §5). With this task an eliminated nation has no forces to attack, and the AI's attack proposers skip one anyway, so that a hand-built state (like `AiCommandLegalityTests`' `eliminated-neighbour` case) never yields a command T69 rejects. The military half is **[derived]**.
- **Done when**:
  1. **Through `Capture`.** A test eliminates nation X through `CityCaptureResolver.Capture` while X has:
     - an army on land;
     - an army in a city of another nation (if the engine allows it; otherwise say so);
     - an army aboard its own launched fleet;
     - a second launched fleet;
     - a fleet under construction.
     Afterwards none of X's armies and none of its launched fleets exist, and the fleet under construction belongs to the capturer with its countdown and build city unchanged.
  2. **Through `Defect`.** The same holds through `CityCaptureResolver.Defect`, with the fleet under construction going to the city's new owner.
  3. **No dangling ids, with two nations.**
     - The test includes a surviving nation Y with an army aboard its own fleet. Y's army, its fleet and the link between them are unchanged.
     - If the engine allows cross-nation boarding: X's army aboard Y's fleet is deleted, and Y's fleet stays with its link cleared; Y's army aboard X's launched fleet dies with the fleet. If the engine forbids it, build the state directly and show both anyway.
     - After every elimination, a sweep over the whole state finds no `AboardFleetId` or `CarriedArmyId` naming a missing record, and a save and load round-trips the state.
  4. **Nothing refunded.** Every nation's treasury is unchanged by the disposal itself. The code remark says the deleted units, supplies and money are destroyed by rule, citing the report.
  5. **Only on elimination.** A capture that leaves X with a city deletes nothing, and the disposal runs only when `JustEliminated` is true.
  6. **The AI skips eliminated targets.** In a state where an eliminated nation's army sits next to an AI army, and its fleet next to an AI fleet, neither `ProposeArmyAttacks` nor `ProposeFleetAttacks` proposes a candidate against it. A test shows that, and shows the same state *without* the elimination still yields the attack.
  7. `dotnet build IC2.sln` and `dotnet test IC2.sln` green, and the diff lists only Owns paths.
