- **Scope**:
  - **Protects**: under `classical-faithful`, the AI orders a new fleet and consolidates its army units exactly by the original's gates and amounts, through the same commands a human uses (`OrderFleetCommand`, `JoinUnitsCommand`, `DisbandUnitCommand`). It issues no order those commands would refuse, and nothing changes under `improved`.
  - **The rule** ([`2026-10-07-strategic-ai-turn.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-07-strategic-ai-turn.md) §2.4, **[confirmed: decompile]**, except where marked):
    - **Order a new fleet** (`FUN_00450768`): at most one order a turn, when the game's fleet table holds fewer than **99** fleets (all nations' fleets, `DAT_004a0326`: the same count at which the human's Build fleet is refused, R57), its treasury is above **3000**, and its fleet score is **0**, or **1** with more than **50** cities, or **2** with more than **100** cities. The fleet score sums the nation's own fleets: **+1** for each launched fleet and **+4** for each fleet still under construction (the fleet record's `+0x0A` construction countdown: `-1` when launched, `≥ 0` while building; the clone's `FleetState.IsUnderConstruction`) **[confirmed: decompile, the body of `FUN_00450768` in the whole-application dump, `:53838`; the report's shorthand "own fleet +1, at sea +4" is corrected by it]**. So one fleet under construction blocks a new order, and a second or third launched fleet needs more than 50 or 100 cities.
      - The fleet's ships are `min(100, cities + 50)` (**[derived]** in the report: the size parameter's meaning). The order still passes `OrderFleetCommand`'s own range (`naval.orderMinShips`..`orderMaxShips`).
      - The port (`FUN_004496e0`): an own coastal city with a free adjacent water cell, **nearest the capital**, not already hosting a construction order.
        The human's Build fleet order, played in the original (Wine), makes the same choice **[confirmed: six orders from Rome, [`2026-10-09-fleet-build-city-nearest-capital.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/c36b255/docs/reports/2026-10-09-fleet-build-city-nearest-capital.md), research `c36b255`; the rule is `[derived]`]**: the own city with a code 0 or 1 in its 3×3, nearest the capital (Chebyshev), skipping one already building a fleet. So the **coastal test** is that play-confirmed predicate, which is what `CoastalCity.IsCoastal` computes on the shipped world (a neighbour of terrain type `PassableByFleets`, codes 0 and 1), and the **nearest-capital** and **not-already-building** parts are confirmed for the human order and read from the decompile for the AI. The decompile's word **"free"** (a *free* adjacent water cell) is **not** established by play (the report lists "whether the sea must be free of other units" as not established) and the AI report gives no predicate for it, so this entry adds none: the port is any own city `OrderFleetCommand` accepts (Done-when 4) that passes the three parts above. If the implementer finds a predicate for "free" in the research excerpt, they stop and report instead of choosing one. The tie between equal distances was not played either, so the rule that follows stays `[designed]`: equal distances resolve by a stable rule the implementer states: the report's loop order if it records one, else city id ascending, marked `[designed]` with what was searched.
    - **Consolidate army units** (`FUN_00450858`), every own army, slot by slot in the army's slot order:
      - a regular unit (origin label 0) below **⅔** of its type's standard size merges with the next same-type regular slot below **⅓** (quality averaged, as `JoinUnitsCommand` already does);
      - a mercenary unit below **⅙** of its type's standard size is disbanded (`DisbandUnitCommand`),
        **except an army's last unit**, which is kept: `DisbandUnitCommand` refuses the last unit by design (#565),
        and the report does not say what the original does there. This is `[designed]` (a divergence the entry
        names: the report's §2.4 row has no last-unit case and the dump's body was not read). An army reduced to
        one unit by an earlier disband in the same pass keeps that unit too.
  - **Out of scope**: merging armies and merging fleets ([T159](T159.md)); `improved`'s economy; the army and fleet phases.
- **Done when**:
  1. **The fleet order's gates.** On hand-built states, one test per gate fails when the gate moves by one: the game's fleet count, counting other nations' fleets (98 orders, 99 does not), the treasury (3001 orders, 3000 does not), and each fleet-score branch (0; 1 with 51 cities but not 50; 2 with 101 cities but not 100), with the score's weights pinned: one own launched fleet scores 1, one own fleet under construction scores 4 and blocks the order. At most one order a turn.
  2. **The fleet order's size and port.** The ships equal `min(100, cities + 50)`. The port is the own city with a code 0 or 1 in its 3×3 nearest the capital (Chebyshev), skipping a city that already hosts a construction order; no occupancy test on the water cell is added (see the rule above). The tie rule has a test in both collection orders.
  3. **Consolidation.** Tests pin the ⅔ and ⅓ thresholds for regulars, the ⅙ threshold for mercenaries (each failing when its fraction or comparison changes), the same-type and regular-only rules, the slot order, and the averaged quality. A unit at exactly a threshold is tested on both sides. **The last unit:** an army that starts with one undersized mercenary keeps it, and an army whose disbands would leave none keeps its last unit; no refused order is issued in either case.
  4. **Through the human's commands.** Every order goes through `OrderFleetCommand`, `JoinUnitsCommand` or `DisbandUnitCommand` and is accepted. A test shows a case the command would refuse (for example, ships outside the ruleset's range) is never proposed.
  5. **`improved` is unchanged.** A seeded `improved` run's AI decisions are identical before and after.
  6. **Nothing regresses.** The soak reports 0 rejected within its budget. The changed goldens and seeded expectations are regenerated or updated and listed with their reason. Build and test are green, and the diff lists only Owns paths.
