#### T123 The tactical battle's rules engine: a port of the decompiled `TBattleMap`

- **Design milestone**: M21 (the tactical battle, v0.5.0 *Battles*, [#496](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/496)). It ports `game-design.md`'s Combat, "The tactical battle": the state, copy-in, placement, the half-round loop, movement, shooting, melee, morale and rout, surrender, the end, and the write-back, with every random draw in the original's order. It is Godot-free and session-free: nothing opens it in the game until T125. **Labels**: `phase:2 lane:engine`
- **Branch**: `task/T123-tactical-battle-engine` · **Model/effort**: **Opus** / High. Battle resolution is architecture-class under [§3.3](../build-process.md#33-model-selection): this is a new battle model, every later v0.5.0 task builds on its state and seams, and an integer-semantics or draw-order slip is invisible to a casual reading and fails the golden master much later · **Reviewer**: **very complex** under [§3.4](../build-process.md#34-why-the-reviewers-model-differs-from-the-implementers) criteria (a) and (b): Claude implements, so Sol **and** the Luna pair, plus `/code-review --effort ultra` as on the four architecture PRs ([§3.5](../build-process.md#35-where-the-code-review-skill-fits))
- **Start after**: T122 · **Merge after**: T122. T124, T125 and T129 start after this task. It is **never in flight with** T121 (a change under `src/IC2.Engine/Battle/**` moves the tournament mid-run).
- **Owns**:
  - `src/IC2.Engine/Battle/Tactical/**` (new), **except** `src/IC2.Engine/Battle/Tactical/General/**` (T124's);
  - `tests/IC2.Engine.Tests/Battle/Tactical/**` (new), **except** `tests/IC2.Engine.Tests/Battle/Tactical/General/**` and `tests/IC2.Engine.Tests/Battle/Tactical/GoldenMaster/**` (T124's and T129's).
- **Scope**:
  - **Protects**: the decompiled rule values and formulas of `game-design.md`'s "The tactical battle" (copy-in, setup and slow advance, movement and detours, shooting, melee, rout and cascade, surrender, end, write-back) and the original's `Random` draw order and `Random(0)` semantics. Forbidden: a rule or constant that differs from the design, a draw added, dropped or reordered, a 64-bit or checked product where the original's is 32-bit and wrapping, a draw from anything but the battle's draw seam, and any change to `InstantBattleResolver`, `BattleCasualties` or a battle the original auto-resolves.
  - Every rule is **[confirmed: code, static, [`2026-10-04-decompiled-tactical-battle-rules.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-04-decompiled-tactical-battle-rules.md)]** as `game-design.md` restates and tags it; that section is the contract, and its paragraphs are named below. The constants come from the ruleset: T122's `combat.tactical` and `unitTypes[].shotVulnerability`, the existing `combat.detailedResolver` (the matrix and melee divisors) and the existing write-back fields (quality floor and cap, promotion chance, unity swing and cap, supply divisor). It is a **fresh port**: it does not use or extend `src/IC2.Engine/Battle/Candidates/**`.
  - **The state** ("The board and the battle state"): an immutable `TacticalBattleState` holding the report's battle block (attacker and defender army ids, side to move, placed, counter, 40 slots of the report's 11 fields, the 14 × 12 icon-code grid) plus which sides are computer-controlled and which have *Computer general* on. The icon code and the slot-at-cell scan (last live slot wins) are the design's.
  - **The draw seam**: an `IBattleDraws` with the original's `Random(n)` semantics (`[0, n)`; `Random(0)` returns 0 and consumes a draw). The game's implementation wraps an `IRng` stream; the stream's name and derivation are T125's. A test implementation scripts the values, and a second replays Delphi's generator (`RandSeed := RandSeed × $08088405 + 1`, `Random(n) = (RandSeed × n) shr 32`, unsigned 32-bit, [`2026-10-03-new-game-turn-order-shuffle.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-03-new-game-turn-order-shuffle.md)), so that T129 can run the original's seeds.
  - **The rules**, one function per original routine, each named after it in a doc comment: copy-in (`FUN_00437DE4`, including the computer side's `+3` to the strategic army morale and the in-place sort of its army's unit records, returned as a changed `GameState`); placement by a human order; the half-round setup (`FUN_00439968`, also exposed as `Resume` for a loaded block) and end (`FUN_00439C20`); the driver (`FUN_00439C84`) calling an `ITacticalGeneral` for computer-played half-rounds, with a null general for tests; the human orders (select, place, move, set a target, shoot, clear a target, end the half-round, *Computer general* on/off, surrender); movement (`FUN_00438D24`, `FUN_00438A6C`) with the computer-only branches; shooting (`FUN_0043910C`); melee (`FUN_004393EC`); rout (`FUN_00438FB0`); surrender; the end test and the winner.
  - **The write-back** ("Write-back", steps 1–5 of `TBattleOver_OK`): compaction of the winner's survivors in slot order, promotion with one `Random(4)` each, money and capped supplies, the loser army's deletion, unity ±25 (cap 990) and the news event, applied to a `GameState`. It also returns a `TacticalBattleReport`: the *Battle ended* window's contents (winner, loser, per-type and total start and finish troops, the captured money and supplies) and the exchange log the screen shows. **Step 6, the peace dialog, is not here**: its draw comes after the promotions and its routine is diplomacy's, which no file under `src/IC2.Engine/Battle/**` may reference (`FieldBattleTests.AssertNoBattleSourceReferencesDiplomacy`). The engine exposes the post-promotion draw seam so that T125 takes the `Random(5)` from the same stream.
  - **Integer semantics** ("Integer semantics"): 32-bit products that wrap (C# `unchecked` `int`), the shot bound's `min` in 16 bits, and pass-2 shots allowed below 0 (the counter is signed). `classical-faithful` and `improved` run the same rules here; nothing reads a preset flag.
  - **The trace**: every exchange, rout, placement, move and draw is recorded in order on the state's log (a `TacticalEvent` list), so that the conformance tests and T129 compare half-round by half-round.
- **Done when**:
  1. **One test class per routine**, under `tests/IC2.Engine.Tests/Battle/Tactical/`, each citing the report section and the `game-design.md` paragraph it checks, under a scripted `IBattleDraws`. Together they cover the report's golden-master checks 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 10 statically:
     - copy-in: morale `max(60, min(90, Random(q × 4) + armyMorale))` for every live slot, attacker slots first, with `Random(0)` consuming one scripted value; positions `(s mod 13, s div 13)` and `(s mod 13, 11 − s div 13)`; a computer side's `+3` written to its `GameState` army and its records sorted by the design's key, ties kept in order (strict `>`); the human side unchanged;
     - the icon code after every exchange equals `type × 3 + min(2, troops div (std div 3))` (+20 for the defender), and the thresholds LI 5,000/10,000, HI 2,000/4,000, archers 1,166/2,332, LC 2,333/4,666, HC 833/1,666 hold at their edges;
     - setup: moves reset, targets cleared, moves 1 for non-HI computer units only while `dmin > 2` and the counter read before the increment is below 10;
     - a shot's loss from `n`, the archers' doubling at distance 1 only, the 16-bit `min`, the morale `−min(3, …)` on troops before the loss, and the shot and move decrements;
     - a melee's two losses with `f` = 1, 2, 4 and 5 targeting slots (capped at 4), each loss's 30,000 and 40 % caps and `+ 1`, the `±2/−3` by `troops div loss` with a tie going against the attacker, and four draws in the order attacker, attacker, defender, defender;
     - the real matrix: a light-infantry attack on heavy infantry with the report's §5 values hits the attacker's cap for scripted draws at the top of the range, and a test pins that the matrix is read from the ruleset, not a literal (two rulesets with different matrices give different losses);
     - rout: the floor `std div 25`, the automatic removal at morale ≤ 19, the safe band above 39, the two-draw test in 20–39 (draws consumed only there), the cascade −6 with removal below 30 and no second level, the enemy's `+5` capped at 99 and its target cleared, and the end when a side empties;
     - the write-back: compaction, `max(6, q)` then `Random(4) = 0` → `min(9, q + 1)`, money, supplies capped at `troops div 100`, the loser deleted, unity ±25 capped at 990, the news event, and the both-sides-empty edge going to the defender.
  2. **Movement** tests pin the Bresenham path, both detour pairs (column, row and diagonal cases, the swap on a `y`-major line), the cost rule, ties to the first alternative, the three stop conditions, the human's second walk, and the computer-only branches (a unit with a target does not move; the last-move forfeit by `S`; the adjacency and in-range checks before each step).
  3. **The draw order.** A test plays a scripted battle (both sides human-ordered, so no general) whose draws are logged, and asserts the log equals the design's "Every random draw, in order" for it: copy-in, then per shot two draws and its rout's, then per melee four draws and both routs', then the promotions. A second test replays a fixed Delphi seed through the replay implementation and asserts the first ten `Random(n)` values against literals computed by hand from the generator in the test's comment.
  4. **Integer semantics.** A melee test with troops above 23,000 against a high-power defender asserts the wrapped 32-bit `troops × D` value the original computes (the expected value derived in the test's comment), and a test asserts a shot bound whose `min` differs in 16 and 32 bits.
  5. **Determinism and isolation.** The same state, orders and draws give byte-identical states and traces. `grep -rn "Candidates\|System.Random\|Diplomacy" src/IC2.Engine/Battle/Tactical` prints nothing, and the determinism source scanner passes.
  6. **Nothing shipped changes.** `git diff --stat origin/main...HEAD -- src/IC2.Engine/Battle/InstantBattleResolver.cs src/IC2.Engine/Battle/BattleCasualties.cs src/IC2.Engine/Battle/Commands src/IC2.Engine/Presentation src/IC2.Engine/Ai tests/fixtures` is empty, and `BattleDeterminismTests`, `FieldBattleTests`, `AiSoakTests`, `TournamentTests` and the CLI golden tests pass unchanged.
  7. `dotnet build IC2.sln` and `dotnet test IC2.sln` are green, and the diff lists only Owns paths.
- **Hazards**:
  - **No task changes `InstantBattleResolver`'s outcomes for a battle the original still auto-resolves** (AI against AI in the field, every siege, every naval battle). This task does not touch the instant path at all; if the write-back seems to want a helper from it, call the helper as it is or duplicate the arithmetic with a citation, never edit it. A shipped seeded value that moves is a stop-and-report.
  - **The design is the contract, the report is its source.** Where they seem to differ, stop and report; do not choose. Several rules look like bugs and are not to be fixed: negative shots in pass 2 (T124's, but the counter must allow it), stacking on one cell, the wrap, the defender winning an emptied battle, `Random(0)` consuming a draw.
  - **Draw order is the whole golden master.** One extra or missing draw shifts every later value. Draw only where the design's list draws: the two rout draws only in the 20–39 band with troops at or above the floor; no draw for a human's movement.
  - **The +3 and the sort change the strategic army at copy-in**, before the battle is fought, and persist whatever the result. They are applied only for a computer-controlled side, never for a human side with *Computer general* on.
  - **The general is an interface here.** Pass 1–3, placement by formation and the flank are T124's; this task calls them through `ITacticalGeneral` and tests with a null or scripted general. The movement routine's computer-only branches are this task's, because they are inside `FUN_00438D24`.
  - **Wine-observed facts are checks, not sources.** The probe and the sweep (research `dee8150`) observed the block layout, the copy-in sort, the shots, the slow advance and the post-battle effects; T129 replays them. If a static test here and one of their observations disagree, report it; T129 decides nothing either.
