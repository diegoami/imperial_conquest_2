- **Owns**:

  - `godot/Checks/AiPhaseBattleCheck.cs` and `godot/Checks/AiPhaseBattleCheck.tscn` (new);
  - `tests/IC2.Engine.Tests/Ui/Screens/BattleReportRouterTests.cs` (new), and the one `<Compile Include>` line for `BattleReportRouter.cs` in `tests/IC2.Engine.Tests/IC2.Engine.Tests.csproj`.
- **Scope**: when a human seat's turn starts, it sees the result of every battle fought against it during the AI phase, in the existing battle-result window (`BattleResultScreen`). Battles stay resolved at once (option A) **[designed: the user's decision of 2026-10-02]**. The original instead opens its tactical screen for that human during the AI phase itself **[Wine candidate: [`2026-10-02-two-human-seats.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-02-two-human-seats.md)]**; the clone keeps the instant resolver and moves the report to the turn start.
  - **What the clone does today** (checked when this entry was written). `GameSession` captures every `BattleResolved` of a `Submit` call, the human's own orders and every AI seat's turn that an `end` plays, into `SessionOutput.Battles` and `GameSession.LastBattles` (T25). `MainGameScreen.OnCommandIssued` queues a `BattleResultScreen` for **each** of them, in order, one at a time, and **then** checks for a hotseat handoff. `ScreensCheck.CheckTwoBattlesInOneEndShowBothBattleResultOverlaysInTurn` drives exactly this for one human seat. So:
    - with **one human seat**, every battle the AI phase fought against it is already shown, one window per battle, right after `end` returns to that seat: that is its turn start;
    - with **two or more human seats**, the windows go to whoever is at the screen when `end` returns, and **before** the handoff screen. A battle fought against the next human is shown to the one who just ended, before the "pass the device" screen hides that player's view. A battle fought against the one who just ended is shown at once, not at that seat's next turn.
  - **The rule** [designed: the user's decision of 2026-10-02, applied per seat]: each battle of a `Submit` call goes, in the order fought, to every **human** seat on either side (`BattleResult.AttackerNationId`, `DefenderNationId`).
    - If that seat is the active seat once the call returns, its window is shown now, after the handoff screen when there is one.
    - Otherwise it is held for that seat and shown, in the order fought, when that seat next becomes the active seat, after its handoff screen and before the battles of the call that made it active.
    - A battle with no human seat on either side is shown as today: now, to the active seat [designed: unchanged from T25].
    - A held battle whose seat is no longer human (it fell, or was handed to the AI, T87) is dropped.
  - **A human's own attack** in its own turn is shown at once, as today: its seat is the active one.
  - `BattleReportRouter` holds the rule, Godot-free: given a call's battles, the human seats and the active seat after the call, it returns the battles to show now and keeps the held ones per seat. `MainGameScreen` asks it after each `Submit`, then shows the handoff (if any), then the battles it returned.

- **Hazards**:
  - **Several battles in one AI phase: one window per battle, or one list? [open], for the user.** The user did not decide this. **The recommended default is one window per battle, in the order fought**, which is what T25 already does for one human seat, so nothing new is built for it. A list window would be a new screen under `godot/Screens/**`. If the user picks the list before dispatch, the main session amends Scope and Done-when 4; otherwise build the default.
  - **Held battles live in the screen, not in the save.** A hotseat game saved and resumed between the AI phase and the held seat's turn loses the held windows; the news log still has every battle. Persisting them would add to the save's format, which is not this task's. If the review or the user wants it, it is a follow-up.
  - **The AI phase before the first human turn.** A New Game whose first human seat is not first in the turn order plays the earlier AI seats at construction (`GameSession.AdvanceToHumanSeat`). Their battles reach the screen only with the first `Submit`'s battles (`_pendingBattleResults` is flushed then), so they are shown after the human's first command, not at the turn start. Fixing that needs a read of the pending battles in `GameSession.cs`, an engine file outside this entry. Leave it, and say so in the PR; it is a follow-up if wanted.
  - **AI-versus-AI battles** keep T25's behaviour: a window each, shown to the active seat. Whether to stop showing them (they are in the news log) is a design question this task does not decide.
  - **Don't change the engine.** The battles, their order and their fields are `GameSession`'s (T25); this task only routes them. If the router needs something the engine does not expose, stop and report.
  - **Never two Godot processes at once**: run the headless checks one at a time.
