- **Start after**: the `v0.5.1` release · **Merge after**: [T155](T155.md) (it gates `AiEconomyPhase.cs`'s recruitment towns).
- **Owns**:
  - `src/IC2.Engine/Ai/AiFaithfulEconomy.cs` (new): the original's economy phase for `classical-faithful`.
  - `src/IC2.Engine/Ai/AiEconomyPhase.cs`: **only** dispatching to it under `classical-faithful`. `improved` keeps the designed economy unchanged.
  - `src/IC2.Engine/Model/Ruleset.cs` and the three rulesets: the threat-budget, recruitment and tax constants as ruleset values, and a flag selecting the economy (additive). `classical-faithful.json` changes only through `scripts/export-classical-world.cs`.
  - Tests: `tests/IC2.Engine.Tests/Ai/**` (its tests), the corpus and `known-reports.json` (its entries and the report only), and the CLI goldens (regenerated through the CLI only).
- **Scope**:
  - **Protects**: under `classical-faithful`, the AI's economy is the original's (the user's decisions of 2026-10-01 on #515 and 2026-10-09 on #910):
    - recruitment is driven by the threat budget, at the capital, with the original's random types and amounts, at most 8 orders a turn, and down to the deficit floor;
    - the tax rate changes only in week 11, by the original's policy;
    - **the AI never orders a fortification**.
  - **The rule** ([`2026-10-07-strategic-ai-turn.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-07-strategic-ai-turn.md) §2, **[confirmed: decompile]**; the week-11 tax and the free mercenary hire are corroborated in play by [`2026-10-07-ai-turn-corroboration.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-07-ai-turn-corroboration.md) §1–2):
    - §2.1, *the threat budget*: wars, enemy army values and proximity to the capital, against own strength (allies, own armies);
    - §2.2, *recruit and mobilise* (`FUN_004504f4`), with its three gates, the 8-order cap and recruiting into deficit down to `−wealth/500`; types and amounts `battalion/3 + Random(4·battalion/5)`, as #515's comment records;
    - §2.3, *the tax policy*: week 11 only, writing the rate directly;
    - §2.4, *the four tail sub-phases*, as far as they are economy.

    Every recruitment still passes T155's town gate.
  - **Out of scope**: the army phase ([T156](T156.md)), fleets, diplomacy, `improved`'s economy, and the free-for-100-talents fleet repair (a separate fidelity item).
- **Done when**:
  1. **The threat budget is the original's.** Unit tests pin §2.1's terms on hand-built states, and each fails when its term is removed (show the mutation).
  2. **Recruitment follows it.** On hand-built states: no deficit means no recruitment; a deficit gives orders at the capital, no more than 8 a turn, with types and amounts drawn as the report says through `IRng` (seeded and reproducible), and recruiting continues down to `−wealth/500` and not below.
  3. **The week-11 tax.** The rate changes only in week 11, by §2.3's rule, and a test fails if the week moves.
  4. **No fortifying under `classical-faithful`.** Over a 60-turn seeded `classical-mediterranean` run, the AI orders no fortification (the gap analysis measured about 1,000 a game). Under `improved` the designed behaviour is unchanged (a test).
  5. **Nothing regresses.** The soak reports 0 rejected within its budget. The changed goldens are regenerated through the CLI and listed with their reason. Build and test are green, and the diff lists only Owns paths.
- **Hazards**:
  - **Every seeded `classical-faithful` game changes** (the AI's recruitment, taxes and the treasury it leaves): list each changed golden and seeded test.
  - **The draw stream.** The original's random types and amounts consume draws the designed AI did not; say how many a turn costs.
