#### T157 Under classical-faithful, the AI's economy follows the original: a threat budget, the week-11 tax, no fortifying

- **Scope**:
  - **Protects**: under `classical-faithful`, the AI's economy is the original's (the user's decisions of 2026-10-01 on #515 and 2026-10-09 on #910). Recruitment is driven by the threat budget with the original's city choice, gates, types and amounts; the tax rate changes only in week 11, by the original's three rules; and **the AI never orders a fortification**.
  - **The rule** ([`2026-10-07-strategic-ai-turn.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-07-strategic-ai-turn.md) §2.1–2.3, **[confirmed: decompile]**):
    - **§2.1, the threat budget.**
      - `threat = 15000 × (nations at war with me) + Σ value(a) over every army of a nation at war with me + Σ value/4 over foreign armies within 10 of my capital + Σ value/2 over armies at war within 20 of my capital`, each sum rounded down.
      - `own = 6000 × (allies) + Σ value(a) over my armies + Σ value/2 over allied armies`.
      - If `own < threat`, recruit for the deficit `threat − own`.
      - `value(a) = (troops/100) × DAT_00478FD6[type]` (the per-type AI value, [`decompiled-mobilization-and-mercenary-restock.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/decompiled-mobilization-and-mercenary-restock.md) §4). Distances are Chebyshev from the capital city.
    - **§2.2, recruit and mobilise** (`FUN_004504f4`):
      - at most **8 new orders a turn**;
      - a new order may be placed while `treasury > −wealth/500`; **below that floor, at most 3 orders a turn**;
      - each order is charged `(troops/200) × initialPrice[type]` and raises mobilisation by the player's increment.
    - **§2.2, the order's city** (`FUN_004502e0`): the **capital** by default. When at war, with an own army within 15 of the capital and **more than a third of all queued orders already at the capital**, the order goes to the own city with **fortification ≥ 75** that has fewer queued orders than the capital and lies nearest an enemy capital. Every order still passes T155's town gate.
    - **§2.2, type and amount** (`FUN_004501f4`):
      - the type comes from `Random(100)` buckets: Foot < 35, Guards 35–59, Bowmen 60–74, Lancers 75–89, Dragoons 90–99;
      - the troops are `standardSize/3 + Random(standardSize × 4/5)`, **rounded down to a multiple of 100**;
      - the amount is **capped at the type's standard battalion size**, as #515's research comment records.
    - **§2.3, the tax policy, week 11 only** (the AI's economy phase that runs with the week counter at 11), applied in this order:
      1. `if (unity < 650 or treasury > wealth/2000): tax = max(5, tax − 6)`;
      2. `if (treasury < 0 or (own ≤ threat and treasury < 1000)): tax = min(40, tax + 9)`;
      3. `if (treasury > 0 and unity < 500): tax = 0`.

      The week-11 placement and the first two rules are corroborated in play, Wine-only ([`2026-10-07-ai-turn-corroboration.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-07-ai-turn-corroboration.md)). The zero rule stays `[derived]`.
  - **Out of scope**: the army phase ([T156](T156.md)); fleets; diplomacy; `improved`'s economy; §2.4's tail sub-phases (named above, for a later task); and the free-for-100-talents fleet repair.
- **Done when**:
  1. **The threat budget is the original's.** Unit tests pin each of §2.1's seven terms on hand-built states, and each fails when its term is removed or its divisor changed (show the mutations).
  2. **Recruitment's gates.** On hand-built states:
     - no deficit means no orders;
     - a deficit gives orders, and never more than 8 a turn;
     - with the treasury above `−wealth/500` orders continue; at or below it, **at most 3** that turn;
     - each order's cost and mobilisation increment equal the player's.

     Each boundary has a test that fails when it moves by one: 8 to 9, 3 to 4, and the floor's comparison.
  3. **The order's city.** The capital by default. A state that meets all three conditions (at war, an own army within 15 of the capital, more than a third of queued orders at the capital) sends the order to the fortified town ≥ 75 nearest an enemy capital, with fewer queued orders than the capital. Removing any one condition keeps it at the capital: one test per condition.
  4. **Type and amount.** Seeded tests pin each bucket's boundary (34/35, 59/60, 74/75, 89/90); every amount is a multiple of 100, between `standardSize/3` and the battalion cap. Each test fails without its rounding or its cap. Draws go through `IRng` and reproduce for a seed.
  5. **The week-11 tax.** Each of the three rules has a test, in rule order, that fails if its condition or its bound changes. The rate does not change in any other week.
  6. **No fortifying under `classical-faithful`.** Over a 60-turn seeded `classical-mediterranean` run, the AI orders no fortification (the gap analysis measured about 1,000 a game). Under `improved` the designed economy is unchanged: a test pins one designed fortification decision.
  7. **Nothing regresses.** The soak reports 0 rejected within its budget. The changed goldens and seeded expectations are regenerated or updated and listed with their reason. Build and test are green, and the diff lists only Owns paths.
- **Hazards**:
  - **Every seeded `classical-faithful` game changes** (the AI's recruitment, taxes and the treasury it leaves).
  - **The draw stream.** The original's types and amounts consume draws the designed AI did not; say how many a turn costs.
