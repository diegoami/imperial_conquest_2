- **Scope**: Research [`decompiled-elimination-cleanup.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/decompiled-elimination-cleanup.md) §3 and §4 (research `43a44a1`) is the source for every rule below; each one is **[confirmed: decompile]** unless it is tagged otherwise.
  - **The leader falls: `FUN_0044C8F0`** (:50761). It first splits on who holds the seat:
    - **An AI seat:** the news line "*<nation> depose their leader <leader>.*".
    - **A human seat:** the `THumanFalls` screen, reading "*Your nation has been conquerred by <X>.*" when conquered-by is set. The seat's human flag is then cleared, and the seat goes to the computer. If no human seat is left, the game is over.

    Then, for either kind of seat, the fall itself:
    - a new leader, drawn from the nation's 12 names;
    - `unity = max(unity, min(550, unity + 150))`;
    - `treasury = treasury < 0 ? 0 : treasury + 1000`;
    - every relation between −5 and −1 is set to 0.

    Check the engine's deposition against every step.
  - **When a human's leader falls: `FUN_00452034`**, at the start of the human's turn. The triggers are 250 BC, 334 cities, unity below 400, or debt. The engine deposes only after the human types `end` (#380). Move the check to the start of the turn, before any order.
  - **Rebirth: `FUN_0044C360`** (:50507), called from the quarterly rebellion `FUN_0044C204`. A city rebels when it is not a capital, its loyalty is below 30, and its allegiance is a dead nation (unity below 1). The dead nation then comes back if **more than 7** cities with that allegiance have loyalty below 40. On rebirth:
    - unity 450, conquered-by −1, and treasury, tax base and city count 0;
    - its 40 slots' troops 0, and its relation row all 0;
    - a new leader;
    - each such city defects to it, with relation −8 towards its former owner;
    - the strongest such city becomes its capital;
    - it is no longer eliminated.
  - **#374.** The `DiplomacyRejections` remark overstates two facts:
    - A human eliminated by defection ends at unity 150, not 0 (**[derived]**, the order quirk).
    - `TPolitics_ChangeIR` gates four actions, not five.

    Correct the remark. The engine keys "alive" on `Eliminated`, so the unity-150 quirk is **not** reproduced; the remark says so.
