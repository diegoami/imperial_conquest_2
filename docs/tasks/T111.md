- **Scope**: the Unit map → Army entries act on the selected army. When a fleet carrying an army is selected, they act on the army aboard **[derived: code, the prologue of each `TUnitMap_*` army order]**, within the engine's rules for an army aboard: Join armies and Split army refuse it (Split army's case is below). A foreign or missing army disables them.
  - **Supply army** is [T134](T134.md)'s, which wires it before this task merges. This task does not edit `SupplyDialog.cs` or its row.
  - **Transfer unit** (`TArmyToArmy`, titled *"Army to army transfer"*; the menu says *Transfer unit*, the original's hint and help *Transfer units* **[derived: form, the feature inventory, row UA03]**): the partner is **the game's pick**, `AdjacentPartner.Army` (T106), never the player's (the user's decision that the game picks the partner). There are two unit lists, with **Transfer** (T106's `army-transfer`) and **Disband** (T107's `disband-unit`) under each, and spinners for supply and money. The supply spinner moves `min(step, room, giver's supply)` with the receiver's room `max(0, troops div 100 − supply + 1)` (`FUN_00442310`), and the money spinner `min(step, 1000 − receiver's money, giver's money)`; steps are 10 and 100 **[confirmed: code, [`2026-10-03-army-to-army-ok-supply-rebalancing.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-03-army-to-army-ok-supply-rebalancing.md) item 6]**. These are the dialog's limits; the engine's `OK` (T117) then rebalances supply. OK submits **exactly one** `army-transfer` for the whole dialog, with the selected army first (A) and the partner second: the units moved to the partner as `units=`, the units moved back as `back-units=`, and the supply and money netted to one direction each (`supply=` or `back-supply=`, `money=` or `back-money=`). T117's command commits it all and rebalances once, as the original's single `OK` does [designed: the user's decision of 2026-10-03]. Cancel submits nothing. With no partner, the dialog says there is no army next to this one, and nothing is submitted **[designed wording]**.
  - **Split army**: the original opens its army-to-army dialog, `TArmyToArmy`, titled *"Split army"*, with the two unit lists; the units moved to the second list form the new army **[derived: form and code, `TArmyToArmy_InitializeForm`, `form_xrefs.tsv`; the feature inventory [`2026-10-05-player-facing-feature-inventory.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-05-player-facing-feature-inventory.md), rows UA04 and D03]**. (`TSplitArmyUnit` is Change units → Split unit's dialog, row D06; this entry named it here before the inventory.) The dialog may therefore share `ArmyTransferDialog`'s layout [designed: a default the reviewer may question]. It needs two or more units; the original's refusal is *"You can not split an army containing only 1 unit."* **[derived: code, `TUnitMap_SplitArmy`]**. Units, supply and money are allocated by hand, under the 198-army cap **[confirmed: the same report, `ptolemy-run-ui-inventory-and-leader-draw.md` §4]**; a real split moved 7 of 16 units and 100 of 256 talents in one order **[confirmed: save pair, [`pending-offer-block-army-split-and-naupactus.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/pending-offer-block-army-split-and-naupactus.md)]**. The split uses the transfer dialog's limits, because it is the same form **[confirmed: code, [`2026-10-03-army-to-army-ok-supply-rebalancing.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-03-army-to-army-ok-supply-rebalancing.md), "Split army uses the same form", and item 6]**: A is the selected army and B the new one, which opens with no units, supply or money. Units move between the two lists with **Transfer**, and the selected army keeps at least one unit (the engine's rule, `armies.invalid-unit-selection`). The supply spinner moves `min(step, room, giver's supply)` with the receiver's room `max(0, troops div 100 − supply + 1)`, and the money spinner `min(step, 1000 − receiver's money, giver's money)`, in steps of 10 and 100, as Transfer unit's do. **OK** submits **exactly one** `split-army <army> <new-army> <i,j,…> [supply=<tons>] [money=<talents>]` ([T141](T141.md)'s form): the indexes of the units in the second list, as the selected army numbers them before the split, and the supply and money netted toward the new army. T141's command then rebalances supply as the original's `OK` does. **Cancel** submits nothing (the original deletes the army it created on opening; the engine creates none before `OK`). The split dialog has no Disband buttons: Change units disbands a unit [designed: one dialog, one command, as Transfer unit's; the original's form shows Disband under each list]. **An army aboard a fleet**: the engine refuses its split (`armies.army-embarked`, **[designed, no confirmed evidence either way]**, `SplitArmyCommand`'s remarks), and whether the original allows it is **[open]**: the code read lists no such refusal for `TUnitMap_SplitArmy`, unlike Join armies, and where its new army would stand is untraced. So when the selected army is aboard a fleet (or a carrying fleet is selected), Split army opens no dialog, says *"An army aboard a fleet cannot be split."* **[designed wording]** and submits nothing.
  - **Join armies**: no dialog. It takes the `AdjacentPartner.Army` partner and submits `join-armies` (at most 20 units and 100,000 troops, neither aboard a fleet). With no partner, it shows the same message as Transfer unit.
  - **Change units** (`TChangeArmyUnits`, buttons Rename unit, Split unit, Join units, Disband, OK and Cancel; Rename unit opens *"Rename unit"* with Current name and New name, Split unit opens *"Split unit"* with 100s and 1000s spinners **[derived: form, the feature inventory, rows UA06, D05 and D06]**): rename, split, join and disband single units (T107's `rename-unit`, `split-unit` and `disband-unit`, and the existing `join-units`).
  - **Disband army**: `ConfirmPrompt` (T99) first, then `disband-army`. The engine keeps its rule that this happens only next to one of the nation's own cities.
  - **The context panel loses** the army panel's Disband button. (The city panel's Transfer Supply slider is removed by T134.)
  - **Regiment quality (experience) is shown**, as in the original's army information screen ([`army-records-and-roman-roster.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/army-records-and-roman-roster.md), whose Roman roster lists each regiment as average, good, very good or elite). Folded in by the user's decision of 2026-10-02. The unit list (T99's right-click view) and the army panel show each unit's quality next to its troops and type: tier 5 "poor", 6 "average", 7 "good", 8 "very good", 9 "elite". The report maps codes 6 to 9 explicitly, from the roster read against the save, and supports 5 as "poor" from a later 15,000-soldier transfer ([`city-units-army-transfer-and-mercenaries.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/city-units-army-transfer-and-mercenaries.md)). A tier below 5 shows its number until the original's captions for those tiers are read (**[open]**). A foreign army's unit list obeys T99's fog only where the fog already applies (moves, supply, morale, money); quality isn't withheld unless the evidence says the original hides it.
