- **Scope**:
  - **Protects**: the AI seat's turn as it plays today, command for command and draw for draw, around the battle (the commands before it, the battle's write-back, and the commands after it, as the AI would have proposed them from the written-back state), and every battle with no human seat. Forbidden: an AI turn that skips, repeats or reorders a command because of the suspension; an AI-phase battle against a human resolved by the instant path under `HumanBattles = Tactical`; any change under `HumanBattles = Instant`.
  - **The rule** **[confirmed: code (`FUN_0044AEE4` makes no exception for the attacker's side); Wine candidate: [`2026-10-02-two-human-seats.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-02-two-human-seats.md)]**: with `HumanBattles = Tactical`, when an AI seat's turn attacks an army of a human seat, the AI's command stops at the attack, the battle opens for that human (the defender places first, so the human acts first), and when it ends the AI seat's turn resumes from the written-back state. The battle is shown at once, to that human, after the handoff screen if the human is not the active seat; it is not held for the turn start.
  - **How the turn is suspended and resumed** **[designed: found by the implementer's attempt of 2026-10-13, which stopped before coding because the Owns list could not deliver the task]**. The AI turn runs inside `TurnCoordinator.RunTurn` (the Orders phase, then later phases), and nothing outside `AiTurn` can pause it, so the suspension is a **replay**: a gate around the dispatcher catches an AI command that attacks a human's army and makes `AiTurn` stop at it (the suspension point in `AiTurn.cs`); the battle opens on the state at that attack; when it ends, the same seat's turn is replayed from the same pre-turn state and seed with the battle's result injected at that attack, so every earlier proposal and draw is reproduced exactly (this also supports two battles in one turn). `TurnCoordinator` and `AiTurnSystem` are not edited. If the replay cannot reproduce the prefix command for command, stop and report: that is the hazard below. What was searched for an alternative and came up empty: the original resumes in place inside a call stack (`FUN_0044AEE4`'s caller), which has no clone counterpart; no report describes a coroutine form.
  - **Save while an AI-phase battle is open is refused** (the user's decision of 2026-10-13, over saving the continuation, which would need another save-format step): the `save` verb answers with a designed message (*"Finish the battle first: the turn is in progress."*, **[designed]**; searched on 2026-10-13 and found nothing: the research repository's `docs/` for "cannot save", "can't save", "unable to save" and "save … not allowed", and this repository's `docs/` and `src/` for the message's wording, so the original's own wording, if it has one, is unknown) and writes nothing, as T139 does for a pending offer. Reloading therefore never meets an AI-phase battle.
  - **Determinism.** The AI's stream advances exactly as if the battle had been resolved at that point; the battle's own draws are on its own stream (T125). A suspended and resumed turn gives the same state as one played without the suspension in which the battle's result is injected.
