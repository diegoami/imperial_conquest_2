#### T126 An AI seat's attack on a human's army opens the tactical battle during the AI phase

- **Design milestone**: M21 (the tactical battle, v0.5.0 *Battles*, [#496](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/496)). The user decided it on 2026-10-04 (#496, answer 2): an AI seat's attack on a human's army opens the tactical battle during the AI phase, as in the original. T116's option A stays the behaviour under `HumanBattles = Instant`. **Labels**: `phase:2 lane:engine`
- **Branch**: `task/T126-ai-phase-tactical-battles` · **Model/effort**: **Opus** / High. The AI seat's turn becomes resumable at a command boundary, which is an engine seam ([§3.3](../build-process.md#33-model-selection)): an error there changes every AI turn · **Reviewer**: **very complex** under [§3.4](../build-process.md#34-why-the-reviewers-model-differs-from-the-implementers) criterion (a): Claude implements, so Sol **and** the Luna pair
- **Start after**: T125, T116 · **Merge after**: T125, T116. T127 merges after this task when this task runs. It is **never in flight with** a task that edits `src/IC2.Engine/Ai/AiTurn.cs`, `AiMilitaryPhase.cs`, `GameSession.cs` or `godot/Screens/BattleReportRouter.cs`.
- **Owns**:
  - `src/IC2.Engine/Ai/AiTurn.cs`: **only** a suspension point after a dispatched command whose outcome is an open battle, and the resume entry;
  - `src/IC2.Engine/Presentation/GameSession.cs`: **only** the AI loop `PlayUntilOneFullLapOrRepeat` (~:1343–1385) and `AdvanceToHumanSeat` (~:663), to stop on an open battle and to resume the seat's turn after it;
  - `src/IC2.Engine/Presentation/TacticalBattleSession.cs`: **only** the opening of a battle from an AI seat's command, with the human as defender;
  - `godot/Screens/BattleReportRouter.cs`: **only** so that a battle the human already fought on the board is not held for its turn start;
  - `tests/IC2.Engine.Tests/Presentation/AiPhaseTacticalBattleTests.cs` (new), and `tests/IC2.Engine.Tests/Ui/Screens/BattleReportRouterTests.cs`: **only** new cases.
- **Scope**:
  - **Protects**: the AI seat's turn as it plays today, command for command and draw for draw, around the battle (the commands before it, the battle's write-back, and the commands after it, as the AI would have proposed them from the written-back state), and every battle with no human seat. Forbidden: an AI turn that skips, repeats or reorders a command because of the suspension; an AI-phase battle against a human resolved by the instant path under `HumanBattles = Tactical`; any change under `HumanBattles = Instant`.
  - **The rule** **[confirmed: code (`FUN_0044AEE4` makes no exception for the attacker's side); Wine candidate: [`2026-10-02-two-human-seats.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-02-two-human-seats.md)]**: with `HumanBattles = Tactical`, when an AI seat's turn attacks an army of a human seat, the AI's command stops at the attack, the battle opens for that human (the defender places first, so the human acts first), and when it ends the AI seat's turn resumes from the written-back state. The battle is shown at once, to that human, after the handoff screen if the human is not the active seat; it is not held for the turn start.
  - **Determinism.** The AI's stream advances exactly as if the battle had been resolved at that point; the battle's own draws are on its own stream (T125). A suspended and resumed turn gives the same state as one played without the suspension in which the battle's result is injected.
- **Done when**:
  1. `AiPhaseTacticalBattleTests`: with `Tactical`, an AI seat's turn that attacks the human's army stops with an open battle and the human to place; after `battle general on` plays it out, the AI seat's turn resumes, and its remaining commands equal those of a reference run that applies the same battle result at the same point (compared command by command). Two AI attacks on the human in one turn open two battles, in order.
  2. With `Instant`, the same scenario gives the state and output of `main` byte for byte; an AI-versus-AI battle in the same turn resolves at once in both modes.
  3. `BattleReportRouterTests`' new case: a battle the human fought on the board is not held; T116's own cases pass unchanged.
  4. `AiSoakTests`, `BattleDeterminismTests`, `SeatCliTests` and the CLI goldens pass unchanged.
  5. `dotnet build IC2.sln` and `dotnet test IC2.sln` are green, and the diff lists only Owns paths.

  - **The suspension must not change the AI.** If `AiTurn`'s loop cannot stop and resume at a command boundary without changing what it proposes, stop and report: re-planning the AI turn is a design question, not this task's.
