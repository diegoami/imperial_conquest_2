- **Scope**: orders are given on the map, as on the original's Unit map. The evidence is the audit's §2.1, read from `TUnitMap_UnitMapClick`, `TUnitMap_SelectUnit` and `TUnitMap_CheckForMove` **[derived: code]**; the prompts and the war declaration are **[confirmed]** by `decompiled-diplomacy-peace-terms-and-instant-battles.md`. The context panel's **Move** and **Attack** buttons go, and so does the armed "pending action" (`BeginMoveOrder`, `BeginAttackOrder`, `ResolvePendingAction`).
  - **`MapClickRules`** is a pure function: from the selection, the clicked tile's contents, the mouse button, the relations, the distances and the selected unit's moves, it returns one outcome: select, show the unit list, move, move fleet, attack army, besiege city, attack fleet, embark, disembark, drop the selection and show the target, or nothing. It holds no Godot type, so the tests compile it as `CommandOutcomeText.cs` is compiled.
  - **Left click on an own army, fleet or city** selects it and shows its details. **Right click on any city, army or fleet** shows its unit list (a city's garrison, an army's units, a fleet's ships and any army aboard) and changes neither the selection nor any order (the user's decision on the two mouse buttons).
  - **An own army selected**:
    - a land tile, at any distance: `move <army> <x> <y>`. The engine walks toward it, partway when the moves fall short. The army **stays selected while it has moves left**, and the selection ends when they reach 0;
    - a foreign army: `attack-army`; a foreign city: `besiege-city`;
    - an own fleet with no army aboard: `embark-army`, and the fleet becomes the selection;
    - the attack, besiege and embark outcomes need moves ≥ 1 and a target at Chebyshev distance exactly 1. Otherwise the selection is dropped and the target shown;
    - an own city: the city becomes the selection, and the army gets no order.
  - **An own fleet selected**: a sea tile: `move-fleet`, with the same stay-selected rule. A foreign fleet at distance 1 with moves ≥ 1: `attack-fleet`. With an army aboard, a land tile at distance 1: `disembark-army`; the selection ends and the army's details show. A fleet never besieges a city or attacks an army.
  - **The attack prompt.** Before `attack-army`, `besiege-city` or `attack-fleet` against a nation the active seat is **not at war with**, `ConfirmPrompt` asks *"Are you sure you want to attack this city ?"* (or *army*, *fleet*), with the original's own spacing. **Yes** submits the order; `GameSession` already composes the declaration of war in front of it (`ComposeDeclareWarIfNeeded`), so the UI never submits a second `declare-war`. **No** drops the selection. At war, there is no prompt.
  - **Foreign-army fog.** The army panel shows a foreign army's moves, supply, morale and money as withheld (no number), as the original's does **[confirmed: `ptolemy-run-ui-inventory-and-leader-draw.md` §5]**. Fleets and cities are unchanged.
  - **Cancel selection**: **Shift+X** **[confirmed: the same report, §4]** and **Esc** **[designed]** clear the selection. The menu entry is T100's.
  - The selection also ends after an attack, a besiege, a disembark, or a unit's moves reaching 0.

- **Hazards**:
  - **The click model is [derived: code], and an EXPLORE experiment in the original is confirming it** (requested 2026-10-01). This task does not wait for it. If the experiment disagrees (the button mapping, mouse-down versus mouse-up, or what a refused target shows), the difference is a follow-up through the bug list, not a reason to hold this task.
  - **The right click is the user's rule, not the audit's reading.** In the audit, `SelectUnit` runs after either button, so a right click could also attack. The user's decision on the two mouse buttons says a right click only opens the unit list; follow the decision.
  - **Embark and disembark stay unreachable in play until T93** ([#456](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/456)): the engine still wants the same tile (bug #453). Assert the composed command in `MapClickRulesTests`, never the engine's acceptance; `CommandVerbCatalog.ConfirmedUnreachable` still lists both.
  - The original refuses an attack on a fleet docked at its own city (*"You cannot attack a fleet docked at its own city !"*). If `AttackLegality` lacks that rule, file it on the bug list; it is not this task's file.
  - `.Text` is not what the player sees. Assert the fog on the laid-out panel after layout frames, as T96 does.
