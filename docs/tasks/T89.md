- **Scope**: The city loop of the original's quarterly tick (`FUN_00451B40`) calls `FUN_0044C204` for every **non-capital city with loyalty below 30** ([`city-population-growth.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/city-population-growth.md) :41, **[confirmed]**). In outline, from that report and [`dat-neighbour-mask.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/dat-neighbour-mask.md) §5:
  1. **Rebirth.** If the city's allegiance is another nation and that nation is dead (unity below 1), the rebirth check runs. That belongs to **T87**. Here, this branch does nothing except leave room for T87's call.
  2. **Allegiance.** If the city's allegiance is a live nation other than its owner, the city defects to it.
  3. **An attacker.** If the city already belongs to its allegiance nation, it goes to a nation at war with the owner that has an army within 10 tiles.
  4. **A neighbour.** Failing that, it goes to the best nation `n` that neighbours the owner (the owner's bit is in `n`'s mask) and has unity above 0. The score is `cities(n) − 2 × distance(city, capital(n))`.

  The transfer is the original's defection (`FUN_0044BED8`), with its news line "*C defects from X to Y.*" and, when this was the owner's last city, its elimination block.

  **The exact rule** (branch order and conditions, the distance metric, how the army search runs, tie-breaks, the no-candidate case, and any `Random` draws) comes from research `decompiled-quarterly-rebellion.md`, which is being written for this task. Where it differs from the outline above, the research wins.
