#### T200 New Game lists every playable scenario, and the nation menus come from the chosen world

- **Release / priority**: 0.7 series ([release-plan §2.4](../release-plan.md#24-planned-07-series-an-additional-playable-campaign) item 1); P1. Issue [900](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/900), "Must change" items 1 and 2.
- **Start after**: nothing (disjoint from T199's engine files). **Blocks**: T202's playthrough, T204.

### Problem

- New Game reaches only the two classical scenarios: `RulesetPresets` hard-codes two cards with fixed scenario ids (`godot/UI/RulesetPresets.cs`).
- The Nations menu and the toolbar's 16 swatches are a static table of the classical ids (`GameCommandTable.NationRows`). On another world, picking a nation throws (`NationStatusModel`).
- `GameDataRepository` already loads every `data/scenarios/*.json`, so the data side is ready.

### Scope

1. **New Game lists scenarios from `GameDataRepository`.** Each card shows the scenario's own name, its world and ruleset, and its victory line. The victory line is read from the resolved scenario (after T199) or from the ruleset (before it).
   - The two classical presets keep their current cards and wording. `RulesetPresetsTests` pins those.
   - Test and example scenarios stay out of the list unless marked playable. **User decision D2:** add an optional `"listed": true` scenario field (recommended, defaulting to false for new files), or list everything except `toy-*`.
2. **Nation rows come from the live world.** The Nations menu rows and the toolbar swatches are built from `World.Nations` in turn order at game start, with up to 16 nations. The command ids keep the `nations.<id>` shape, so the classical world's ids, labels and order are unchanged.
3. **No nation-specific code path throws on another world.** That covers `NationStatusModel`, the ContextPanel, the Area map's nation views and the seat screen.

### Owns

- `godot/UI/RulesetPresets.cs`, `godot/UI/RulesetChooserScreen.cs`, `godot/UI/NewGameFlow.cs`, `godot/UI/NewGameSelection.cs` and `godot/UI/ScenarioSeatScreen.cs`.
- `godot/UI/GameCommandTable.cs`, `godot/UI/CommandToolbar.cs` and `godot/UI/GameMenuBar.cs`, for the nation rows only.
- `godot/UI/MainGameScreen.cs`, for nation-row binding only.
- `godot/UI/NationStatusModel.cs`.
- `data/scenarios/*.json`, for the `listed` field only, per D2.
- Tests in `tests/IC2.Engine.Tests/Ui/**`, and `godot/Checks/{RulesetFlowCheck,MenuBarCheck,NationsAreaMapCheck}.cs`.

### Done when

1. On the classical world the menu bar, the toolbar and New Game are unchanged. `GameCommandTableTests`, `MenuBarCheck` and `RulesetFlowCheck` pass with their current expectations.
2. A third listed scenario, such as `example-tiny-duel` marked listed in a test-only data root, appears in New Game. Starting it gives Nations rows and swatches for exactly that world's nations, and every nation row opens its status panel without throwing. Both are shown by a headless check on the real `MainGameScreen`.
3. Save, load and resume of the non-classical game rebuilds the same rows.
4. `dotnet test` and all Godot check scenes pass, and CI is green.
