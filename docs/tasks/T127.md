#### T127 The Godot battle screen and the *Battle ended* window

- **Design milestone**: M21 (the tactical battle, v0.5.0 *Battles*, [#496](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/496)) and M18 (the Godot UI). It draws the battle T125 opens, takes the human's orders on the board, and shows the *Battle ended* window; it switches the Godot UI's sessions to `HumanBattles = Tactical`. **Labels**: `phase:3 lane:ui single-instance`
- **Branch**: `task/T127-battle-screen` · **Model/effort**: Sonnet / High · **Reviewer**: **Opus**/Medium **+ human visual review** (the tier rule decides; complex)
- **Start after**: T125, T128 · **Merge after**: T125, T128, and T126 when T126 runs. It is **never in flight with** another task or fix under `godot/**`, except an engine task's one-line entry in `godot/UI/CommandVerbCatalog.cs`.
- **Owns**:
  - `godot/Screens/TacticalBattleScreen.cs` (new): the board, icons, cursor, toolbar, side panel, unit lists, caption and the surrender confirmation;
  - `godot/Screens/TacticalBattleViewModel.cs` (new, Godot-free): what each cell, button and panel line shows, from T125's `TacticalBattleView`;
  - `godot/Screens/BattleEndedScreen.cs` (new) and `godot/Screens/BattleEndedViewModel.cs` (new, Godot-free), from T125's `TacticalBattleReport`;
  - `godot/UI/MainGameScreen.cs`: **only** opening the battle screen when the output carries an open battle, routing its orders to the session, and showing the *Battle ended* window and then any peace offer;
  - `godot/UI/GameMenuBar.cs`: **only** disabling Save and Save As while a battle is open;
  - `godot/UI/NewGameSelection.cs` (~:113) and `godot/UI/LoadGameScreen.cs` (~:176): **only** `HumanBattles = Tactical` on the session they build;
  - `godot/Checks/TacticalBattleCheck.cs` and `godot/Checks/TacticalBattleCheck.tscn` (new);
  - `godot/Checks/*.cs` and `godot/Screens/Checks/ScreensCheck.cs`: **only** the steps where a human attack now opens a battle, driven through it with *Computer general on* (Done-when 3), each named in the PR;
  - `tests/IC2.Engine.Tests/Ui/Screens/TacticalBattleViewModelTests.cs` and `BattleEndedViewModelTests.cs` (new), and their `<Compile Include>` lines in `tests/IC2.Engine.Tests/IC2.Engine.Tests.csproj`.
- **Scope**:
  - **Protects**: that the screen shows the engine's battle state exactly (every cell's icon code, every unit's numbers, every exchange) and sends only T125's orders; that no rule lives in the screen; and that Save and Save As cannot run while a battle is open. Forbidden: a rule computed in Godot code, a battle the screen can leave without finishing, surrendering or quitting, and a change to how an instant battle is shown (T25, T116).
  - **What it shows** (`game-design.md`, "The battle screen" and the User interface's item 3, **[confirmed: code, static, report §10; Wine candidate: [`2026-10-04-battle-probe.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-04-battle-probe.md) item 1]**): the 14 × 12 board of 32 px cells drawn from the icon codes (T128's keys, tinted per side's nation by `BattleIconTint`), empty cells as ground, the selection cursor; the caption `A  v  D          N to place units.` / `to move units.`; the eight toolbar buttons with their tooltips; the side panel with the selected unit's details (the morale tier `(morale − 27) div 8` with the original's six names) and the last exchange (`SHOOTS AT` / `TROOP LOSSES n` / `Unit destroyed !`; `ATTACKS` / `UNIT  LOSSES`, each side's `n` or `routed`); the three unit lists; *Change pauses* as a display-only delay between exchanges [designed: default 0 s; whether it is a saved setting is **[open: #496]**].
  - **Orders**: the original's mouse orders as `game-design.md` lists them (left click selects, places, moves, sets a melee target; right click shoots, or clears an own unit's target), each sent as T125's matching order. A click on the side panel during a computer-played half-round turns *Computer general* off. The layout around the board is **[designed]**.
  - **The *Battle ended* window**: the winner line, the per-type and total table (winner's start and finish, loser's start), the money and supplies lines as the original words them; OK applies T125's end, then the peace offer if any.
- **Done when**:
  1. `TacticalBattleViewModelTests` and `BattleEndedViewModelTests`, on reports and views built by T125's session from scripted battles: each cell's key and tint side, the caption for each phase, each panel line, the morale tier names at their edges, and the window's table and wording (with the original's `"no suuplies."`) equal the engine's values. They fail on `main` and pass after.
  2. **The headless `TacticalBattleCheck`** builds the real `MainGameScreen` on a scripted classical-mediterranean state with Rome's army adjacent to an AI army, issues the attack, and asserts: the battle screen is the active overlay with the defender's placement done and Rome to place; a placement and End turn reach the engine; *Computer general on* plays it to the end; the *Battle ended* window's numbers equal the engine's `TacticalBattleReport`; after OK the map returns with the post-battle state. While the battle is open, the menu bar's Save and Save As are disabled.
  3. **Instant battles are shown as before.** An AI-versus-AI battle and a siege in the same check show the battle-result window as on `main`; `ScreensCheck`, `AiPhaseBattleCheck`, `MenuBarCheck`, `MapClickCheck` and `SaveResumeCheck` pass (any of their human attacks that now open a battle is driven through it with *Computer general on*, and the PR names each such edit).
  4. `dotnet build IC2.sln` and `dotnet test IC2.sln` are green, the `godot-headless` job is green, `scripts/check-godot-churn.ps1` leaves a clean tree, and the diff lists only Owns paths.
- **Hazards**:
  - **The human visual review** plays one battle in a windowed run (placement, a shot, a melee, a rout, the end) and posts screenshots of each phase and of the *Battle ended* window. It is the reviewer's gate, not a numbered Done-when.
  - **No rules in Godot.** Range, adjacency and legality come from the engine's view; a click the engine refuses shows its message.
  - **Checks whose human attacks change.** Switching the UI to `Tactical` makes a human attack in an existing check open a battle. Drive it through *Computer general on* and say so in the PR; never switch a check back to `Instant` to keep it green, unless the check is about instant battles.
  - **Never two Godot processes at once**: run the headless checks one at a time.
