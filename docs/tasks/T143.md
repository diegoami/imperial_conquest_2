- **Scope**:
  - **Protects**: the gate itself. `MercenaryHireCost.Compute` stays `(troops × quarterlyPrice[type]) / mercenaryHireTroopDivisor × quality`, and an army whose purse is below it is refused with `mercenary.insufficient-money` and *"Your army has too little money to pay these mercenaries."*, as today. Every other refusal, its code and its order (T76's distance and war refusals included), the pool slot's consumption, the hired unit's fields, the quarterly mercenary pay (`ArmyUpkeep`), the AI's hire (T76's, free) and both presets' flags are unchanged. Forbidden: an edit under `src/IC2.Engine/Ai/**` or `src/IC2.Engine/Economy/**`; a ruleset key or flag; a change to `tests/fixtures/corpus.json`, a CLI golden or the save format.
  - **The price is a gate, not a charge** **[derived: code, `TRecruitMercs_RecruitMercUnit` @ 00441360 refuses when `purse < (troops × price[type] div 1000) × quality` (:43633-43639), and nothing in the function writes the purse or the treasury (:43618-43701); Wine candidate: two hires left army 1's purse at 30 and the treasury at 2,270 both times, `Q4_03` → `Q4_04` → `Q4_06`, release `run-exp-v050-rules`]**. An accepted `hire-mercenary` changes the army only by its new unit, and changes no purse and no treasury.
  - **Both presets** **[designed for `improved`: the user's decisions keep a preset difference only where they name one, and none was named for the hire; `flags.economyPurses` is not read by the hire today and is not made to be]**.
  - **The first charge is the quarterly pay** `((troops div 200) × price × quality) div 5` per unit, from the purse **[derived: code, `FUN_00451B40`; the same report]**, which `ArmyUpkeep` already applies; this task does not touch it.
  - **The event**: `MercenaryHired` keeps its type and its domain-event name, `recruitment.mercenary-hired`; its last field becomes `HireGate`, the value `MercenaryHireCost.Compute` returned, so a reader cannot take it for money paid.
