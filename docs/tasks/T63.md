- **Scope**: The merged instant resolver ports the **field** battle's arithmetic faithfully and nothing else. Two research passes on 2026-09-23 read the siege, naval and storm call sites and the casualty helper itself at **instruction level**. They found four confirmed defects in merged code, all in the arithmetic around `FUN_0044AE20`:

  | Bug | What the merged code does | What the original does |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | #289 | never deletes a unit after casualties | deletes any unit left below `standardBattalionSize / 10` (national) or `/ 5` (mercenary), before the promotion roll, at every call site |
  | #290 | siege and naval casualties use the field ratio `loser × 40 / winner`; the naval whole-unit loss removes `n` units, by shift | siege: `max(1, min(15, def × 6 / atk))`; naval: ratio `d`, whole-unit loss `n + 1`, swap-with-last |
  | #292 | storms compute `r = dmg + 100` (so `d ≥ 112`, rising with damage) and never touch the army aboard | `r = max(1, 10000 / (dmg + 100))` (so `d` falls from 86 to 57), and the army aboard takes the casualty pass |
  | #293 | a siege attempt never changes the city | every attempt strips a pending fortification order, then erodes loyalty, fortification and population, then floors population |

  **The formulas below are the contract**, each `[confirmed]` from research commits [`3f6ca09`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/commit/3f6ca09) and [`1762c84`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/commit/1762c84). They are written up in [`decompiled-defection-and-siege-attrition.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/decompiled-defection-and-siege-attrition.md) (siege, `FUN_0044b27c`, `FUN_0044b230`), [`decompiled-diplomacy-peace-terms-and-instant-battles.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/decompiled-diplomacy-peace-terms-and-instant-battles.md) (naval, `FUN_0044B5D0`, `FUN_0044B4F8`) and [`supply-driven-morale-and-fleet-attrition.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/supply-driven-morale-and-fleet-attrition.md) (storm call, `0x00451823`). All arithmetic is integer, truncating toward zero.

  - **Deletion pass** (`FUN_0044AE20`, #289): after the casualty loop and **before** promotion, delete every unit whose troops are below `standardBattalionSize / 10` (national) or `standardBattalionSize / 5` (mercenary). A deleted unit gets no promotion roll. The pass runs at every call site, including a naval or storm pass at ratio `0`.
  - **Siege** (`FUN_0044b27c`, #290 and #293). Per attempt, in this order:
    1. strip a pending fortification order: `if fort > 100 then fort = fort % 100`;
    2. compute `def` (the existing `CompleteDefenderStrength`, including its `× 9/10` when the army's owner equals the city's allegiance) and `atk` (the existing `SiegeStrength.Attacker`);
    3. erode, for loyalty, then fortification, then population: `f = max((3f)/4, min((19f)/20 + 1, (f × def)/atk))`;
    4. floor population: `pop = max(pop, maxPop/6 + 1)`;
    5. apply the attacker's casualties with `ratio = max(1, min(15, (def × 6)/atk))`, **on a success or a failure alike**, including the deletion pass;
    6. then the existing win test and capture.

    The defender's troops take no casualties anywhere on this path.
  - **Naval** (`FUN_0044B4F8(winnerFleet, loserPower, winnerPower)`, called in both branches; a tie goes to the defender):
    1. `r = max(1, (loser × 100)/winner)`, then `d = (r × r)/100`, which lies in `0…100`;
    2. `ships −= ships × d/300`, then `condition −= condition × d/300`;
    3. the carried army takes the casualty pass with ratio `d`;
    4. if it is still alive and `d > 70`, remove `(count × d)/250 + 1` units, each chosen by `Random(current count)` through `IRng`. Remove it **swap-with-last**, and re-read the count every iteration. If the army empties, delete it and clear `FleetState.CarriedArmyId`.
  - **Storm** (`0x00451823`, #292): the same function, called as `(fleet, 100, dmg + 100)`, so `r = max(1, 10000/(dmg + 100))`. It includes step 3 and step 4 for the army aboard.

  **Edge-case decisions** (proposed by the main session on 2026-09-23 and confirmed by the user on review of this entry's branch):
  - **Decision 1, the 16-bit clamp** (the original compares the clamp in 16 bits, so a near-empty besieger's ratio can wrap to 1 instead of 15): a new `bugPolicySiegeRatioClamp` field. `classical-faithful` reproduces the 16-bit comparison; `improved` clamps in 32 bits. This follows the existing `bugPolicyDiplomaticThaw` pattern ([`game-design.md` "Two shipped presets"](../game-design.md), Q8).
  - **Decision 1, extended 2026-09-23 by the user:** the erosion's `(f × def)/atk` also wraps in 16 bits in the original. The same `bugPolicySiegeRatioClamp` field governs it: `classical-faithful` reproduces both 16-bit behaviours, and `improved` computes both in 32 bits. The wrap is a **signed** 16-bit comparison (`JG`/`JL` at `0x00448FD0`/`0x00448FD8`), not an unsigned one.
  - **Deletion-pass slot order, decided 2026-09-23 by the user:** the deletion pass matches the original's removal. It walks from slot 19 down and moves the last unit into each deleted slot (swap-with-last), the same removal the naval whole-unit loss uses. It does not keep the survivors' order.
  - **Decision 2, zero strength** (the original divides by zero for a siege at `atk = 0`, or a naval battle at `winner = 0`): in **both presets**, the command that would start such a battle is rejected with a typed reason, before any state changes. `[designed]`: the original crashes here, so there is no behaviour to port.
  - **Decision 3, the emptied besieger** (in the original, a winning besieger emptied by its own casualties still captures, and the city gets owner −1): the model cannot represent an ownerless city (`CityState.Owner` is a non-null nation id). So in **both presets**, a besieger emptied by its casualties does not capture: the attempt resolves as failed, after erosion. `[designed]`, and it departs from the original. It was proposed as reproduce-in-faithful, and is changed here because that is not representable.
  - **Decision 4, presets**: every formula above applies to **both** presets. These are confirmed core arithmetic, not bugs to reproduce.
  - **Decision 6, draw count**: the original always draws exactly 20 `Random(15)` per casualty pass. `IRng` cannot replay the original's sequence anyway, so the port keeps **one draw per unit**, as a documented departure; T59's hazard already records it.
  - **Decision 7, battle result**: `BattleResult` gains the city's loyalty, fortification and population before and after a siege attempt, for T25's screen. Keep it presentation-agnostic.
