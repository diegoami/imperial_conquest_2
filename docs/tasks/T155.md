- **Scope**:
  - **Protects**: the original's rule that a town takes a new recruitment order **only if it is its nation's capital or its current fortification is at least 75%**. No order path, whether the CLI, the Godot dialog, the city panel or the AI, may place a recruitment in another town. A town that drops below 75% **keeps** the units already in training, and they still mobilize.
  - **The evidence** ([#515](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/515) and its comments; research [`2026-09-29-which-cities-may-recruit-and-troop-amounts.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-09-29-which-cities-may-recruit-and-troop-amounts.md)):
    - The dialog's town list (`FUN_004544E0`) and `TArmyRecruits.RecruitUnit` (`0x454E78`) admit the capital or a town whose current fortification (city `+0x1A`) is ≥ 75. Values over 100 encode a pending order, `points×100 + current`, so the test reads the **current** part **[derived: code]**.
    - 118 of 118 training towns in six saves satisfy it **[confirmed: saves]**.
    - Live, a town raised from 72% to 75% recruits the next turn, and a town at 69% is absent **[confirmed: a headless run]**.
    - The capital exception is **[derived: code]** only, because Rome starts at 78%; the entry keeps it as the code reads.
  - **Folded #903:** the Recruit unit dialog's training list, cost and mobilisation labels read a model built once at open (`RecruitUnitDialog.cs:107`). They must reflect each Recruit, Mobilize and Disband at once.
  - **Out of scope**: the original's recruitment AI (capital only, random types, `battalion/3 + Random(4·battalion/5)`, at most 8 orders a turn, never fortifies), and the Build fleet dialog unless it shares #903's stale-snapshot defect. If it does, list that in the PR as a finding for a follow-up; do not fix it here.
- **Done when**:
  1. **The engine refuses.** `recruit-standing` in a town that is neither its nation's capital nor at current fortification ≥ the ruleset's threshold is refused, with a reason naming the town and the rule. Tests, each failing when the check is removed:
     - a capital at 10% recruits;
     - a non-capital town at 74% is refused and one at 75% recruits;
     - a town at current 70% with a pending order to 85% (the encoded word) is refused;
     - a town that falls from 80% to 60% keeps its training slots, and `mobilize` on them still succeeds.
  2. **The threshold is ruleset data.** `recruitment.recruitTownMinFortificationPercent` (or an equivalently named key) is 75 in `classical-faithful` and `improved`, with `_provenance` citing the report and `[derived: code]`. A corpus entry carries the 75, under build-process §2.4's top-up contract. `toy-ruleset.json` carries the key too, at a value that keeps its existing scenarios and tests meaningful, and the PR says what value and why.
  3. **One predicate.** The engine, the AI's candidate cities and the UI all call `RecruitmentEligibility`. A test shows that the AI never issues a recruitment the engine refuses, and the 50-seed soak still reports **0 rejected**.
  4. **The UI follows.**
     - Strategy → Recruit unit lists only the towns that may recruit; *All cities* shows the training of every owned town but offers recruitment only in eligible ones.
     - The city panel's Recruit control is absent or disabled, with the rule's reason, in an ineligible town.
     - A headless Godot check shows both.
  5. **#903 is fixed.** After Recruit, Mobilize or Disband, the dialog's training list, cost and mobilisation labels match `Session.State` without reopening. A test or Godot check fails before the fix and passes after.
  6. The goldens that change are regenerated through the CLI and listed in the PR with their reason; `dotnet build IC2.sln` and `dotnet test IC2.sln` are green; the headless Godot checks pass; and the diff lists only Owns paths.
- **Hazards**:
  - **Every seeded `classical-mediterranean` measurement may shift** if the AI recruited in ineligible towns before: list each changed golden, soak log or seeded test with that reason.
  - **Do not re-implement the recruit or the fortification order.** T55's and T115's handlers stay as they are, except for the one refusal.
