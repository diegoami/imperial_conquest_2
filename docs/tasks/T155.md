- **Scope**:
  - **Protects**: the original's rule that a town takes a new recruitment order **only if it is its nation's capital, or its fortification word is at least 75**. The word is the current level, plus 100 for each point of a pending fortify order, so a town with any pending order passes whatever its current level. No order path, whether the CLI, the Godot dialog, the city panel or the AI, may place a recruitment in another town. A town that drops below 75% **keeps** the units already in training, and they still mobilize or disband.
  - **The evidence** ([#515](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/515) and its comments; research [`2026-09-29-which-cities-may-recruit-and-troop-amounts.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-09-29-which-cities-may-recruit-and-troop-amounts.md)):
    - **The order** (`TArmyRecruits.RecruitUnit`, `0x454E78`) and **the AI's recruitment** both compare the **raw** fortification word (city `+0x1A`) with 74. Over 100, the word encodes a pending order, `points×100 + current`, so any town with a pending order passes, whatever its current level. The capital always passes **[derived: code; #515's stage-2 comment, item 1]**. The engine's refusal follows this.
    - **The dialog's town list** (`FUN_004544E0`) decodes the word (`% 100`). It shows a town if it is the capital, if its **current** fortification is ≥ 75, or if it **already has units in training** **[derived: code; #515's first research comment]**. That last case lets units in training be mobilized or disbanded from the list. The Recruit unit dialog follows this list. Recruit is then offered only in towns the order would accept.
    - 118 of 118 training towns in six saves satisfy it **[confirmed: saves]**.
    - Live, a town raised from 72% to 75% recruits the next turn, and a town at 69% is absent **[confirmed: a headless run]**.
    - The capital exception is **[derived: code]** only, because Rome starts at 78%; the entry keeps it as the code reads.
  - **Folded #903:** the Recruit unit dialog's training list, cost and mobilisation labels read a model built once at open (`RecruitUnitDialog.cs:107`). They must reflect each Recruit, Mobilize and Disband at once.
  - **Out of scope**: the original's recruitment AI (capital only, random types, `battalion/3 + Random(4·battalion/5)`, at most 8 orders a turn, never fortifies), and the Build fleet dialog unless it shares #903's stale-snapshot defect. If it does, list that in the PR as a finding for a follow-up; do not fix it here.
