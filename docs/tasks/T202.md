#### T202 The Italy-before-Rome campaign: world, ruleset and scenario

- **Release / priority**: 0.7 series (release-plan §2.4 item 4); P1. Issue [900](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/900).
- **Start after**: T199 (the scenario states its own goal), T200 (reachable from New Game) and T201 (terrain authoring).

### Decisions the user makes before this task is ready (D3–D6)

- **D3, date and length.** When the campaign starts and ends: the start year, the end year (the reign limit), and whether there is a scenario turn limit.
- **D4, nations.** At most 16, from #900's list: Etruscans, Veneti, Oscans, Samnites, Greek colonies, Latins/Rome, Umbrians, Gauls of the Po, Carthage as an outside power, and others. Also each nation's colours and which seats are human-playable.
- **D5, map.** The extent (the Italian peninsula with Sicily and Corsica/Sardinia? the Po valley?), the grid size and orientation (portrait is likely), and the city list and density.
- **D6, barbarians.** Either in the minimum campaign (which needs T203's mechanic first) or ordinary Gaulish factions now with the mechanic later. Either way #900's barbarian request stays open until T203 resolves it.

### Scope (after D3–D6)

1. **A new world**, `data/worlds/italy-<year>.json` plus its terrain sidecar from T201's importer, with cities, nations, starting relations and starting armies and fleets.
2. **A ruleset copy**, because rulesets have no inheritance. It keeps the classical rules and changes only the calendar years and the map-scale constants the campaign needs. Each change is marked `[designed]` with its reason.
3. **A scenario** with seats, the victory condition and goal, and `listed: true`.
4. **Provenance.** Every historical fact (a city, a date, a faction) cites a source under the existing provenance rules. Everything else is marked `[designed]`. No original-game data or art is used.

### Owns

New files only: `data/worlds/italy-*.json` and its sidecar, `data/rulesets/italy-*.json`, `data/scenarios/italy-*.json`, the source PNG under `tools/` or `assets/` (our own art), and tests that load and resolve it.

### Done when

1. The world, ruleset and scenario resolve, pass cross-document validation (T199) and appear in New Game (T200).
2. A seeded all-AI game runs 200 seat-turns with no exception, no rejected command and no full-round stall (#1290's rule).
3. Every non-designed fact has a citation in the data's `_provenance`, checked by a test.
