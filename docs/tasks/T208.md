### Scope

Add independent global, default-off switches for fleet auto supply and auto repair. Use adjustable fleet supply target (percent of current ruleset capacity) and repair target (condition percent, no higher than the ruleset maximum). Reuse T207's treasury reserve for repair spending. Only an **owned city** eligible under the existing command's adjacency/ownership rules supplies a fleet; do not silently take supply from another fleet/army or buy from a foreign town. Only a launched, non-carrying fleet beside an owned city may be repaired. Execute in fleet state order on the same confirmed human End turn as T207, after army support; no action on Cancel/Make more moves or an AI seat.

Compose ordinary `buy-fleet-supply <fleet> <city> <tons>` and `repair-fleet <fleet> <points>` commands and re-read state after each accepted command. Request enough tons/points toward target, bounded by live town stock, capacity, ruleset maximum and the treasury reserve. The repair handler charges `ships × points / repairCostDivisor` and zeroes moves; choose a legal points amount whose calculated cost does not drive the treasury below the player's reserve. If none is affordable or the fleet is under construction/carrying an army/not at an owned town, skip and report why. No direct state mutation, hidden debt or automatic foreign purchase.
