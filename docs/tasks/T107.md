- **Scope**: the original's `TChangeArmyUnits` dialog renames, splits, joins and disbands single units (its methods are `RenameUnit`, `SplitUnit`, `JoinUnits`, `Disband` and `RemoveUnit`) **[confirmed: `decompiled-unit-map-orders-and-record-fields.md`]**. `join-units` exists; this task adds the other three.
  - **`split-unit <army> <unit> <troops>`** splits `<troops>` off a unit into a new unit of the same type, quality and regular-or-mercenary marker, in the same army. The new unit takes the auto-naming scheme T15 uses for a new unit. The army's unit cap (20) holds, and both parts keep at least the minimum troops the report gives.
  - **`rename-unit <army> <unit> <name>`** sets `UnitSlot.Name`, within the length the report gives for the record's name field.
  - **`disband-unit <army> <unit>`** removes the unit; its troops are lost. The report's rules for any refund and for an army's last unit hold.
  - All three act only on the active nation's own army, and `<unit>` is the unit's index in the army.
- **Done when**:
  1. `ChangeUnitsCommandTests`: on the classical scenario, `split-unit` of 1,000 troops off a Roman unit leaves two units of the same type, quality and marker, whose troops sum to the original. `rename-unit` sets the name. `disband-unit` removes the unit, and the army's troops fall by exactly its troops. It fails on `main` (no commands) and passes after.
  2. Each rule the report gives has a boundary test: a split at 20 units is rejected; a split that leaves either part below the minimum is rejected; a name past the field's length is rejected or cut, as the report says.
  3. A foreign army, an unknown army or a unit index out of range is rejected for each verb, with the state unchanged.
  4. `CommandCoverageTests` passes, with an **accepted** line for each of the three verbs in the success golden. The CI `OrderCoverageCheck` is green with the mirror entries.
  5. `dotnet build IC2.sln` and `dotnet test IC2.sln` are green, and the diff lists only Owns paths.
- **Hazards**:
  - **Where the report is silent, escalate.** In particular: what disbanding an army's last unit does (refuse it, or disband the army?); whether a disbanded mercenary or regular refunds anything; and what `RemoveUnit` is, as distinct from `Disband`. Don't decide them; a research read may be needed first.
  - `SplitArmyCommandHandler` and `JoinUnitsCommandHandler` are T15's: reuse their naming and cap helpers, and don't edit them.
