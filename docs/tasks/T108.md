- **Scope**: the original's `TArmyRecruits` dialog has three buttons over a city's units in training: **Recruit unit**, **Mobilize** and **Disband** (methods `RecruitUnit`, `MobilizeUnits` and `DisbandUnits`) **[confirmed: `menu-and-toolbar-inventory.md`, `ptolemy-run-ui-inventory-and-leader-draw.md` §1]**. The clone has `recruit-standing` and `mobilize`, and nothing removes a slot.
  - **`disband-slot <slot>`** removes the active nation's recruitment slot `<slot>` (its index in `NationState`'s slots). **Nothing is refunded**, for a ready slot and an unready one alike: the whole recruiting price, taken from the treasury when the order was placed, is lost **[derived: code, `TArmyRecruits_DisbandUnits` @ 004553B0 → `FUN_0044A610` never writes the treasury; Wine candidate: two disbands left Rome's treasury at 1,880 (`Q3_00` → `Q3_01` → `Q3_02`), the read named in Start after]**.
  - **The later slots shift up** to close the gap, and the nation's queue-full word is cleared (`FUN_0044A610` :49003-49014) **[derived; Wine candidate: Rome's queue went 5 → 4 → 3 with the later entries moved up]**.
  - **The original asks first**: *"Are you sure you want to disband N unit(s)."* (Yes / No / Cancel), and only Yes acts; with several entries selected each is processed, last row first, by the same formula **[derived: code, :56133-56170]**. The confirmation is the UI's (T109); the verb removes one slot.
  - The slot's upkeep stops from the next quarter, because the quarterly bill reads the slots.
  - **Two further facts the research read should settle** (added from the feature inventory [`2026-10-05-player-facing-feature-inventory.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-05-player-facing-feature-inventory.md), a provisional report, row S05b and row E18): the original asks *"Are you sure you want to disband ..."* first, and its help says the money already invested is lost **[help, not tested]**, which points to no refund; and disbanding a queued order **lowers the nation's mobilisation rate by exactly what placing it added**: `mobilized = max(0, mobilized − 1 − troops × 1000 / wealth)`, with `wealth` the nation record's `+0x430` (`NationState.Wealth`) and the division truncating **[confirmed: code, `TArmyRecruits_DisbandUnits` (`0x004553B0`), [`decompiled-mobilization-and-mercenary-restock.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/decompiled-mobilization-and-mercenary-restock.md) §5, the mirror of the order's `min(100, mobilized + 1 + troops × 1000 / wealth)`; the feature inventory's row E18 states the same amount since its revision at research `678e970`]**. `disband-slot` applies it (Done-when 7). Played: HI 3,200 took mobilisation 32 → **30** and HI 4,000 then 30 → **28** at wealth 2,577,000, where `4,000 × 1000 / 2,577,000 = 1.55` separates the floor (28) from rounding (27) **[Wine candidate: the same report]**. Because the order's rise is capped at 100 and this fall is not, the two are inverses only when the order did not hit the cap and wealth is unchanged: from 99, an order whose step is 2 gives 100 and its disband gives 98 **[derived]**.
- **Done when**:

It fails on `main` (no command) and passes after.

  4. One test for a ready slot and one for an unready slot, each with no refund (the report finds no difference between them).
  5. `CommandCoverageTests` passes, with an **accepted** `disband-slot` line in the success golden. The CI `OrderCoverageCheck` is green with the mirror entry.
  6. `dotnet build IC2.sln` and `dotnet test IC2.sln` are green, and the diff lists only Owns paths.
  7. `DisbandRecruitmentSlotCommandTests`: after `disband-slot` on a slot of `t` troops, the nation's `MobilizedPercent` equals `max(0, m − 1 − t × 1000 / Wealth)` from its value `m` before, computed from the state, not a literal; a case where that is below 0 leaves it at 0. It fails on `main` (no command) and passes after.
- **Hazards**:
  - **No refund, and none invented.** The read gives none; a refund here would be a rule with no source.
  - Slot indexes shift when a slot is removed, as in the original (above). Check that `mobilize` and the readiness system still find the right slots afterwards, and add a test if they don't.
