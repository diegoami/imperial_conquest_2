- **Scope**: the original's `TArmyRecruits` dialog has three buttons over a city's units in training: **Recruit unit**, **Mobilize** and **Disband** (methods `RecruitUnit`, `MobilizeUnits` and `DisbandUnits`) **[confirmed: `menu-and-toolbar-inventory.md`, `ptolemy-run-ui-inventory-and-leader-draw.md` §1]**. The clone has `recruit-standing` and `mobilize`, and nothing removes a slot.
  - **`disband-slot <slot>`** removes the active nation's recruitment slot `<slot>` (its index in `NationState`'s slots). Any refund, and any difference between a ready slot and an unready one, follows the research read named above.
  - The slot's upkeep stops from the next quarter, because the quarterly bill reads the slots.
  - **Two further facts the research read should settle** (added from the feature inventory [`2026-10-05-player-facing-feature-inventory.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-05-player-facing-feature-inventory.md), a provisional report, row S05b and row E18): the original asks *"Are you sure you want to disband ..."* first, and its help says the money already invested is lost **[help, not tested]**, which points to no refund; and disbanding a queued order **lowers the nation's mobilisation rate by exactly what placing it added**: `mobilized = max(0, mobilized − 1 − troops × 1000 / wealth)`, with `wealth` the nation record's `+0x430` (`NationState.Wealth`) and the division truncating **[confirmed: code, `TArmyRecruits_DisbandUnits` (`0x004553B0`), [`decompiled-mobilization-and-mercenary-restock.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/decompiled-mobilization-and-mercenary-restock.md) §5, the mirror of the order's `min(100, mobilized + 1 + troops × 1000 / wealth)`; the feature inventory's row E18 states the same amount since its revision at research `678e970`]**. `disband-slot` applies it (Done-when 7). The refund is still the read named in Start after.
- **Done when**:
  1. `DisbandRecruitmentSlotCommandTests`: on the classical scenario, after `recruit-standing` in Rome, `disband-slot` on that slot removes it, and the treasury changes by exactly the refund the research report gives (zero if it gives none). It fails on `main` (no command) and passes after.
  2. The next quarter's upkeep bill no longer includes the slot, asserted against the quarterly system's own result.
  3. An index out of range, or a slot of another nation, is rejected, with the state unchanged.
  4. If the report distinguishes ready and unready slots, one test for each.
  5. `CommandCoverageTests` passes, with an **accepted** `disband-slot` line in the success golden. The CI `OrderCoverageCheck` is green with the mirror entry.
  6. `dotnet build IC2.sln` and `dotnet test IC2.sln` are green, and the diff lists only Owns paths.
  7. `DisbandRecruitmentSlotCommandTests`: after `disband-slot` on a slot of `t` troops, the nation's `MobilizedPercent` equals `max(0, m − 1 − t × 1000 / Wealth)` from its value `m` before, computed from the state, not a literal; a case where that is below 0 leaves it at 0. It fails on `main` (no command) and passes after.
- **Hazards**:
  - **No research read, no task.** A refund invented here would be a rule with no source. If the report leaves the refund open, escalate rather than pick one.
  - Slot indexes shift when a slot is removed. Check that `mobilize` and the readiness system still find the right slots afterwards, and add a test if they don't.
