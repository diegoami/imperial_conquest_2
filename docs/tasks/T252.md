# T252 — Faithful recruitment chooses its order city once

Task issue: [#1648](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/1648). Corrects [#1645](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/1645), from [research `7d4168c`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/7d4168c/docs/reports/2026-10-31-ai-economy-vs-remake-main.md).

## Contract

Owns `AiFaithfulEconomy`'s recruitment order-city selection, focused tests and factual docs. Select the city once after the mobilization pass and before the order loop, as the original caller does. Keep its identity for the seat's new orders. Read the live city/nation for each command; do not retain a stale `CityState`, rerun `ChooseCity`, or select another city when queue composition changes.

Preserve budgets, deployments, queue/value limits, mobilization stop gates, tax policy, unit-type/size draws, command legality and safe no-city behavior. A city becoming invalid is a safe stop, not permission to resample the destination. Keep Improved economy unchanged. Add no save fields or global phase-driver redesign.

## Done when

1. A real-command regression fails before and passes after: enough orders cross the capital-queue fraction boundary, but every order remains at the one initially chosen city. Check recruitment slots and command destinations, not only the chosen helper result.
2. Verify independent selection on the next seat invocation, live population/ownership/queue validation, partial/full queues, no legal city and existing stopping gates. Preserve meaningful native type/size draw behavior without a new RNG source or seed search; retain safety at unmeasured invalid contexts.
3. Run economy/AI/determinism, native-export and affected CLI regressions, solution build and hosted CI. Attribute golden changes without deleting script commands, changing seeds or injecting offers.

Research compares 1,200 AI seats: four city differences, 141 earlier-state differences and 1,055 matching seats. The diagnostic chooses the original city in the four cases; it does not independently check every city-helper branch, draws, consolidation, supply/hire or rounds beyond five. Preserve these limits and the Wine-only/native-Windows distinction.
