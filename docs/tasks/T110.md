- **Scope**:
  - **The viewed nation.** Choosing one of the 16 nations, from the Nations menu or its toolbar swatch, makes it the *viewed nation*. Its menu item is checked, its swatch is pressed (a radio group), and its **status panel** shows in the context panel, for a foreign nation too **[derived: code, `TPremierForm_SelectNation`, `ChangeNation`]**. For the active seat's own nation, the panel lists the leader, capital, cities, population, unity as a word, tax %, mobilized %, treasury and the nation's relations **[confirmed: `ptolemy-run-ui-inventory-and-leader-draw.md` §1]**. **For a foreign nation it shows public facts only**: its cities, capital and leader, and its relation with the viewer's nation. It shows no treasury, tax rate, population, unity, mobilization or units in training, in line with the fog on foreign armies (the user's decision of 2026-10-01). **All nations** views every nation and shows no status panel. When a turn starts, the viewed nation is the active seat's **[designed]**.
  - **The highlights.** Area map → Show cities, Show capital, Show armies, Show fleets and Show all paint highlights on the mini-map (T102) for the viewed nation, or for every nation under All nations. They hide nothing **[derived: code, `TAreaMap_ShowCities` to `0x0043E440`]**. Each entry is a check item that turns its highlight on or off. Show all sets all four. Changing the viewed nation re-scopes the highlights that are on.
  - **The Area-map strip**: a row of `ui.command.*` buttons above the mini-map, for the five Show entries, the six Show mercenaries entries (disabled until T113) and Find a city, each running its table row.
  - **Find a city** (`TFindCity`): a nation dropdown (any nation) and that nation's cities, with capitals marked. Choosing a city centres the order map on it and highlights it on the mini-map **[derived: code, `TFindCity_FillListBox`, `ChangeCity`]**.
  - **The bottom toolbar goes.** Its Cities, Armies and Fleets toggles hid layers, which the original never does (the user's decision on highlights). T100 already moved its other buttons. `GameMapView` always draws every layer.

- **Hazards**:

  - **Unity as a word**: use the word table the report gives. If it gives none, show the number, and say so in the PR.
  - **How the original marks a capital in Find a city is [open]** (probably upper case). Mark it plainly, and leave a code comment.
  - `TAreaMap_ToggleMap` and the strip's gold-coin button stay out: what they do is **[open]** (audit §1.5, §3.2).
  - The keyboard shortcuts for these entries (N, A, C, F, L, Q) have unknown modifier keys (audit §1.8). Don't invent them.
  - The active nation's own panel keeps its list of units in training (bug #513's fix). A foreign nation's shows none (public facts only). Issue #520 item 2 (foreign *city* queues) is untouched.
