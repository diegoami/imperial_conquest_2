- **Scope**: the original's `TArmyToArmy` dialog shows two unit lists, with Transfer and Disband under each, and spinners for supply and money. Conservation is exact **[confirmed: `army-to-army-transfer-confirmed.md`]**. The clone has no command for it.
  - **`army-transfer <from> <to> [units=<i,j,…>] [supply=<tons>] [money=<talents>]`** moves the listed units of `from`, and the given supply and money, to `to`, all in one command. To move the other way, swap the two armies. Both armies belong to the active nation, and they stand at Chebyshev distance **exactly 1**, the original's partner rule. Troops, supply and money are conserved exactly, and the dialog's steppers never move more than the giving army holds (`min(otherArmyAmount, …)`) **[confirmed: `army-to-army-transfer-confirmed.md`]**. The receiving army keeps the army rules of `decompiled-unit-map-orders-and-record-fields.md`: at most 20 units and 100,000 troops (the Join armies limits), supply within its capacity (`troops div 100 + 1`, as `supply-capacity-rounding.md` corrects it), and the purse cap. **Whether `TArmyToArmy` itself enforces any of these is [open]**: neither report reads a cap in its code.
  - **An army left with no units** has its supply and money merged into the other army, and is disbanded (`FUN_0044ab90`) **[confirmed from code: `army-to-army-transfer-confirmed.md`, the `OK` paragraph; the branch was not exercised in the observed saves]**.
  - **`AdjacentPartner`**: `Army(state, armyId)` returns the **last** own army at Chebyshev distance exactly 1 (`FUN_00449D64`, through `FUN_004492A0`'s `distance == 1`). `Fleet(state, fleetId)` returns the last own **launched** fleet at distance exactly 1 (`FUN_00449DD8`) **[derived: code, audit §1.6]**. Either returns none when there is no such unit. The UI uses these for Transfer unit, Join armies, Join fleets and Transfer ships, so the player never picks the partner (the user's decision that the game picks the partner). The CLI keeps naming both ids.
  - Disbanding a single unit from either list is T107's `disband-unit`, not this command.
