# T243 — The faithful enemy-army pick breaks a tie by table order

Triage of #1216 item 1, owner decision 2026-10-30: under `classical-faithful`, take the original's tie-break. Evidence: [`2026-10-30-army-table-order-after-deaths117.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/325c94c/docs/reports/2026-10-30-army-table-order-after-deaths117.md), addenda 1–3 (research `325c94c`).

**The original:** `FUN_0044ee60` (:52715–52776) scans every army of a nation at war in table order and replaces its pick only on a strictly greater score, so the lowest table index wins a tie. In play an inert hook counted 6 ties in 501 picks over the 43 death turns and 5 in 1,127 picks over three 30-turn starts, most of them won by the farther army; swapping two records in emulation changed Carthage's target.

**The remake** (`AiArmyTargetTree.ScoreArmyTarget`) breaks a tie by the lower army id (`string.CompareOrdinal`, T156's approved adaptation). Since T240, `state.Armies` is the original's live table order under `classical-faithful`. The id rule differs from table order once a sweep has moved a record, an id was reused, or ordinally from ten armies on (`army-10` < `army-9`).

## Contract

- Under the original AI (`flags.aiEconomy = original`) the enemy-army pick keeps the first equal target in `state.Armies` order: it replaces the pick only on a strictly greater score.
- The designed AI keeps T156's id tie-break.
- Nothing else changes: not the score, not the city pick's or the resupply pick's tie-breaks, and not #1216's item 2 (word-sized score storage), which stays open on #1216.

## Owns and done when

`AiArmyTargetTree.ScoreArmyTarget` and focused tests that prove:
- with two equal-score enemies listed `army-9` then `army-10`, the original AI picks `army-9` and the designed AI `army-10`;
- when table order and id order agree, both pick the same army;
- after a T240 sweep moves the last army into an earlier dead slot, the original AI's pick follows the new order and the designed AI's does not.

Regenerate only the goldens the change moves, and say which. Builds and hosted CI must be green. Refs #1216 (item 1); #1216 stays open for item 2.
