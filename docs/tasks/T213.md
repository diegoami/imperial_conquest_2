### Scope and evidence boundary

Original `FUN_0044E670` uses `floor(troops/1000)` for the city-choice threshold, admits an owned city when its stock is **strictly greater** than that threshold, and minimizes adjusted distance with **20 subtracted for a capital**. The current remake uses `/100`, admits own stock below the threshold and subtracts the capital amount from a maximized negative-distance score, thereby disfavoring capitals. These are three distinct defects at the raw selector. Automatic supply capacity still uses `/100`; do not change its separate formula. The report's six Pella/Thessalonica stock and capital variants pin the `29/30` and `297/298` boundaries, swapped capital preference and positive agreements.

Keep the original's nearest-owned-city fallback and foreign-city gates separate. The report's foreign `+20` is a distance cost, but the six controls did not independently isolate foreign preference; do not silently change that sign in this task. The production `ResolveResupplyCity` reachable-city layer is an intentional documented policy and must not be removed as a side effect. Do not hardcode pilot coordinates or select a city based on a final End-turn outcome. The new player-directed Retreat and supply mode (#1403) is a different QoL policy; this task corrects the faithful AI's existing raw selector only.
