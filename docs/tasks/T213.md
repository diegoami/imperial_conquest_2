### Scope and evidence boundary

Original `FUN_0044E670` uses `floor(troops/1000)` for the city-choice threshold, admits an owned city when its stock is **strictly greater** than that threshold, and minimizes adjusted distance with **20 subtracted for a capital and 20 added for a foreign city**. A foreign winner whose adjusted distance exceeds 15 falls back to the nearest owned city. The current remake uses `/100`, admits own stock below the threshold, and reverses the capital and foreign adjustments in its maximized negative-distance score. The raw original selector may choose an adjacent stocked city; the designed reachable-destination layer separately treats adjacency as arrival. Automatic supply capacity still uses `/100`; do not change its separate formula. The report's six Pella/Thessalonica stock and capital variants pin the `29/30` and `297/298` boundaries, swapped capital preference and positive agreements. The retained 112 original-instruction outcomes provide additional same-state foreign/adjacent/fallback controls, not new original game runs.

Keep the original's nearest-owned-city fallback and foreign-city gates separate. The foreign sign and adjusted-distance gate are source-backed and checked against the retained 112 instruction outcomes; the six new stock/capital controls did not isolate them alone. The production `ResolveResupplyCity` reachable-city layer is an intentional documented policy and must not be removed as a side effect. Do not hardcode pilot coordinates or select a city based on a final End-turn outcome. The new player-directed Retreat and supply mode (#1403) is a different QoL policy; this task corrects the faithful AI's existing raw selector only.
