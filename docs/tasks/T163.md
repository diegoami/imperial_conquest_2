#### T163 Small, medium and big towns draw different markers: the original's four population tiers

- **Design milestone**: M18 (the Godot screens). Planned from bug [#818](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/818): the user asked on 2026-10-11 for small, medium and big towns to look different ("they look the same"). Every shipped ruleset ships `mapMarkers.cityPopulationTierThresholds` empty, so every town but a capital draws the tier-1 marker. **Labels**: `lane:ui release:v0.5.3`
- **Branch**: `task/T163-town-tiers`, from `origin/main` · **Implementer and reviewer**: the user's choice ([CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) rule 17; the standing pair of 2026-10-11)
- **Start after**: none · **Merge after**: none.
- **Owns**:
  - `src/IC2.Engine/Assets/AssetKeys.cs`: a fourth town key (`city.tier4.icon`) beside `CityTier1Icon`–`CityTier3Icon`, in every list that enumerates the city keys.
  - `godot/Assets/MapMarkerKeys.cs`: the city path draws four tiers (its `TieredIcon` cap at the third key, and the remarks that call the city cell "purely its identity" and the tiers "not confirmed", corrected with the evidence).
  - The three rulesets' `mapMarkers.cityPopulationTierThresholds` and its provenance; `classical-faithful.json` only through `scripts/export-classical-world.cs` (its override for this key only; a re-run reproduces every other byte).
  - Both asset packs: `assets/packs/placeholder/` (`city/tier4.bmp`, its `manifest.json` entry, and `scripts/generate-placeholder-assets.ps1`) and `assets/packs/authored/` (`city/tier4.bmp` and its `manifest.json` entry, made the way the pack's existing city markers were made; the implementer finds and states how).
  - Tests: `tests/IC2.Engine.Tests/Ui/MapMarkerKeysTests.cs`, `tests/IC2.Engine.Tests/Assets/AssetKeysTests.cs` (its key counts) and `tests/IC2.Engine.Tests/Assets/PlaceholderPackIntegrationTests.cs` (`CityTierIcons_ArePixelDifferent` gains tier 4), and any other asset-pack or manifest test that counts or enumerates the city keys, for those values only.
- **Scope**:
  - **Protects**: every non-capital town draws the marker of its current population tier, and the capital its own marker, in both packs.
  - **The rule** ([`2026-10-07-city-marker-variants.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-07-city-marker-variants.md), **[confirmed: decompile + saves]**): `map code = 20 + owner + 16 × variant`; variants 0–3 are population tiers on the city's current population in thousands, **< 25, 25–49, 50–99, ≥ 100**; variant 4 is the national capital. So the thresholds are `[25, 50, 100]`.
  - **The user's decisions of 2026-10-11** (on #818):
    1. **Live tiers.** A town's marker always reflects its current population, recomputed when drawn, as the clone does today. The original's tier is set only when a town changes owner, so it goes stale as the town grows; not copying that is a recorded deliberate difference (`[designed]`, the user's decision).
    2. **All five variants:** four population tiers plus the capital, so a fourth town marker in both packs.
  - **The fourth marker** is visibly larger or richer than the third, in the pack's existing style (`[designed]`: the original's art is not shipped). Tiers 1–4 must be distinguishable at the map's normal zoom.
  - **Out of scope**: army and fleet markers (unchanged; the report re-confirms them); the capital marker's art.
- **Done when**:
  1. **The tiers.** For populations 24, 25, 49, 50, 99 and 100 (thousand), a non-capital town draws tier 1, 2, 2, 3, 3 and 4; a capital draws the capital marker at any population. Each boundary fails when moved by one.
  2. **Every ruleset** ships `[25, 50, 100]` with the report's provenance, and a re-run of the exporter reproduces `classical-faithful.json` byte for byte (the main session re-runs it if the implementer cannot).
  3. **Both packs** load with the fourth key; a missing `tier4.bmp` fails pack validation the way a missing tier-3 does today.
  4. **On the map.** A headless check or a screenshot of the game screen shows towns of different tiers drawn with different markers (the main session posts the screenshot for the user).
  5. **Nothing regresses.** Build, test and all Godot headless checks are green, and the diff lists only Owns paths.
