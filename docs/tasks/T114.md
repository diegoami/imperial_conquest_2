- **Scope**: `JoinArmiesCommandHandler`, `JoinFleetsCommandHandler` and `FleetToFleetTransferCommandHandler` reject unless the two units stand on the **same tile**. The original takes the partner at Chebyshev distance **exactly 1** (`FUN_00449D64` for armies, `FUN_00449DD8` for fleets, through `FUN_004492A0`'s `distance == 1`) **[`FUN_00449D64` itself: confirmed: code, [`2026-10-03-army-to-army-ok-supply-rebalancing.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-03-army-to-army-ok-supply-rebalancing.md) item 5, which reads it as Transfer unit's gate; its use by Join armies, and `FUN_00449DD8`: derived: code, audit §1.6]**. A run of the original saw the fleet half: Join fleets and Transfer ships worked between fleet 2 at (101,46) and fleet 5 at (101,47), and did nothing with no own fleet adjacent **[Wine candidate: [`2026-10-02-fleet-orders-live.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-02-fleet-orders-live.md), quoted in #555's comment of 2026-10-02]**.
  - **Each of the three handlers requires the two named units to stand at Chebyshev distance exactly 1**, measured with `LandingTile.ChebyshevDistance`, as T106's `ArmyTransferCommandHandler` already does (~:46–53). Distance 0 and distance 2 or more are both rejected, with a rejection named for adjacency (`NotAdjacent`, codes `armies.not-adjacent` and `naval.not-adjacent`, after the domain-scoped style of the existing codes [designed]). A diagonal neighbour is at distance 1.
  - **The CLI keeps naming both ids** (T106's Scope). The handler accepts **any** own unit at Chebyshev distance exactly 1 as the named partner, and does not require it to be the one `AdjacentPartner` would pick [designed: the user's decision of 2026-10-03]. The original never lets the player name the partner (the game takes the **last** own unit at distance 1); the UI (T111, T112) always submits `AdjacentPartner`'s pick, so the two agree there.
  - **Everything else stands**: the ownership, embarked-army, under-construction and carrying-army refusals, the 20-unit and 100,000-troop caps, the fewer-than-101-ships cap, the purse cap and its overflow to the treasury, and the survivor's moves set to 0 (the original zeroes them too **[Wine candidate: the same report, `T_JOIN_FLEETS.SAV`]**). The survivor keeps its own tile, as now; the absorbed unit is removed.
  - The remarks of the three command files stop saying "co-located" and cite the partner rule above.

- **Hazards**:
  - **A freshly split pair cannot rejoin until one of them moves.** The clone puts a split army on its parent's tile ([#584](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/584)) and a split fleet likewise ([#596](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/596)); both are post-v1.0.0. The original puts the new unit one tile away, (+1,+1) for an army and an adjacent tile for a fleet, so its partner is in reach at once **[Wine candidates: [`2026-10-02-unit-map-mouse-orders-and-tax-range.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-02-unit-map-mouse-orders-and-tax-range.md) observation (e); the fleet-orders report]**. After this task a split followed at once by a join is rejected, and a human seat's new army has 0 moves under `classical-faithful` (`SplitArmyCommandHandler` ~:103), so the parent has to step away first. That is the expected outcome of this task: #584 and #596 are **not** folded in [designed: the user's decision of 2026-10-03]. Don't touch the split handlers.
