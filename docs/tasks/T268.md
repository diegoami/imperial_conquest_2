# T268 — Persistent hostile barbarian faction lifecycle

Issue [#1702](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/1702),
foundation for #1683. Own optional scenario/native faction metadata, validation
and initialization, shared faction policy, diplomacy/transition guards,
capture/elimination, upkeep/deposition/victory exclusions, non-playable seat
eligibility and SAV refusal, with focused controls and docs.

One AI-only faction stays active without a capital or cities, cannot win, and
its held cities still count for total conquest. It remains at war with living
regular nations and never makes peace, trade or alliances. No ordinary strategic
AI recruitment/diplomacy or treasury upkeep. Losing its last town preserves
roaming forces. Normal city supply/capture behavior remains; the next slice
owns spawning and dedicated movement/landing/capture decisions.

Defaults must serialize/hash exactly as before. Optional role fields are omitted
when inactive; old native files remain valid. Original SAV export explicitly
refuses the role rather than dropping it. No shipped campaign is enabled by
this foundation. Italy activation will use a separately authored campaign/world
variant so existing Italy saves keep their original referenced data.

Validate cityless rotation, all diplomacy entry points and thaw/treaties, last
town loss, no treasury billing, victory eligibility, non-playable selection,
legacy JSON/hash identity and incompatible-SAV refusal. Full CI before merge.

UI reference: private original manual §1, “Human and computer leaders” and
`M01_04_new_game_rome_ticked.png`: regular nations remain tickable in seat order.
The owner's v0.9 barbarian decision is the explicit exception: the dedicated
AI-only faction is excluded from human-seat candidates; existing campaigns
retain the same candidates and layout. No original manual material is shipped.
