#### T201 A reproducible terrain authoring path, and maps of any proportion fit the screen

- **Release / priority**: 0.7 series (release-plan §2.4 item 3); P1. Issue [900](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/900), "Must change" item 4 and "Looks wrong, works".
- **Start after**: nothing. **Blocks**: T202.

### Problem

- `export-classical-world.cs` reads only the original's DAT, at 320×140. There's no way to author a new world's terrain grid.
- The area map's integer scale floors at 1, so a portrait map overflows its 320×180 budget (`AreaMapGeometry.cs`).
- The main map's cover-zoom never shows a portrait map whole (`GameMapView`).

### Scope

1. **A terrain importer, as an offline tool.** It turns an indexed PNG, with a documented palette mapped to the shipped terrain codes 0–11, into a world's run-length terrain and its `.terrain.b64` sidecar.
   - It validates dimensions, codes and river connectivity: river codes 6–11 must form connected strokes ending at sea or at the map edge.
   - Its output is deterministic, so rerunning reproduces the committed sidecar byte for byte, like the classical export's reproducibility test.
   - Cities, nations and starting forces stay hand-authored JSON. The tool only does terrain.
2. **`AreaMapGeometry`** supports fractional or down-scaling for maps larger than its budget in either axis, keeping the 320×140 classical fit pixel-identical.
3. **`GameMapView`** fits any aspect ratio, including portrait, without showing background outside the map. The classical behaviour is unchanged.
4. **`docs/scenario-authoring.md`** documents the terrain workflow end to end.

### Owns

`tools/**` (the new importer), `docs/scenario-authoring.md`, `godot/UI/AreaMapGeometry.cs`, `godot/UI/GameMapView.cs` (fit and zoom only), and their tests and checks (`AreaMapGeometryTests`, `AreaMapCheck`, `MapClipCheck`).

### Done when

1. The importer round-trips a test PNG to a sidecar that loads as a valid world, and rerunning is byte-identical. Bad codes, a disconnected river and wrong dimensions are each refused with a clear message.
2. Portrait (160×200) and wide (400×120) synthetic worlds fit the area map and the main map with no overflow and no visible background. The classical fit is unchanged, which `AreaMapCheck` and `MapClipCheck` assert.
3. `dotnet test` and all Godot check scenes pass, and CI is green.
