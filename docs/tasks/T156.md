  - `src/IC2.Engine/Ai/AiMilitaryPhase.cs`, `src/IC2.Engine/Ai/AiView.cs` and `src/IC2.Engine/Ai/AiSiegeGateTally.cs`: **only** routing each army's decision through the tree, and retiring the designed siege-ratio gate (`RequiredAttackRatioPermille`) and the designed march weights from army decisions. - **Scope**:
  - **Protects**: an AI army at war chooses its action by the original's target tree, in both presets (the user's decision of 2026-10-09). In particular, **it attacks an enemy city whenever the city score exceeds 100 and no better army target exists**, so no AI army parks beside a weaker enemy city it can reach (#907's Gaul at Pisae: strength 35,640 against defence 32,950 scores about 236). And **an army that did not move takes the garrison fallback**, so no army stays idle with moves left while a target exists.
  - **The rule** ([`2026-10-07-strategic-ai-turn.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-07-strategic-ai-turn.md) §3.3 and §5, **[confirmed: decompile]**, with the in-play corrections of [`2026-10-09-ai-intercept-and-fleet-hunt-in-play.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-09-ai-intercept-and-fleet-hunt-in-play.md), a Wine candidate):
    - *the resupply/defence city* (`FUN_0044e670`): an own city whose supply is below the army's strength (`troops/100`), or, only at war, a foreign non-war city with stock above strength + 80 and `money > strength/5`; own capitals score −20, foreign +20; with nothing, or the best foreign city beyond 15, the nearest own city;
    - *the best enemy city* (`FUN_0044ece4`): `strength×110 / cityDefense − distance`, halved in another region, doubled if `cityDefense < strength` and `distance < 7`, doubled for a capital if `cityDefense×⅔ < strength`; returned as `score + distance`;
    - *the best enemy field army* (`FUN_0044ee60`): the same ratio less distance, halved across regions, capped at 1000, +1000 when the enemy is weaker and within 7;
    - *the decision*: the report's §3.3 tree verbatim (the 100, 85 and 71 thresholds, the supply and morale conditions, the 8, 19 and `2×moves` distances, the mercenary run with its defend-or-chase branch), then **the garrison fallback** (`FUN_0044ebe8`) when `FUN_0044aab4` says the army did not move.
  - **`improved`** uses the same tree for these decisions and keeps its designed extras elsewhere (personalities, fleets, diplomacy, its economy).
  - **Out of scope**: the economy phase ([T157](T157.md)), the fleet phase, the turn's fixed phase order and the action cap (the designed loop stays the driver; only each army's choice changes), and the battle itself.
- **Done when**:
  1. **The scorers are the original's.** Unit tests pin each scorer on hand-built states: the city score's region halving, its two doublings, and `+ distance` on return; the army score's 1000 cap and its +1000; the resupply city's −20 and +20, and the beyond-15 fallback. Each test fails when its term is removed (show the mutation).
  2. **The decision tree is the original's.** A table-driven test walks every branch of §3.3: attack the army, attack the city, the mercenary run, defend versus chase, and move to the resupply city. Each case fails if its threshold moves by one (100→101, 85→86, 71→72).
  3. **#907's reproduction attacks.** `classical-mediterranean`, seat Rome: Gaul's `army-9` at (92,34) beside Pisae besieges or attacks Pisae within 3 turns of arriving, in both presets.
  4. **AI armies no longer park.** Over 16 rounds on 3 seeds and 2 seats (the measurement #907's diagnosis used), no AI army stands 8 or more consecutive turns beside a hostile city it can attack with a score above 100. And at least one AI city capture happens after turn 10 in a 60-turn seeded `classical-mediterranean` run under each preset (the gap analysis's AI-01 and AI-02 measured none). The PR states the counts before and after.
  5. **Nothing regresses.** The 50-seed soak reports 0 rejected and 0 probe mismatches within its budget. The goldens that change are regenerated through the CLI and listed with their reason. `dotnet build` and `dotnet test` are green, and the diff lists only Owns paths.
- **Hazards**:
  - **Every seeded game changes.** The AI attacks where it used to wait, so list every changed golden, soak line and seeded test.
  - **Movement is unchanged.** The original's destination call walks and attacks as it goes; the clone keeps its own move and attack commands. The tree chooses the target, and the existing commands carry it out. If a chosen target cannot be reached by the existing commands, stop and report it rather than adding a mover.
