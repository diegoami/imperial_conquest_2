# T245 — Faithful capital defence before ordinary army targeting

Correction for [#1620](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/1620), authorised by the owner on 2026-10-30. Evidence: [capital-defence report](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/04a9e45/docs/reports/2026-10-30-capital-defence-rule123.md), research `04a9e45`. Its original-code and emulation findings are distinct from its Wine observations; the measured 90 turns had no equal-distance tie or nine-entry cap drop. Native Windows confirmation is not claimed.

## Contract

- Apply only to the original AI policy. The designed AI keeps its current decisions. Use T240/T244's live table order; introduce no save fields or UI changes.
- Run capital defence once per AI seat before the ordinary per-army target loop. It spends moves before that loop, rather than replacing its scorer or injecting a repeatedly regenerated scored candidate.
- Retain the first nine qualifying foreign armies in table order: same original geographic region/coast side as the capital, Chebyshev distance at most 19 at war or 9 otherwise. Later qualifying armies do not displace the first nine.
- Consider own responders with moves in table order, dispatching at most the number of listed threats plus two. Preserve the original offensive-commitment skip: qualifying enemy-army or city target, its score/distance gate, and capital distance above three times moves. Reuse existing scorers and navigation, without modifying their unrelated rules.
- Choose the nearest eligible live listed threat, preserving the earlier list entry on a distance tie. A non-war threat is eligible only when the responder is at least 20 tiles from the capital. Intercept only when the chosen threat is on land and at war; otherwise move toward the capital. The covered-cell test is on the threat, not on the responder. An embarked responder sends the move to its carrier through the existing fleet command, retaining its cargo links.
- Execute through the existing command dispatcher and faithful navigation/continuation. Keep command rejection and substantive-change counters honest. Do not issue an illegal land-move command for a carried army or a sea destination. Any necessary adaptation of an original branch to this command model must be explicitly identified before implementation, not silently skipped.

## Research clarification

[Research PR128](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/pull/128), report commit `1582986`, corrects an intake error: `0x44F2A3` tests the chosen threat's covered cell. No second predicate tests the responder's aboard state; DBA8 moves its carrier instead. The rules specification was already correct. The owner explicitly confirmed this clarification on 2026-10-30.

Use the promoted report's strict score-above-100 and distance-below-moves skip, with capital distance above three times moves. The older strategic report's section 3 places defence before the per-army support/hire loop. No chosen eligible threat means no dispatch. Count dispatch attempts separately from accepted substantive commands: a no-op consumes the bounded dispatch but cannot conceal a stall. Existing undefined navigation contexts, unsupported cross-region ferry routes and the wider faithful fleet-routing gap #1409 retain their existing limits; forwarding a carrier command does not establish original fleet-path parity.
