Correction for [#1620](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/1620), authorised by the owner on 2026-10-30. Evidence: [capital-defence report](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/04a9e45/docs/reports/2026-10-30-capital-defence-rule123.md), research `04a9e45`. ## Contract

- Apply only to the original AI policy. The designed AI keeps its current decisions. Use T240/T244's live table order; introduce no save fields or UI changes.
- Run capital defence once per AI seat before the ordinary per-army target loop. It spends moves before that loop, rather than replacing its scorer or injecting a repeatedly regenerated scored candidate.
- Retain the first nine qualifying foreign armies in table order: same original geographic region/coast side as the capital, Chebyshev distance at most 19 at war or 9 otherwise. Later qualifying armies do not displace the first nine.
- Consider own responders with moves in table order, dispatching at most the number of listed threats plus two. Preserve the original offensive-commitment skip: qualifying enemy-army or city target, its score/distance gate, and capital distance above three times moves. Reuse existing scorers and navigation, without modifying their unrelated rules.
- Choose the nearest eligible live listed threat, preserving the earlier list entry on a distance tie. A non-war threat is eligible only when the responder is at least 20 tiles from the capital. Use the original intercept versus capital-fallback predicate once clarified below.
- Execute through the existing command dispatcher and faithful navigation/continuation. Keep command rejection and substantive-change counters honest. Do not issue an illegal land-move command for a carried army or a sea destination. Any necessary adaptation of an original branch to this command model must be explicitly identified before implementation, not silently skipped.
