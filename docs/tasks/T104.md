- **Scope**: the original's `TBalanceSheet` is a read-only dialog with only an OK button. It shows the active nation's budget for the quarter **[derived: code, `TBalanceSheet_PaintBalance`]**, and its terms are exactly the quarterly treasury credit and upkeep bill **[confirmed: `decompiled-quarterly-billing-and-economy.md`, `upkeep-payment-and-desertion.md`]**:
  - **Income**: tax income, trade income, total.
  - **Expenditure**: `cities × 7 + wealth / 20000`, ships × 3, recruitment-slot upkeep, regulars' upkeep, mercenaries' pay, total.
  - **The treasury**, and **`wealth / 500` capped at 20,000**, the deposition-for-debt threshold.

  `BalanceSheet.For(state, nationId, ruleset)` returns these as a record. Every term is computed by the **same function** the quarterly systems call, never a copy of its formula. The `balance` verb prints the sheet for the active nation; it is read-only and submits nothing.
  - **Captions [designed]**: the original's captions are in the form stream, which no report has read (audit §5, gap 2). The record names its fields plainly, and the debt-threshold line is called "Debt limit" until a read says otherwise.
- **Done when**:
  1. `BalanceSheetTests`, on a classical-mediterranean game advanced to the last week before a quarter: Rome's projected income total equals the treasury credit the quarterly system then applies, and its expenditure total equals the bill it then charges. Both are read from the state before and after the quarter tick, with nothing else moving the treasury in between. It fails on `main` (no type) and passes after.
  2. The same holds for a nation with fleets and mercenaries, so the ship and mercenary terms are non-zero.
  3. The debt-limit term equals `min(wealth / 500, 20000)` and equals the value `Deposition` tests against.
  4. A source scan of `BalanceSheet.cs` finds none of the literals `7`, `20000` and `500`: those terms come from the shared functions or the ruleset.
  5. `balance` prints every line of the sheet, and the state is unchanged after it, asserted by serialising the state before and after.
  6. `CommandCoverageTests` and the CI `OrderCoverageCheck` are green.
  7. `dotnet build IC2.sln` and `dotnet test IC2.sln` are green, and the diff lists only Owns paths.
- **Hazards**:
  - **The rounding of the last figure is [open]** (audit §1.3). Use `Deposition`'s own computation; if it rounds differently from the dialog, the dialog's rounding is a research question, not a guess.
  - Exposing a term from a quarterly system must not change its order of operations or RNG draws. The existing economy and corpus tests are the guard: none may change.
  - If a term (trade income, `FUN_004499ec`) has no single function today, extract one in place. Don't re-derive it.
