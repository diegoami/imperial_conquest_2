- **Scope**:
  - **Protects**: the rules of the game's end stay the engine's, unchanged: `Deposition.ShouldFallAtHumanTurnStart`, `Deposition.ApplyEffects`, the elimination and conquest paths, the watch-mode hand-over and the game-over refusal of further commands. The five texts `HumanLeaderFallsMessage` chooses are unchanged, character for character (they are the original's, two of them corpus literals). Every CLI line is unchanged, so every CLI golden is byte-identical. `VictoryCheckSystem`, `GameWon` and `GameExpired` (read by `AiGameRunner` only) are unchanged. Every other overlay keeps its order. Forbidden: an edit under `src/IC2.Engine/Economy/**`, `src/IC2.Engine/Victory/**` or `src/IC2.Engine/Cities/**`; a new `NationState` or `GameState` field, or any change to the save format (the start figures are already state); a screen that parses CLI text to find a fall.
  - **What the original shows** **[confirmed: code, [`decompiled-elimination-cleanup.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/decompiled-elimination-cleanup.md); [`decompiled-diplomacy-peace-terms-and-instant-battles.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/decompiled-diplomacy-peace-terms-and-instant-battles.md); [`news-log-format-and-messages.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/news-log-format-and-messages.md) Q4]**. One window, `THumanFalls` (`THumanFalls_InitializeForm`, `0x00455E38`), ends a human seat's game. It opens at a human seat's turn start when the year has reached 250 BC, the seat holds every city (334 on the original map), its unity is below 400, or it is in debt (`FUN_00452034`), and when the seat is conquered during another nation's turn (`FUN_0044BED8`, `FUN_0044C528`). Its one line of text is chosen from the state, in this order: *"You have conquerred the Mediterranean, a unique achievement."*; *"You have reached the end of your allotted 20 years."*; *"Your unpopularity has forced the army to overthrow you."* or *"Your army have deposed you because they have not been paid."*; *"Your nation has been conquerred by <nation>."*. **There is no separate victory screen**: holding every city is announced by this window. Afterwards the seat is handed to the computer; if no human seat is left, the game closes its forms and the game is over; if the fallen seat was the active one and humans remain, its turn ends **[derived: code, `TPremierForm_HumanLeaderFalls`, `decompiled-elimination-cleanup.md`]**. The clone's session already applies all of this and prints the same texts (T87, `HumanLeaderFallsMessage`); nothing under `godot/` shows them (#701).
  - **What the form holds** **[derived: form `THumanFalls`, the feature inventory [`2026-10-05-player-facing-feature-inventory.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-05-player-facing-feature-inventory.md), row EG01, a provisional report]**: the window is titled *"End of Game"*, and besides the reason it has labels for the leader's years in power (`lbl_changes`) and a start-against-end table of the nation's population, cities and money (`lbl_nat1/2`, `lbl_pop1/2`, `lbl_cities1/2`, `lbl_money1/2`), with one OK button. No screenshot shows it filled in, and no run reached it (the inventory's note: *"none was reached in play"*), so the form's fields are **[derived: form]**, not seen. **The clone copies it** (the user's decision of 2026-10-05 on PR #729, *"Copy the original"*): the title, the years in power and the table, below.
  - **The figures the table needs are already state.** `NationState.PopulationAtStart`, `CityCountAtStart` and `TreasuryAtStart` are set by `GameStateFactory` at a new game (and by `OriginalSaveImporter` at an import), and `GameState` already round-trips them through the save, so this task adds **no field and no save-format change**. The end figures are the nation's `Population`, its city count (`GameState`'s count of cities whose `Owner` is the nation) and its `Treasury`, taken when it falls and carried on `SeatFall`, so a later fall in the same call cannot shift them. **The years in power** are `calendar.startYearBc` minus the current `CalendarState.YearBc` **[derived: code, `THumanFalls_InitializeForm` reports the reign length as 270 minus the current year, as `classical-faithful.json`'s `_provenance` for `calendar.startYearBc` quotes [`decompiled-diplomacy-peace-terms-and-instant-battles.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/decompiled-diplomacy-peace-terms-and-instant-battles.md)]**. The original counts from the fixed start year, not from a leader's accession, so no state is added for it; in `v0.5.0` every human leader holds power from the start anyway (New player, New nation and Abdicate are not in the gate, T100).
  - **What is not known**: the window's layout, picture and the values the table shows, and what the screen shows once the forms close at the game's end. No report, screenshot or recording cited here shows them, so those parts are **[designed]** below. The research gap check may add them; a difference found later is a follow-up, not a reason to hold the task.
  - **The engine exposes the falls** (T25's pattern for battles). Each human seat that falls during a `Submit` call, including a fall in the construction-time prelude, which arrives with the first `Submit`'s output as the prelude's lines do, yields one `SeatFall`: the nation, the reason (`AllCities`, `HardEndYear`, `Unpopularity`, `Unpaid`, `Conquered`, with the conqueror for the last), and the text `HumanLeaderFallsMessage` already prints. A nation falls at most once per call, however many of the session's paths report it. `SessionOutput.SeatFalls` and `GameSession.LastSeatFalls` carry them in the order they happened; `SessionOutput.GameOver` and `GameSession.IsGameOver` are true once no human seat can give orders: the game is over (`_gameOver`), or a `--seat`-style session's seat is lost (`_seatLost`).
  - **The window** **[designed, each a default the user may change at the visual review, except where a tag says otherwise]**: (1) one window per fallen seat, in order, titled *"End of Game"* **[derived: form `THumanFalls`]**; its body is the engine's text, unchanged, then the line *"<Leader> ruled <nation> for <n> years."* **[designed wording]**, then the table **[derived: form, its labels; layout designed]**: a header row naming the nation, and three rows, Population, Cities and Money, each with a Start and an End column; (2) when the game is over, a second line reads *"The game is over."*, and the buttons are **Main menu** (it returns to the main menu, as File → Close does, with no "leave the game?" question since nothing is left to lose) and **View map** (it closes the window; the map stays visible, and the engine refuses every further order with its existing line); (3) when another human seat remains (hotseat), the one button is **Continue**, and play goes on as the session already continues it; (4) the window comes after the call's battle windows and before the hotseat hand-off screen, so the next player never sees another's fall after taking the device.
  - **`MainGameScreen`** reads `Session.LastSeatFalls` in `OnCommandIssued` next to `Session.LastBattles`, queues one window per fall, and lets `ShowNextOverlay` show them in the order above. `GameEndViewModel` holds everything the window says and offers, Godot-free, so the test project can pin it.
- **Done when**:
  1. `SeatFallOutputTests`, through `GameSession` on scripted states, one test per reason: a human seat that owns every city at its turn start yields `AllCities` with the conquest text; a state past `victory.hardEndYearBc` yields `HardEndYear`; unity below `economy.debtUnityThreshold` (400) yields `Unpopularity`, and a treasury below the debt limit with unity at or above it yields `Unpaid` (`Deposition.InDebt`); the human's last city captured in an AI seat's turn yields `Conquered` naming the captor. Each `SeatFall.Text` equals the line the session printed in the same call, each `SeatFall`'s end population, city count and treasury equal the nation's state at the fall (for `Conquered`, 0 cities), and `GameOver` is true when no human seat remains. It fails on `main` (no `SeatFalls`) and passes after.
  2. **Hotseat.** With two human seats, one falling yields exactly one `SeatFall` for it, `GameOver` false, and the next `Submit` is accepted for the other seat; when the second falls too, `GameOver` is true.
  3. **Once per call.** A seat that is both reported by `AppendFallMessagesForNewlyLostHumanSeats` and by `AnnounceAndAdoptWatchModeIfSeatIsLost` in one call yields one `SeatFall`.
  4. **Nothing else changes.** Every CLI golden is byte-identical (`git diff origin/main -- tests/fixtures/cli` is empty), and `SeatCliTests` passes unchanged.
  5. `GameEndViewModelTests`: for each reason, the title is *"End of Game"* and the body is the fall's text; the years line gives `calendar.startYearBc − YearBc` (a fall in 260 BC under a 270 BC start reads 10 years); the table's Start column equals the nation's `PopulationAtStart`, `CityCountAtStart` and `TreasuryAtStart`, and its End column the `SeatFall`'s figures, none of them a literal; the game-over case adds *"The game is over."* and offers Main menu and View map; the hotseat case offers Continue alone. No text is typed in the view model that the engine already provides.
  6. The headless `GameEndCheck` builds the real `MainGameScreen` on scripted states and presses End turn **from the menu**: (a) a human seat that holds every city at its next turn start sees a `GameEndScreen` titled *"End of Game"*, whose body is *"You have conquerred the Mediterranean, a unique achievement."*, whose laid-out table shows the nation's start and end population, cities and money read from the state, and whose Main menu raises the screen's request to return to the main menu; (b) a human seat whose last city falls in the AI phase sees the window naming the captor, after any battle window of that `end`; (c) with two human seats, the first one's fall shows its window with Continue, then the hand-off screen for the other seat, in that order.
  7. `ScreensCheck`, `AiPhaseBattleCheck`, `MenuBarCheck`, `MapClickCheck`, `SaveResumeCheck` and `ContextPanelWidthCheck` pass unchanged.
  8. `dotnet build IC2.sln --nologo -v q` and `dotnet test IC2.sln --nologo -v q` are green, the `godot-headless` CI job is green, and `git diff --name-only origin/main...HEAD` lists only Owns paths. **After the task's changes are committed**, the headless checks are run once more and `scripts/check-godot-churn.ps1` then leaves a clean tree.
- **Hazards**:
  - **The human visual review** posts screenshots of a windowed run: the victory window, a conquered window, the hard-end-year window, the hotseat Continue window and the game-over window with its two buttons, each with its years line and table. "Looks right" is the user's call (Q-B), and it settles the design calls above; a change the user asks for there is rework of this task.
  - **The window is copied from a form never seen filled in.** Its title, labels and table come from `THumanFalls`'s controls **[derived: form]**; their values' meaning (whether the money row is the treasury or a wider total, what the start column holds for an imported save) is not read. Keep the tags in the PR; a difference the desktop original or a research read shows later is a follow-up, not a reason to hold the task. An imported original save's start figures are the import's own, not the game's start (`OriginalSaveFieldMapping`'s remarks on the `AtStart` fields).
  - **The texts are the original's, misspellings included** ("conquerred"). Do not correct them.
  - **The hard end year is cited as the screen's comparison**; whether the original's turn loop also ends the game there was not re-checked (`classical-faithful.json`'s `_provenance` for `hardEndYearBc`). The engine already applies it at the turn start; this task shows it and does not change it.
  - **A defect in the engine's fall paths** (a fall that prints nothing, prints twice, or reaches the wrong seat) is a bug in merged code: suspend, file it through the bug list, and do not work around it in the window ([build-process.md §4.6](../build-process.md#46-bugs-and-follow-ups)).
  - **Never two Godot processes at once**: run the headless checks one at a time.
  - Wiring `GameEndCheck` into `.github/workflows/ci.yml` is a one-line follow-up after the merge (build-process §7), not this task's.
