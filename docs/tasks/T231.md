# T231 — User-friendly army transfer and split purses

Owner decision after independent review F1/#1542 (2026-10-27): prefer flexible, predictable transfers; no further original-game measurement is required. This is deliberate remake policy for both presets, superseding T222's original signed-arrow quirk.

## Contract

- Army Transfer and Split money arrows move only in the requested direction. Bound each step by working giver funds and receiver room up to PurseAccounting.PurseFieldMax (32,767), without a 1,000-purse limit, reverse pullback, negative purse or dropped money. Spinners and suggested split allocations follow the same bounds.
- OK accepts these allocations above 1,000, including simultaneous unit/supply changes. Preserve money/treasury, other eligibility gates and ordinary unchanged-OK supply rebalance. Explicit raw allocations outside the representable receiving purse range refuse atomically. Existing emptied-army merge/saturation policy is unchanged.
- Preview dispatches the same production command as OK. If rejected, show the reason and unchanged live balances rather than staged fictional post-OK balances; disable confirmation. Cancel remains inert.
- Preserve save schemas, native and original-SAV representation. Supply quantities, fleet money and treasury funding remain outside scope. Supply army money from an army provider already delegates to the same ArmyTransferModel and intentionally inherits this flexible army-to-army policy.

## Owns

ArmyDialogModels transfer/split money bounds and rejected preview; ArmyTransferDialog preview/OK feedback; ArmyTransferCommandHandler/SplitArmyCommandHandler allocation ceilings and stale comments; focused real command/model/dialog tests and old expectations; task/index/design policy cross-references and the four derived rulesets’ purse provenance metadata (no numeric/flag changes).

## Done when

1. Both directions, repeated presses, 900/1,200 and both-over-1,000 controls match committed balances. Field-limit controls conserve money and treasury. Include staged units/supply, Cancel, rejected preview and split allocation above 1,000.
2. Actual transfer/split UI checks and focused handler/parser/model tests pass. Rejection assertions use real session rejection wording.
3. Build/diff/churn checks and CI pass. Release notes explicitly distinguish this owner policy from original signed arrows. Prepare a new candidate for independent follow-up; do not move the reviewed freeze or tag yet.
