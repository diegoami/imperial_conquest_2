- **Scope**: `AiWeights.cs` holds the AI's scoring constants (31 `const`s: `AttackArmyBaseScore = 3000`, `RecruitBaseScore = 1000` and others). They are `[designed]`, since the original's AI is out of scope by standing decision, and the file itself says they belong in data. Tuning the AI, including the fix for #268 (fortification always outscores recruitment at `expansionDrive` 500), needs them in data first.
- **Done when**:
  1. **The weights are ruleset data.** Every constant in `AiWeights.cs` becomes a field of one new ruleset record, the `ai` block, with the **same value** in all three rulesets. Each field's `_provenance` says `[designed]`, what it weighs, and that the original's AI is out of scope. The loader rejects a ruleset missing the block (§2.6).
  2. **Nothing reads a C# constant.** `AiWeights.cs` is deleted, or reduced to nothing that holds a number. The AI reads every weight from the ruleset it is running under.
  3. **No behaviour moves.** The 50-seed soak's per-seed logs and the AI determinism tests are **byte-identical** before and after. Show the comparison.
  4. **A weight changes a decision.** A test sets one weight (for example the recruit base score) differently in a test ruleset and shows an AI turn choosing differently.
  5. **The dead peace weights go, not move (#404 N7).** Since T88, nothing reads `MakePeaceBaseScore` or `SuePeaceStrengthRatioPermille`. They are deleted, not carried into the `ai` block. The loop over human seats in `AiDiplomacyPhase.Propose`, which only calls the now-empty `ProposePeace`, is removed with its `ownPower`, and so is `ProposePeace` if nothing else calls it. Done-when 3's byte-identical comparison covers this change too.
  6. **The AI's capital estimate matches the resolver (#425).** `AiMilitaryPhase.IsControllerCapital` uses `CapitalOwnership.IsAnyNationsCapital`, as `InstantBattleResolver` does since T91. A test uses a city that another nation's stale capital names, where the owner-only check fails. The remarks at ~:29 and ~:34 are made true.
     - This change **may move behaviour**, and Done-when 3's byte-identical comparison covers only the weights move. So make this change in its own commit, **after** the weights commit. Show the comparison from before it to after it, and list every soak log line that moves, with its reason.
  7. `dotnet build IC2.sln` and `dotnet test IC2.sln` are green, the CLI golden transcript is unchanged except for what Done-when 6 moves, and the diff lists only Owns paths.
- **Hazards**:
  - **Byte-identical is the whole point.** Integer and float arithmetic must stay exactly as it is. A `const int` becoming a `double` field changes rounding.
  - **Preset-neutral provenance.** T68's guard rejects the word "toy" in a shipped preset's provenance.
  - **Not tuning.** Changing any weight's value is out of scope; that belongs to the #268/#259/#267 work.
