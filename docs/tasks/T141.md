#### T141 Split army and Split fleet carry units or ships, supply and money in one command

- **Design milestone**: M18 (the Godot UI), as the engine half of two of its orders. A correction task from GPT-6 Sol's review of PR #734 ([T111](T111.md), [#569](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/569)), finding R1 ([the review](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/pull/734#issuecomment-5996181168)): T111's Split army dialog allocates units, supply and money by hand, as the original's does, but the session's `split-army` takes one unit index and nothing else, so the dialog cannot commit what it shows. [T112](T112.md)'s Split fleet has the same gap: its dialog shows ships, supply and money, and `split-fleet` takes ships only. `SplitArmyCommand` already carries several unit indices, money and supply; only the parser drops them. - **Scope**:
  - **Protects**: the split's other rules stay as they are: the two-unit and 20-ship minimums, the 198-army cap, the embarked-army and carrying-fleet refusals, the new unit's moves and morale, troop, unit and ship conservation, and **the placement** (T114's `SplitPlacement`, read, never edited; `SplitPlacementTests` pass unchanged). The existing forms `split-army <army> <new-army> <unit-index>` and `split-fleet <fleet> <new-fleet> <ships>` parse as before and, for `split-fleet`, behave exactly as before. Forbidden: an edit under `src/IC2.Engine/Ai/**` (no AI seat splits), to `ArmyTransferCommandHandler.cs` or to `SplitPlacement.cs`; a new verb (the verbs' keys in `godot/UI/CommandVerbCatalog.cs` and `CommandCoverageTests` stay); a ruleset key; a change to the save format.
  - **The original's split is one dialog `OK` that moves units, supply and money** **[confirmed: code, [`2026-10-03-army-to-army-ok-supply-rebalancing.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-03-army-to-army-ok-supply-rebalancing.md) (research `a380a8e`), "Split army uses the same form": `TUnitMap_SplitArmy` creates an empty partner with `FUN_00449F08` and opens `TArmyToArmy`, "All of the above applies to a split"]**. A real split moved 7 of 16 units and 100 of 256 talents in one order **[confirmed: save pair `1_rome_270_winter_9_b.sav` → `_winter_11.sav`, [`pending-offer-block-army-split-and-naupactus.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/pending-offer-block-army-split-and-naupactus.md) (research `d5c7482`): 75,536 troops and 16 units became 37,081 + 38,455 and 9 + 7; money 256 became 156 + 100]**.
  - **`split-army` takes several units, supply and money**: `split-army <army> <new-army> <i,j,…> [supply=<tons>] [money=<talents>]`. The third token is a comma-separated list of non-negative unit indexes, so a single index still parses; `supply=` and `money=` are whole numbers, each at most once, and default to 0. They fill `SplitArmyCommand`'s existing `UnitIndicesToNewArmy`, `SupplyTonsToNewArmy` and `MoneyToNewArmy`. The parser checks only this syntax (as `HandleArmyTransfer` does since T117) and prints the usage line otherwise; everything the rules disallow stays the handler's.
  - **The money bound**: the new army receives at most `economy.purseCapPerUnit` (1,000) talents, refused with `PurseCapExceeded` above it. It is the dialog's money stepper, `min(step, 1000 − money(receiver), money(giver))` **[confirmed: code, the same report, item 6, `:44044–44088`]**; the new army opens with 0, so its bound is the cap. It is the bound T117 kept for the transfer.
  - **The rebalance**, after the units, supply and money are moved, with A the parent (the selected army) and B the new army, as `TArmyToArmy_OK` runs it **[confirmed: code, the same report, items 1–2, `:44572–44649`]**:
    1. `capA = troops(A) div 100` (`SupplyCapacity.ArmyCapacityTons`). If `supply(A) > capA`, A's excess goes to B and A is left at `capA`.
    2. `capB = troops(B) div 100`. If `supply(B) > capB` (including what step 1 pushed), B's excess goes back to A and B is left at `capB`.

    So supply is conserved exactly, and when both end over capacity B ends at `capB` and the parent keeps the rest. It runs on **every** accepted split, one with `supply=0` included: splitting troops off lowers the parent's capacity. Money is never rebalanced. The original's steps for an emptied army (the merge) are not reached: the engine keeps its rule that each side keeps at least one unit **[designed: what the original does when the player moves every unit is the merge of T117's step 3, a move rather than a split; the engine's refusal stays]**. Both presets: `SplitArmyCommandHandler` reads no flag for it, as T117 did not.
  - **`split-fleet` takes supply and money**: `split-fleet <fleet> <new-fleet> <ships> [supply=<tons>] [money=<talents>]`, with the same option syntax. The original's Split fleet dialog is a two-column table of the first and the second fleet's ships, supply and money, with 1s and 10s arrows for ships and 10s and 100s for supply and money **[Wine candidate: [`2026-10-02-fleet-orders-live.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-02-fleet-orders-live.md) (research `38b4c01`), `T_SPLIT_FLEET.SAV`; `TFleetToFleet` is opened by Split fleet, the feature inventory row D04]**. Each amount is between 0 and what the parent holds (`InvalidSupplyAllocation`, `InvalidMoneyAllocation`), and moves exactly. No capacity rebalance and no purse bound **[designed: `TFleetToFleet_OK` is unread, and `fleet-transfer` applies neither; the same direction]**.

- **Hazards**:
  - **An embarked army stays unsplittable.** `SplitArmyCommandHandler` refuses it (`armies.army-embarked`, **[designed, no confirmed evidence either way]**), and whether the original allows it is open: the code read lists no such refusal for `TUnitMap_SplitArmy` (it does for Join armies), but nobody has traced where its new army would stand ([`decompiled-unit-map-orders-and-record-fields.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/decompiled-unit-map-orders-and-record-fields.md), the order table; the transfer report's last open item, on an aboard army as A). The question is the user's (the plan PR that created this task). Do not change the refusal here.
  - **The dialog's limits are T111's and T112's**: the supply room `troops div 100 − supply + 1` and the 10 and 100 steps bound the spinners, not this command. The command accepts any amount up to what the parent holds (and, for an army's money, the purse bound) and rebalances.
  - **The split dialog's Disband buttons** (the original's form has them under each list) are not this command's: a split commits units, supply and money only, and T111 decides what its dialog shows.
  - **`success.golden.txt` may shift**: its first `split-army` lines move supply 0, and the rebalance can now push some of the parent's supply to the new army. Regenerate it through the CLI and explain the first changed line, as T114 did.
