- Clicking a valid distant land/city destination moves immediately using existing legal movement and stores any remainder. Continue on later confirmed human End turns; never auto-attack, declare war or embark. Cities arrive at a legal adjacent tile. With no moves left, store/replace the destination without issuing a move until the next confirmed End turn.

- Nearby at-war armies and fleets interrupt according to the approved policy; refuse/blocked/no-progress/ownership/embark events retain visible handling. Avoid extra commands or RNG on no-target/disabled paths.
- Real session/planner and Godot interaction checks cover immediate/partial/next-turn travel, replacement/cancellation, markers, city arrival, danger, hotseat, save/reload and untouched manual combat. Coordinate with strategic Undo. Build/test/CI and churn checks pass.
- Manual chapter 2 defines original selection/move clicks; persistent orders and destination markers are an explicit owner-designed extension.
