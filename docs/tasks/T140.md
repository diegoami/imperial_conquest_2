  - **The city panel** (row UM02; the report's rows C01–C10) **[derived: code, `TInformation_ShowCityDetails`; Wine candidate: the research read and `FI_b1_24_city_left_click.png`]**, in this order:
    - *City*: the name, then *"  (capital of <nation>)"* when some nation's `CapitalCityId` is this city; the nation named is the city's **controller** (`CityState.Owner`), not the nation whose capital it is (C01).
    - *Controlled by* (`CityState.Owner`) and *Allegiance to* (`CityState.Allegiance`), as nation names (C02, C03).
    - *Population*: `PopulationThousands × 1,000`, then *"("* `trunc(PopulationThousands × 100 / MaxPopulationThousands)` *"%)"* (C04).
    - *Loyalty*: the loyalty word (below) (C05).
    - *Fortification* (C06): `FortificationCode.FinishedPercent` and *"%"*; when `FortificationCode.IsOrderInProgress` (a raw code of 101 or more, printed as `raw mod 100`), *"  (under construction)"* follows. The original prints 100 as *100%* and 101 as *1%  (under construction)*.
    - **The bracket after it** (C07): *"   ("* troops *")"*, where troops is the sum of `Troops` over **every** `RecruitmentSlot` of the city's **controller** whose `TargetCityId` is this city, **whatever its `StateCode`** (a trained state-24 slot counts as well as a state-0 one). It is shown only when the sum is above 0, on a foreign city as on an own one, and another nation's slots never count. The help's *"conscripts currently being trained"* is too narrow: the research read shows *61% (3,000)* for a state-0 1,000 and a state-24 2,000 at one city.
    - *Tribute* (C08, C09): at a city the active seat controls (`CityState.Owner` is the active seat), `CityTaxContribution.Compute(city)` (`tribute × population / maxPopulation`, truncating; the original stores it in 16 bits, which no reachable value overflows) and *"  talents"*; at any other city, the **tribute word** of the raw `CityState.Tribute` (below).
    - *Supply*: `SupplyTons` and *"  tons"* at a city the active seat controls only; at any other city the line is blank (C10).
    Rome in the screenshot reads 181,000 (100%), excellent, 78% (14,000), 317 talents, 990 tons.
  - **The own army's panel** (row UM03; the report's rows A01–A11) **[derived: code, `TInformation_ShowArmyDetails`; Wine candidate: the research read, and `C_1_left_click.png` of `F:2026-10-02-unit-map-mouse-orders-and-tax-range.md` (c)]**, in this order:
    - *Army of <nation>*; *Moves*; *Supply*: tons, then *"  ("* `SupplyCapacity.PercentFull(SupplyTons, TotalTroops)` (`supply × 10000 / troops`, truncating) *"%)"*; *Morale*: the morale word (below); *Money* and *" talents"* (A01–A05).
    - *Terrain*: the name of the terrain under the army (`ArmyState.CoveredTileCode`). An army with no covered tile (aboard a fleet) has **no** Terrain line, and every later line moves up one (A06).
    - After a blank line, **all five unit types**, each with the sum of the troops of the army's units of that type (0 when it has none), then *Total troops* (`ArmyState.TotalTroops`) (A07, A08).
    - *No. of units*: **the index of the last unit with troops above 0, plus 1**, which is not a count when a unit in the middle has none (A09).
    - After a blank line, *Regulars cost* and *Mercenary pay*, each followed by *"  talents per quarter"* (A10, A11). Per unit, `s = trunc(troops / 200) × price[type]` (the unit type's quarterly price), which the original narrows to a signed 16-bit value; *Regulars cost* is the sum of `s` over the regular units, and *Mercenary pay* the sum of `trunc(s × quality / 5)` over the mercenary units; both sums are 32-bit. That is `ArmyUpkeep.ComputeUnit` per unit, summed by kind. The 16-bit narrowing never changes a result with the shipped prices (1 to 4 in every preset) and troops up to 32,767 (`163 × 4 = 652`), so the clone does not reproduce it; the research read documents it for a ruleset with other prices.

Unity, loyalty and morale share one table of words, indexed differently:
    - **Unity** `trunc(unity / 100)`: 0–499 *very low*, 500–599 *low*, 600–699 *normal*, 700–799 *high*, 800–899 *very high*, 900–999 *excellent*, 1000 and above nothing (all six edges seen on both sides; the game keeps unity within 300–990).
    - **Loyalty** `trunc(loyalty / 10)`: 0–49 *very low*, 50–59 *low*, 60–69 *normal*, 70–79 *high*, 80–89 *very high*, 90–99 *excellent*, 100 and above nothing (every edge from 49/50 to 99/100 seen on both sides; saves hold 38–96).
    - **Morale** `(m − 51) sar 2` (`m − 48` below 51) from the table's entry 4: 40–54 *very low*, 55–58 *low*, 59–62 *normal*, 63–66 *high*, 67–70 *very high*, **71–74 *excellent***, 75 and above nothing (every edge from 54/55 to 74/75 seen on both sides, and 50 seen as *very low*; 40–47 are [derived]). There are **six** tiers, not the five that 51…70 would give, and saves reach 73 ([design-audit.md §2.9a](../design-audit.md)).
    - **Tribute** of a city the viewer does not control, from the raw `CityState.Tribute`: 0–10 *poor*, 11–30 *moderate*, 31–100 *rich*, 101–10,000 *very rich*; above 10,000 the original sets no word and prints the number (every edge seen on both sides).
    - **Relations**: 1 *trade*, 2 *ally*, 3 *war*; 0 and below print nothing (every edge from 0/1 to 3/4 seen).

The default **[designed]** is: a value above the last word's band prints nothing (the original's own result at the seen edges), a value below the first band prints the first word, and no stray-memory output is reproduced.
