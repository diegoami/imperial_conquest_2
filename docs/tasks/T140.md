No ruleset key, `tests/fixtures/corpus.json` value, seeded measurement or CLI golden line changes, and nothing under `src/**` is edited. The foreign army panel keeps T99's withholding (row UM04); the city panel keeps its siege line, its tax-rate line (#608's), its garrison line and its "In training here" list; the unit list (T99's right-click view, T111's quality, T113's mercenaries) and every order section are untouched. Forbidden: an edit under `src/**`; a word or band typed without a citation of the research read; a screen that parses CLI text.
  - **The city panel** (row UM02) **[Wine candidate: `FI_b1_24_city_left_click.png`; the field list derived from code, `TInformation_ShowCityDetails`]**: *City* with *"(capital of <nation>)"* when it is one; *Controlled by* (`CityState.Owner`); *Allegiance to* (`CityState.Allegiance`); *Population* in people (thousands × 1,000) with the percent of the maximum in brackets; *Loyalty* as a word; *Fortification* as a percent (`FortificationCode.FinishedPercent`), with the bracketed troop count the research read names; *Tribute* in talents (`CityState.Tribute`); *Supply* in tons. Rome in the screenshot reads 181,000 (100%), excellent, 78% (14,000), 317 talents, 990 tons.
