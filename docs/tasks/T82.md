#### T82 AI diplomacy as the original has it: offers to a human are notices, and the AI never turns on an ally

- **Design milestone**: none. It is a correction for bug [#357](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/357), from #334's observation. **Labels**: `phase:2 lane:engine`
- **Branch**: `task/T82-ai-diplomacy-fidelity` · **Model/effort**: Sonnet / High · **Reviewer**: **Opus / Medium**
- **Start after**: T69 · **Merge after**: T69 (T69 owns `Diplomacy/**`). It is **never in flight with T66, T76 or T79**, which edit other parts of `src/IC2.Engine/Ai/**`.
- **Owns**:
  - `src/IC2.Engine/Diplomacy/**` and `tests/IC2.Engine.Tests/Diplomacy/**`: the proposal, acceptance and pending-offer paths
  - `src/IC2.Engine/Ai/AiDiplomacyPhase.cs`; `src/IC2.Engine/Ai/AiMilitaryPhase.cs`, **only** its war-declaration targeting and the attack filter; and their tests under `tests/IC2.Engine.Tests/Ai/**`
  - `src/IC2.Engine/Model/Ruleset.cs`, `GameDataSchema.cs` and `data/rulesets/*.json` (`classical-faithful.json` through the exporter): **only** new fields the offer conditions need, with `_provenance`
  - `tests/fixtures/corpus.json` and `known-reports.json`: the new report's ids and name only
- **Scope**: [`decompiled-ai-offers-to-human-seats.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/decompiled-ai-offers-to-human-seats.md) (research `0d2674c` and `35ace81`) establishes the original's flow, and #357 lists where the engine differs. In short:
  - **AI to AI:** set directly.
  - **AI to human:** an offer rolled at the human's turn start (`FUN_00452034`, under its conditions) is shown as an OK-only notice. The relation is never written, and the offer clears at the next human turn start.
  - **Accepting:** the human accepts through the ordinary proposal rules, and for an alliance with an AI only the human's side is checked.
  - **War:** the AI never declares war on an ally that holds a city, and its units attack only nations already at war.
- **Done when**:
  1. **An AI offer to a human is a notice.** An AI never writes a relation with a human seat directly. At the human's turn start, the original's roll (`FUN_00452034`: the proposer, the type, and the trade and alliance conditions, including the neighbour relation, from the report) may store a pending offer. The CLI shows it, and nothing else changes. It clears at the next human turn start. Tests: an offer rolled; the relation unchanged; the offer cleared a turn later.
  2. **Accepting follows the original.** A human's trade or alliance with an AI goes through the report's human-to-AI rules: the trade's three-partner exception for a pending offer, and **only the human's side** checked for an alliance. That changes `AcceptPendingOfferCommand`'s two-sided check. Tests pin each condition, and each fails if the condition is deleted.
  3. **AI to AI stays direct**, and every such call still requires a computer partner.
  4. **No war on an ally.** The AI's war targeting excludes any ally that holds a city. Its units attack only nations it is already at war with, so an attack is never an implicit declaration. A test gives an AI an allied, city-holding neighbour next to an attackable army and shows no declaration and no attack. The T65 #256 fixture's "declares war on its new ally" sequence no longer happens; update that expectation only if it is in this task's Owns, and otherwise report it.
  5. **Seeded outcomes.** Every seeded AI test, soak log or golden that changes is listed in the PR with its reason. The 50-seed soak keeps 0 rejected commands and its stall invariants.
  6. `dotnet build IC2.sln` and `dotnet test IC2.sln` are green, and the diff lists only Owns paths.
- **Hazards**:
  - **The neighbour mask.** The original reads nation field `+0x46` as a symmetric neighbour mask. The engine can derive "borders" from the world, but say how, and show it agrees with the report on the three saves it checked.
  - **Don't break the human-to-human hotseat case.** "Always accepted" applies there, where the target is human.
  - **The soak may change a lot.** Fewer wars on allies and no auto-alliances with humans move AI outcomes. The toy soak has no human seat, so AI-to-AI should dominate there; confirm it.
