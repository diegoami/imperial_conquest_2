#### T139 A post-battle offer of peace is answered in the Godot app

- **Design milestone**: M18 (the Godot UI), with M8's human-consent peace (T88) made reachable from the screen. A new task for bug [#717](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/717), moved into the `v0.5.0` gate by the triage of 2026-10-05 under [build-process.md §4.8](../build-process.md#48-the-playability-gate-until-v050) tests 1 and 2: the original's post-battle offer cannot be answered in the Godot app, and Save is refused while it is pending. It is a task, not the fix lane ([§4.10](../build-process.md#410-the-fix-lane)): no rule's outcome changes, but the answer needs a new window, a new engine query and `MainGameScreen`'s overlay order, which are not the files the bug names, and its window carries design calls for the human visual review. **Labels**: `phase:3 lane:ui single-instance release:v0.5.0` (it launches Godot for its check, [build-process.md §7](../build-process.md#7-concurrency-single-instance-and-local-only))
- **Branch**: `task/T139-peace-offer-window`, from `origin/main` · **Model/effort**: Sonnet / Medium ([§3.3](../build-process.md#33-model-selection): not architecture, so the OpenCode default, then Sonnet) · **Reviewer**: the complex tier ([§3.4](../build-process.md#34-why-the-reviewers-model-differs-from-the-implementers)): GPT-6 Sol at `low` **+ human visual review** ([§9](../build-process.md#9-standing-governance-decisions) Q-B)
- **Start after**: none · **Merge after**: T116 and T138. T116 owns `MainGameScreen`'s battle-overlay queue and the order of `ShowNextOverlay` and `CheckForHotseatHandoff`, and holds a battle for a human seat until that seat's turn; T138 adds the game's-end window to the same order. This task adds one window after both. It is **never in flight with** another task or fix under `godot/**`, except an engine task's one-line entry in `godot/UI/CommandVerbCatalog.cs`, which this task does not touch; nor with T108, T138, T137 or T134's port to `main` (each edits `src/IC2.Engine/Presentation/GameSession.cs`).

  - `godot/Checks/PeaceOfferCheck.cs`, `godot/Checks/PeaceOfferCheck.tscn` (new);

- **Scope**:
  - **Protects**: the peace rules stay the engine's, unchanged: the gate in `InstantBattleResolver` that raises `PeaceTreatyOffered`, `CapturePeaceTreatyOfferIfAny`'s one slot per human seat, `HandlePeaceTreatyAnswer` (`peace-yes` writes the honourable peace, `peace-no` writes nothing), the lapse at that seat's next `end`, and `HandleSave`'s refusal while any offer is pending (the user's decision of 2026-09-28, PR #481). Every CLI line is unchanged, so every CLI golden is byte-identical, and `PeaceTreatyOfferTests` and `SaveAndResumeTests` pass unchanged. `BattleResultScreen` keeps its line *"A peace treaty has been offered."*. Every other overlay keeps its order. Forbidden: an edit to `src/IC2.Engine/Presentation/GameSession.Commands.cs`, `src/IC2.Engine/Battle/**` or `src/IC2.Engine/Diplomacy/**`; a change to the save format; a screen that parses CLI text to find an offer.
  - **What the original shows** **[confirmed: the feature inventory [`2026-10-05-player-facing-feature-inventory.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-05-player-facing-feature-inventory.md), row D09, which cites `coverage.md` §1 "Accept a post-battle peace (TBattlePols)", driven with `lab2_a_after_no.SAV`; a provisional report, seen under Wine]**: after the battle's result, a window titled *"Offer of peace"* with **Yes** and **No**. The opponent offers to end the war on the printed terms, which in practice are the honourable peace; No continues the war. The window's gate is the engine's already (T88). Its sentence is the engine's `PeaceTreatyOfferDialogText`, whose completion after "willing to end" is **[designed]** (its own remarks).
  - **The engine exposes the offer** (T25's pattern for battles, T138's for falls). `PendingPeaceOfferFor(nationId)` returns the `PendingPeaceOffer` addressed to that human seat, or none; `HasPendingPeaceOffers` is true while any is pending, the same condition `HandleSave` refuses on. Both are reads; neither changes the pending set.
  - **The window** **[designed, each a default the user may change at the visual review]**: (1) titled *"Offer of peace"* (the original's title), with the engine's sentence as its body and two buttons, **Yes** and **No**; (2) Yes submits `peace-yes` and No submits `peace-no` through the screen's existing submit path, so `MainGameScreen.CommandIssued` counts it and the reply line shows as any command's does; (3) the window has no Close button and Esc does not dismiss it: the offer is answered, as in the original's modal box; (4) it is shown when the battle windows for the active seat are done, after T116's held battles for that seat, and before the hotseat hand-off check, whenever `PendingPeaceOfferFor(active seat)` returns an offer the screen has not yet shown. A battle in the AI phase that raises an offer for the single human seat is therefore answered at that seat's turn start, after its battle window; an offer for a human seat that is not active (hotseat) waits, in the engine, until that seat's turn, as T116's held battles do.
  - **Save** keeps the engine's refusal: the user's decision of 2026-10-05 (PR #729, *"Keep the refusal"*), confirming that of 2026-09-28. Persisting a pending offer in the save is not planned. In a single-human game the window is answered before the player is back on the map, so Save is never refused for an offer. In a hotseat game, an offer held for another seat still refuses it: `SaveToFile` reads `HasPendingPeaceOffers` first and, when the pending offer is not the active seat's, shows *"<Nation> must answer an offer of peace at its turn before the game can be saved."* in the Save confirmation slot and submits nothing **[designed wording]**. The CLI's refusal line is unchanged.
