#### T204 Campaign acceptance: siege-capable soak, packaged launch and owner playthrough

- **Release / priority**: 0.7 series (release-plan §2.4 item 5); P1.
- **Start after**: T202. **Related**: #267 (toy-3city can't be besieged), #1290 (the full-round stall rule, merged).

### Scope

1. **A separately named, siege-capable soak fixture**, so toy-3city's own fixtures and goldens are left alone. Seeded all-AI games must include sieges and city captures. This is asserted, not assumed: at least one capture per N seeds.
2. **An all-AI soak of the Italy scenario** under the soak's existing checks (no exceptions, no rejections, no mismatches, no full-round stalls), inside the soak budget.
3. **A packaged Windows build** (`scripts/release-assets.ps1`) that launches the Italy scenario from New Game. The existing installer test covers the package.
4. **An owner playthrough checklist** for the user's playtest. It covers New Game reachability, nation menus, movement, a field battle, a siege, save and load, and reaching the victory condition or the turn cap.

### Notes from the work (2026-10-21)

- **The siege soak** runs on `AiTestbed.SiegeWorld()`, the toy world with small, lightly walled towns, named separately so toy-3city and its goldens are untouched (#267). It runs 20 seeds × 600 seat-turns.
- **The Italy soak** runs 5 seeds × 600 seat-turns, 50 rounds of 12 nations. `CampaignSoakTests` holds both soaks.
- **`AiGameResult` counts captures and failed sieges.** `CitiesCaptured` counts owner changes between turns; `SiegesFailed` counts `CityFailsToBeCaptured`. Both are shown in its summary line.
- **`ItalyLaunchCheck`** goes through the real chooser to the Italy card and into the game. `scripts/package.ps1 -Verify` runs it inside the exported build.
- **The checklist** is [`docs/investigations/v070-playthrough-checklist.md`](../investigations/v070-playthrough-checklist.md).

### Done when

1. The siege soak and the Italy soak pass in CI inside the budget.
2. The release build workflow is green with the Italy scenario present.
3. The user reports the playthrough on the checklist. Their result decides the 0.7.0 cut, not this task.
