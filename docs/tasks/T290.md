#### T290 Campaign-selection background and playable-power guidance

Owner request [#1811](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/1811), v0.11; [approved scope](../design/v011-campaigns-and-random-maps.md#campaign-selection-background-and-advice).

**Contract:** plain-English authored sidecars in `data/campaign-guides/`, outside campaign state/save formats. A guide names its scenario IDs, kind (historical/generated), period, concise background, political/chronological abstractions, source titles/HTTPS references, maintained brief, opening-validation evidence and power entries. Each power names a playable nation ID, focus, advice, suggested flag and any required starting fleet. Sharing a setting across faithful/improved or barbarian variants must not hide rules differences; retain the existing rules summary separately. No screen-specific historical prose or invented balance rating.

Cover both Classical presets and every listed historical campaign. Development/toy scenarios may omit a guide. New historical campaigns must supply coverage; generated presets need geography guidance rather than historical claims. Validate guide/scenario identities, unique references, playable IDs, fleet assertions, source/brief references and bounded readable text. Keep background separate from gameplay advice. Recommendations based on opening inspection are explicitly provisional, not human-tested difficulty claims.

**UI:** the existing description pane updates with keyboard or pointer selection, showing background, situation/abstraction, suggested/other principal powers and unchanged rules summary. The pane scrolls independently and starts at the top after selection. Continue, default selection, observer and seat-selection behavior stay intact. No campaign creation or RNG draws to render advice. Original manual chapter 1/New Game was consulted for the flow; this owner-requested campaign-guidance pane is the explicit designed extension.

**Done when:** all current historical campaign IDs have valid sourced guidance; unit tests reject stale/unplayable IDs and false fleet claims; real selection updates and keyboard/scroll/Continue flow work at ordinary/high-DPI sizes in four themes; existing campaign/setup checks and exact-head CI pass. Source briefs identify abstraction and play-validation limits. No starting forces, owners, terrain, AI or rules change.
