- **Scope**: [`decompiled-map-code1-overlay.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/decompiled-map-code1-overlay.md) (research commit `0d2674c`) decompiles the weather effect that `WeatherEventSystem` stubs with `[designed]` placeholders:
  - **The rule.** `FUN_00451304` / `FUN_004511bc` run in the weekly tick `FUN_004514ec`, after the fleet loop and before the calendar advances, and once at new game with (Spring, week 1).
    - **Clear first.** Every fleet's in-rough-sea flag becomes 0, and every rough cell within ±10 tiles of each of **20 fixed centres** becomes calm. The centres are in the DAT at `0x1F876`; the SAV does not store them.
    - **Then roll.** Each centre rolls `Random(N) == 0`, where N and a radius r come from the season and week before the advance (winter: N 5, r 5; spring weeks 1–5 and autumn 7–11: 15 and 4; spring 7–11 and autumn 1–5: 30 and 3; summer: 40 and 2). A hit paints the inner (2r+1)² square always, and the ring out to ±2r at 50% per cell.
    - **Where it paints.** Only a calm sea cell with no city in its 3×3. A fleet on a painted cell gets its in-rough-sea flag set instead.
  - **Its effects.** A fleet pays **3** moves to enter rough sea, against 1 for calm. The storm pass triples damage, capped at 8, for a fleet on rough sea (`:54565`). The fleet panel shows "Sea - calm/rough".
  - **The friendly-coast predicate** (`FUN_004494e4`) is also decompiled: a city of the fleet's own nation in its 3×3. That confirms T14's `[designed]` `naval.friendlyCoastRadiusTiles = 1`.
- **Done when**:
  1. **The 20 centres are world data**, exported from the DAT at `0x1F876` through the exporter, each with its provenance. A test asserts the count and two named centres against the bytes, on the configured machine.
  2. **The overlay is state.** `GameState` holds the set of rough-sea cells, and each fleet's in-rough-sea flag or its equivalent. A native save round-trips it; an older-version save loads through a real migration, with the overlay empty; a future version is rejected.
  3. **The weekly rule is the original's.** `WeatherEventSystem` clears, then rolls and paints, exactly as the report states, at the tick position the report gives. The `[designed]` placeholder effects (`storm-damages-fleet`, `drought-reduces-supply`) are removed. Every constant is ruleset data cited to the report: N and r by season and week band, the ±10 clear box, the 50% ring and the 3×3 city exclusion. Tests pin each part, and each fails if that part is deleted: the clear, the inner square, the ring probability over a fixed seed, the city exclusion, and the fleet flag.
  4. **A new game rolls once**, with (Spring, 1), as `FUN_00448AA4` does.
  5. **The effects.** A fleet entering a rough cell pays the ruleset's rough-sea cost (3). The storm branch triples damage (cap 8) for a fleet on rough sea, and is now reachable: a test shows it firing. The friendly-coast `_provenance` becomes `confirmed`, citing `FUN_004494e4`.
  6. **The import maps the overlay.** An imported save's rough cells, the code-1 cells the report explains, become the imported overlay. `WorldPrefix.Cells` leaves the declared-unmapped set, and T21's waiver test is updated to `{MercenaryRecord.X, MercenaryRecord.Y}`. Tested on a winter save against the bytes.
  7. **`sea_deep` is renamed** to what it is: calm and rough sea, per the report's §4. That covers the world's `tileTypes`, the rulesets' terrain rows and `AssetKeys.TerrainSeaDeepTile`, or the PR states why a rename is deferred. No static world cell becomes rough.
  8. **Seeded outcomes.** The weather rolls use `IRng` on their own stream. Every seeded test, soak log or golden that changes is listed in the PR with the reason. The CLI golden changes only if its scenario is classical, and the toy world has no weather centres, so it should not.
  9. `dotnet build IC2.sln` and `dotnet test IC2.sln` are green with and without the original files, and the diff lists only Owns paths.
- **Hazards**:
  - **Order within the tick.** The report places the roll after the fleet storm and supply loop and before the calendar advance, reading the pre-advance season and week. Getting that order wrong reproduces every number and misplaces every effect by a week.
  - **The toy world has no centres.** A world without centres has no weather, which is a valid world. Don't invent centres for it.
  - **The random-number order** against a live seeded run is not established by the report. Say what order you chose and why.
- **Constraint**: `local-only`. Done-when 1 and 6 read the original files.
