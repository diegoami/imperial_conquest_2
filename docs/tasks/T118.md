- **Scope**: the clone's End Turn ends the turn at once. The original first runs a gate and, when any check below holds for a **human** seat, opens a box titled *"End turn ?"*. Every rule below is **[confirmed: code, [`2026-10-03-end-turn-warning-box.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-03-end-turn-warning-box.md) (research `a9c851d`), `FUN_0045AF00`, `FUN_0045AE68` and `TToEndTurn`, `:57688–57920`, `:58238–58366`]** unless marked. The clone asks in both presets [designed: the user's decision of 2026-10-02, recorded in `game-design.md`'s Game menu].
  - **The query.** `EndTurnWarnings.For(state, world, ruleset, nationId)` returns the lines in the original's order, and whether they open the box. It changes no state. It returns nothing for a computer-controlled nation (the gate forces the turn through for an AI seat).
  - **The checks.** All of the nation's armies come first, in the state's army order, then all its fleets, in fleet order. One line per record that trips, never per unit.
    1. **A land army** (not aboard a fleet) needs supplies when `500 × supply < T`, that is under 20 % of its capacity `T div 100`, where `T` is its total troops, or 1 when 0. The line counts only while the nation owns a city. Line: *"An army of yours needs supplies."*
    2. **The same army** cannot pay its mercenaries when its purse is **strictly below** one round of their pay: the sum, over its mercenary slots, of `ArmyUpkeep.ComputeUnit` (the clone's `((troops div 200) × price × quality) div 5`, the same formula the report reads). Line: *"An army of yours cannot afford to pay its mercenary units."* An army with no mercenaries trips only on a negative purse.
    3. **A launched fleet** with no own city in the 3 × 3 around it. Line: *"One of your fleets is not docked at its own city."*
    4. **The same fleet** needs supplies when `supply < ships div 5`, while the nation owns a port. The port test is the clone's own, `CoastalCity.IsCoastal` over the nation's cities [designed: this plan; the original's own exclusion is in Hazards]. Line: *"A fleet of yours needs supplies."*
    5. **An army aboard that fleet**: check 1's supply test, gated by the **fleet's** port test (4), not the army's city test. Its line is check 1's.
    6. **The same aboard army**: check 2. Its line is check 2's.

    Aboard armies are checked only through their fleet: the army loop skips them.
  - **"Needs repairing" never opens the box.** A fleet with condition below 65 adds *"A fleet of yours needs repairing."* (placed before its not-docked line) only when some other check has already opened it.
  - **At most five lines**, the first five in the order above, and no de-duplication: two hungry armies give two identical lines.
  - **Which units are checked, by preset** [designed: the user's decision of 2026-10-03]. A new flag, `endTurnWarningScope` [designed name], with two values:
    - `notActedOnly` in `classical-faithful`: only units that have **not acted** this week are checked, as the original does. An army has not acted when its moves equal `BaseMoves(troops)` less 1 when `supply × troops div 10000 < 10`. That is the original's own recomputation (`FUN_0044AAB4`), whose supply test differs from the weekly tick's `supply × 10000 div troops < 10` **[confirmed: code, [`army-moves-field-signed-and-the-ffff-underflow.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/army-moves-field-signed-and-the-ffff-underflow.md) ~:142–158]**. Reproduce it as read, inconsistency included. A fleet has not acted when its moves equal `FleetAttritionRule.MovesForTurn` for its current ships, carried army, supply and condition (`FUN_0044AB0C`). Checks 5 and 6 use the **fleet's** test.
    - `everyUnit` in `improved`: every own army, launched fleet and aboard army is checked, acted or not.
    - `toy-ruleset.json` carries `notActedOnly`, the faithful value, as it does for every flag but T115's.
  - **Constants are ruleset data.** Reuse `economy.supplyMorale.*` and the army supply divisor where the number and the rule are the same (the not-acted recomputation's 10, 10,000 and moves terms). The new numbers (the 20 % line, the fleet's `ships div 5`, the repair line's 65 and the five-line cap) go in a new `endTurnWarnings` block, each with `_provenance` citing the report.
  - **The box** (`EndTurnBox`, a modal overlay like `ConfirmPrompt`). Caption *"End turn ?"*. Then the lines, then *"If you have not finished your turn click MAKE MORE MOVES."* and *"If you are finished moving this turn click END TURN."* **[confirmed: code + the `TPF0` resource]**. Two buttons:
    - **End turn** submits `end`, as the button does today.
    - **Make more moves** closes the box and submits nothing. The next End Turn press runs every check again.

    Closing the box any other way (Escape) acts as Make more moves **[derived: the report]**.
  - **When.** `OnEndTurnPressed` asks the query for the active seat. With no line, it submits `end` at once, as today; with lines, it opens the box. The menu entry and the toolbar button share this handler.
  - **The CLI is unchanged.** `end` ends the turn with no question, so scripts and goldens are untouched [designed: this plan].
- **Done when**:

- **Hazards**:
  - **The faithful filter misses large starving armies.** Because the not-acted recomputation uses a different supply test from the tick, a large army on little supply reads as having acted and is never warned about: the armies the warning is for **[derived: the report's Inferences]**. That is the original's behaviour, kept in `classical-faithful` by the user's decision; `improved` checks every unit.
  - **The port test's eastern exclusion.** The original's port test (`FUN_004496BC`) ignores cities at `x ≥ 248`, or `x ≥ 214` and `y ≥ 99`, most likely the Caspian. `CoastalCity.IsCoastal` has no such exclusion: that is bug [#600](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/600), Media's Caspian fleet. Use the clone's notion here and change nothing in `CoastalCity`.
  - **The foreign supply sources are not built.** The original's army test also passes, for a nation that owns no city, on a qualifying foreign city while the nation is at war; a nation with no city is eliminated in the clone (T84), so that case is unreachable. The fleet test also passes on a non-hostile foreign port with stock above 120 while the fleet holds more than 10 talents. The clone uses "owns a coastal city" alone, so a nation that has lost every port gets no fleet-supply line where the original might give one. Say so in the PR; it is a follow-up if wanted.
  - **16-bit wraps are not reproduced.** The original casts the supply percentage and the mercenary-pay sum to 16 bits. Both wraps need supply above about 328 times capacity, or a pay sum above 32,767 talents for one army, so they are practically unreachable **[derived]**.
  - **The fleet's not-acted formula is read from code but not checked against saves** (the report's "What this does not establish"). The live check of the not-acted filter is also still to come. If either disagrees, that is a follow-up.
  - **`CoastalCity.IsCoastal` decodes the terrain grid on every call.** Compute the nation's port test once per query.
