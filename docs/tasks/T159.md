- **Scope**:
  - **Protects**: under `classical-faithful`, only an AI seat merges at a distance, only by the original's gates, at most one army merge and one fleet merge a turn. A human seat and `improved` can never reach these commands. Everything the absorbed unit held (troops, unit slots, supplies) is conserved in the survivor; money follows the existing purse policy (below); and no reference to the absorbed id survives.
  - **The rule** ([`2026-10-07-strategic-ai-turn.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-07-strategic-ai-turn.md) §2.4, **[confirmed: decompile]**):
    - **Merge two armies** (`FUN_004509f0`), **one merge a turn**: the smaller army (below **20,000** troops, not aboard a fleet, with orders outstanding) is absorbed by an own army within **18** (Chebyshev) if their combined unit slots are below **20**, the target has fewer than **80,000** troops, and the target is not aboard a fleet. Purses and supplies carry over. The absorbed army does not move first.
    - **Merge two fleets** (`FUN_00450b30`), **one merge a turn**: an idle fleet (fewer than **40** ships, no army aboard, no destination) is absorbed by an own fleet within **34** if their combined ships are below **100** and the target is free the same way. Money and supplies carry over.
    - **Open until the research report (Start after):** the candidate and survivor order; what "orders outstanding", "no destination" and "free the same way" are as predicates (the clone's `ArmyState` and `FleetState` store no order or destination field, so if the predicate needs one, the amendment must add it to Owns with the model and save changes, or state the clone's equivalent); any construction check; and the absorbed unit's moves and position. The amendment pastes the report's excerpt here and marks each `[confirmed: decompile]` or `[derived]`. No mapping is guessed.
    - **Money:** the merges credit the survivor's purse through `PurseAccounting.Credit`, as `JoinArmiesCommandHandler` and `JoinFleetsCommandHandler` already do. It saturates at the field's bound (32,767) and discards the excess, the user-approved overflow policy documented in `PurseAccounting.cs`, unless the research report shows the original's merge does otherwise, which the amendment would then state.
    - **Related, not settling:** the human's Transfer ships, when it empties a fleet, adds the emptied fleet's money and supplies to the other fleet whole, with no cap, then deletes it, and the treasury does not change **[confirmed: Wine; the handler is code; [`2026-10-10-fleet-emptied-by-transfer-money-and-supplies.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/6b96537/docs/reports/2026-10-10-fleet-emptied-by-transfer-money-and-supplies.md), research `6b96537`]**. That is the human path (`TFleetToFleet_OK`), not `FUN_00450b30`, so it does not decide the AI merge's carry-over; the amendment reads the AI merge's body. Bug [#962](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/962) tracks the clone's human path, and its outcome may bear on the purse policy above.
  - **The asymmetry**: a new AI-versus-human difference under `classical-faithful` (the user's decision of 2026-10-11), recorded in `game-design.md` as above.
  - **Out of scope**: T158's sub-phases; `improved`; the human join commands (unchanged: still adjacent only).
- **Done when**:
  1. **The army merge's gates.** On hand-built states, one test per gate fails when it moves by one: the absorbed army's troops (19,999 merges, 20,000 does not), the distance (18 merges, 19 does not), combined slots (19 merges, 20 does not), the target's troops (79,999 merges, 80,000 does not), aboard-a-fleet on either side, and each predicate the research report settles. At most one army merge a turn. **Competing candidates:** with two absorbable armies and two possible survivors, the pair chosen is the one the report's order gives, tested in both collection orders.
  2. **The fleet merge's gates.** The same for fleets: ships (39 and 40), the distance (34 and 35), combined ships (99 and 100), an army aboard, a destination, and the target's freedom (each as the report settles it). At most one fleet merge a turn. Competing candidates as for armies.
  3. **Conservation.** After each merge, the survivor holds the absorbed unit's troops, slots and supplies (sums equal before and after), and its money (sums equal while the total stays within the purse field's bound). A separate boundary test merges two purses whose total exceeds 32,767: the survivor's purse is 32,767, the excess is discarded, and the treasury is unchanged. The absorbed id appears nowhere in the state (armies, fleets, carried-army links, orders, destinations). A save round-trips. Tested with two candidates, one merged and one not.
  4. **Only the AI, only `classical-faithful`.** Each new command is refused for a human seat and under `improved`, each with its rejection code. A human's `join` is still refused at distance 2.
  5. **Nothing regresses.** The soak reports 0 rejected within its budget. The changed goldens and seeded expectations are regenerated or updated and listed with their reason. Build and test are green, and the diff lists only Owns paths.
