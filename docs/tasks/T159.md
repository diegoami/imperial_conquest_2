- **Scope**:
  - **Protects**: under `classical-faithful`, only an AI seat merges at a distance, only by the original's gates, at most one army merge and one fleet merge a turn. A human seat and `improved` can never reach these commands. Everything the absorbed unit held (troops, unit slots, money, supplies) is conserved in the survivor, and no reference to the absorbed id survives.
  - **The rule** ([`2026-10-07-strategic-ai-turn.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-07-strategic-ai-turn.md) §2.4, **[confirmed: decompile]**):
    - **Merge two armies** (`FUN_004509f0`), **one merge a turn**: the smaller army (below **20,000** troops, not aboard a fleet, with orders outstanding) is absorbed by an own army within **18** (Chebyshev) if their combined unit slots are below **20**, the target has fewer than **80,000** troops, and the target is not aboard a fleet. Purses and supplies carry over. The absorbed army does not move first.
    - **Merge two fleets** (`FUN_00450b30`), **one merge a turn**: an idle fleet (fewer than **40** ships, no army aboard, no destination) is absorbed by an own fleet within **34** if their combined ships are below **100** and the target is free the same way. Money and supplies carry over.
    - Which candidate goes first, and what "orders outstanding", "no destination" and "free the same way" map to in the clone's state: the implementer reads `FUN_004509f0` and `FUN_00450b30` in the report (and the dump it cites, if needed), states the mapping in the PR, and stops and reports where the report does not settle it.
  - **The asymmetry**: a new AI-versus-human difference under `classical-faithful` (the user's decision of 2026-10-11), recorded in `game-design.md` as above.
  - **Out of scope**: T158's sub-phases; `improved`; the human join commands (unchanged: still adjacent only).
- **Done when**:
  1. **The army merge's gates.** On hand-built states, one test per gate fails when it moves by one: the absorbed army's troops (19,999 merges, 20,000 does not), the distance (18 merges, 19 does not), combined slots (19 merges, 20 does not), the target's troops (79,999 merges, 80,000 does not), aboard-a-fleet on either side, and orders outstanding. At most one army merge a turn.
  2. **The fleet merge's gates.** The same for fleets: ships (39 and 40), the distance (34 and 35), combined ships (99 and 100), an army aboard, a destination, and the target's freedom. At most one fleet merge a turn.
  3. **Conservation.** After each merge, the survivor holds the absorbed unit's troops, slots, money and supplies (sums equal before and after). The absorbed id appears nowhere in the state (armies, fleets, carried-army links, orders, destinations). A save round-trips. Tested with two candidates, one merged and one not.
  4. **Only the AI, only `classical-faithful`.** Each new command is refused for a human seat and under `improved`, each with its rejection code. A human's `join` is still refused at distance 2.
  5. **Nothing regresses.** The soak reports 0 rejected within its budget. The changed goldens and seeded expectations are regenerated or updated and listed with their reason. Build and test are green, and the diff lists only Owns paths.
- **Hazards**:
  - **Deleting an entity.** An army or fleet is removed: sweep for every reference (incident 7), and keep the save loadable.
  - **Every seeded `classical-faithful` game changes.**
