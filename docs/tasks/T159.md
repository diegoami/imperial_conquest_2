- **Scope**:
  - **Protects**: under `classical-faithful`, only an AI seat merges at a distance, only by the original's gates, at most one army merge and one fleet merge a turn. A human seat and `improved` can never reach these commands. Everything the absorbed unit held (troops, every unit holding troops, supplies) is conserved in the survivor; a unit record with 0 troops is empty space and is not carried over (the user's decision 6, below) (the army donor is absorbed by its partner; the absorbed fleet by the idle outer fleet); money follows the existing purse policy (below); and no reference to the absorbed id survives.
  - **The rule** ([`2026-10-07-strategic-ai-turn.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/3c3707b/docs/reports/2026-10-07-strategic-ai-turn.md) §2.4 as corrected, and [`2026-10-11-decompiled-ai-merge-armies-and-fleets.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/3c3707b/docs/reports/2026-10-11-decompiled-ai-merge-armies-and-fleets.md), research `3c3707b`). Each merge function runs **once per computer seat per round** and stops after its first merge, **[confirmed: decompile]** (C3, A11, F6); "once per round" is **[derived]** from the seat loop. The army merge runs before that nation's army phase, the fleet merge before its fleet phase **[confirmed: decompile]**. No news line, event or treasury write **[confirmed: decompile]**. The distance everywhere is Chebyshev, `max(|dx|, |dy|)` **[confirmed: decompile]** (C1).
    - **Merge two armies** (`FUN_004509f0`), **[confirmed: decompile]** unless marked:
      - **Order (A1).** Two nested table-order loops, not a sort; nearest, smallest and largest play no part. The **donor** is the first army, from table index 0, that passes the donor gates **and has at least one valid partner**; a donor with no partner falls through to the next. The **partner** (the survivor) is the first army from index 0, other than the donor, that passes the partner gates.
      - **Donor gates (A2)**: owner `+4` is the current nation; `troops(donor) < 20,000` (strict; the sum of the 20 slots' troops, A4); covered cell `+8 >= 0` (not aboard). **There is no orders, destination or moves gate.**
      - **Partner gates (A3)**: owner is the current nation; Chebyshev distance `< 18` (so 17 merges, 18 does not); `extent(donor) + extent(partner) < 20` (strict; **extent** is the highest slot index holding troops `> 0`, plus 1, **not** a count of occupied slots, A5); `troops(partner) < 80,000` (strict; the partner's troops alone); partner `+8 >= 0`. **The two sizes are never compared**: the donor can be the larger army.
      - **Carry-over (A6, A7, A8, A10)**: `partner.money += donor.money`; `partner.supplies += donor.supplies` (plain 16-bit adds, no cap, no clamp, no treasury). Then, while the donor's slot-0 troops are `> 0`, one unit slot moves to the partner's first slot after its highest occupied one (gaps are not reused). Slots are appended whole: no merging of same-type slots, no quality averaging. Arrival order for donor slots `[a, b, c]`: `a`, `c`, `b`, in general slot 0, then the donor's last occupied slot, then the next last **[derived]** (trace of `FUN_0044ac3c`). The partner keeps its position, morale and every other field. Moves: when the donor's moves are `0` the partner's moves become `0`, otherwise the partner's moves are left as they are (no min, no max).
      - **Removal (A9, C2)**: the donor is tombstoned in place (owner `-1`) when its last unit moves, and the table is compacted at the next `FUN_0044adb0`, not during the merge. The tombstone can persist into a following human seat's turn **[derived]**. The clone has no tombstones; it deletes the donor in the same step (the user's decision 4, below).
      - **A12 (donor whose slot-0 troops are `<= 0`)**: money and supplies are added, no unit moves, the donor is **not** deleted, and the call's one merge is used up. Whether such an army can exist in play is **not established** by the report (the user's decision 2, below).
    - **Merge two fleets** (`FUN_00450b30`), **[confirmed: decompile]** unless marked. **The direction is the reverse of the old row**:
      - **Order (F1).** The same two nested table-order loops. The **survivor** is the first fleet, from index 0, that passes the survivor gates **and has a valid partner** (an isolated fleet that passes is skipped and the next is tried). The **absorbed** fleet is the first other fleet from index 0 that passes the partner gates.
      - **Survivor gates (F2)**: owner `+8` is the current nation; `ships < 40` (strict); carried army `+0x16 == -1`; construction countdown `+0xA == -1` (launched). **`+0xA` is the construction countdown, not a destination**; the function reads no destination field.
      - **Absorbed-fleet gates (F3)**: owner is the current nation; Chebyshev distance `< 34` (so 33 merges, 34 does not); `survivor.ships + absorbed.ships < 100` (strict, so 99 is the largest total); `+0x16 == -1`; `+0xA == -1`. The 40-ship cap is **not** applied to it, and the sizes are not compared: it can be the larger fleet.
      - **Carry-over (F4)**: `survivor.ships += absorbed.ships`; `survivor.money += absorbed.money`; `survivor.supplies += absorbed.supplies` (plain 16-bit adds, no cap). The survivor's position, moves, condition and owner are untouched; the absorbed fleet's moves and condition are discarded (condition is not averaged).
      - **Removal (F5)**: the survivor's map marker is re-banded; the absorbed fleet's map word is set to `0` (not to its covered cell) and the fleet is tombstoned (owner `-1`); compaction as for armies (C2).
    - **The predicates on the clone's records.** The clone's `ArmyState` and `FleetState` have no order or destination field, and none is needed, because the report shows no gate reads one. No field is added to Owns. Mapping (each is `[derived]` from the field names and the importer, not a decompile fact):
      - owner `== current nation` → `ArmyState.Nation` / `FleetState.Nation`; the position → `X`, `Y`; troops → `ArmyState.TotalTroops`; ships → `FleetState.Ships`.
      - army "not aboard" (`+8 >= 0`) → `ArmyState.AboardFleetId is null` (`IsEmbarked` false). The clone keeps `CoveredTileCode` beside it; the gate must read `AboardFleetId`, which the importer fills from the fleet's carried-army link (`EmbarkationLinker`).
      - fleet "no army aboard" (`+0x16 == -1`) → `FleetState.CarriedArmyId is null` (`IsCarryingArmy` false).
      - fleet "launched" (`+0xA == -1`) → `FleetState.ConstructionTicksRemaining is null` (`IsUnderConstruction` false).
      - army "orders outstanding" and fleet "no destination": **no gate exists in the original**, so there is no clone field to map; the old summary row's words are dropped.
      - slot **extent** → no stored field. `ArmyState.Units` is a list with no gaps, so the extent equals `Units.Count` **provided every held slot has troops `> 0`** (the user's decision 3, below).
      - the donor's and the partner's moves → `ArmyState.Moves`.
      - the map markers and the absorbed fleet's map word → presentation only; the clone derives markers from state, so nothing is written.
    - **Money:** the report shows the original's merges add the donor's (or absorbed fleet's) money to the survivor as a **plain 16-bit add: no cap, no clamp at 32,767, no treasury deposit** **[confirmed: decompile]** (A6, F4); what the 16-bit add yields above 32,767 (a wrap, possibly to a negative purse) is not read by the report and stays **[derived]** as in `PurseAccounting.cs`. Below the bound this equals `PurseAccounting.Credit`, which `JoinArmiesCommandHandler` and `JoinFleetsCommandHandler` already use. The entry therefore **keeps its policy**: the merges credit the survivor's purse through `PurseAccounting.Credit`, saturating at 32,767 and discarding the excess, the user-approved overflow policy documented there; this departs from the original only above the field's bound (the user's decision 1, below). Supplies are added whole, with no cap, as the original does.
    - **Related, not settling:** the human's Transfer ships, when it empties a fleet, adds the emptied fleet's money and supplies to the other fleet whole, with no cap, then deletes it, and the treasury does not change **[confirmed: Wine; the handler is code; [`2026-10-10-fleet-emptied-by-transfer-money-and-supplies.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/6b96537/docs/reports/2026-10-10-fleet-emptied-by-transfer-money-and-supplies.md), research `6b96537`]**. That is the human path (`TFleetToFleet_OK`), not `FUN_00450b30`, so it does not decide the AI merge's carry-over; the amendment reads the AI merge's body. Bug [#962](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/962) tracks the clone's human path, and its outcome may bear on the purse policy above.
  - **The user's decisions of 2026-10-11** (on PR #983; they settle the five open points the amendment posed, plus decision 6 on Sol's R1):
    1. **Overflow above 32,767:** the merges credit through `PurseAccounting.Credit`, saturating at 32,767 and discarding the excess, as T160 does. This is a `[designed]` divergence from the original's 16-bit wrap, which applies only above the field's bound.
    2. **The donor with slot-0 troops `<= 0` (A12):** treated as unreachable in the clone, because `ArmyState.Units` holds no empty slots. It is not reproduced and not tested.
    3. **Extent versus count:** the slot gate computes the extent from the last unit with troops `> 0`, plus 1, not from `Units.Count`, so it is faithful even if a 0-troop unit ever exists.
    4. **The tombstone window:** the merged-away army or fleet is deleted at once. The original's tombstone until the next compaction has no observable effect in the clone.
    5. **Unit order:** the donor's units are appended to the survivor in the original's order, slot 0, then the last, then the next-to-last, and so on (**[derived]**, A8), not in the donor's own order. A test pins the order.
    6. **Empty unit records (0 troops):** they are empty space, as in the original, whose merge moves only occupied slots, and as the clone's mobilization already treats them (`MobilizationReceivingArmy.FirstFreeUnitSlot` writes a new unit over a trailing 0-troop record). The army merge carries over only the donor's units holding troops, in decision 5's order, appended after the partner's last unit holding troops; the partner's trailing 0-troop records are dropped, so the survivor never exceeds `management.maxUnitsPerArmy` (20) and the extent gate (decision 3) bounds its size. (The user's decision of 2026-10-11 on Sol's R1, PR #984.)

At most one army merge a call. **Competing candidates:** the donor is the first army in table order that has a valid partner (a first army with no partner is skipped), and its partner is the first valid one in table order, tested in both collection orders. Moves: a donor with 0 moves zeroes the partner's moves, otherwise the partner's moves are unchanged.
  2. **The fleet merge's gates.** The same for fleets: the survivor's ships (39 merges, 40 does not), the distance (33 merges, 34 does not), combined ships (99 merges, 100 does not), a carried army on either fleet, and under-construction (`ConstructionTicksRemaining` not null) on either fleet; there is no destination gate. A test shows the absorbed fleet may hold 40 or more ships, and that the **idle outer fleet survives** and the partner is removed. At most one fleet merge a call. Competing candidates as for armies (the survivor is the first fleet in table order that has a valid partner).
  3. **Conservation.** After each merge, the survivor holds the absorbed unit's troops, its units holding troops, and its supplies (sums equal before and after; the survivor's unit count is the partner's units holding troops plus the donor's). A test with 0-troop records on both the donor and the partner shows they are dropped and the troop sums still match (decision 6), and its money (sums equal while the total stays within the purse field's bound). A separate boundary test merges two purses whose total exceeds 32,767: the survivor's purse is 32,767, the excess is discarded, and the treasury is unchanged. The absorbed id (the army donor, the absorbed fleet) appears nowhere in the state (armies, fleets, carried-army links, orders, destinations). A save round-trips. Tested with two candidates, one merged and one not.
