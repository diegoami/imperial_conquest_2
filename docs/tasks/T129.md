#### T129 The tactical battle's golden master: the port replays the original's recorded battles half-round by half-round

- **Design milestone**: M21 (the tactical battle, v0.6.0 *Battles*, moved from v0.5.0 by the user's decision of 2026-10-04, [#496](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/496)). The user's decision of 2026-10-04 moved the check against the original in play out of the design and into the tasks, as a golden master built from the decompile's 11-point list. This is that golden master: it reads the original's mid-battle saves and replays them through the port with the original's own generator. **Labels**: `phase:2 lane:engine local-only`

Its `_r5_` trials' series are byte-identical to the unhooked lab battles, and each has `hooklog-<trial>.csv` (every draw: site, range, result, `RandSeed` before and after, half-round counter, side) and `exchanges-<trial>.jsonl` (each shot, melee, rout test, flank and placement draw with its actor, target, `n` and loss), in the conquest repository's `runs/experiments/data/run-exp-battle-hook/` (branch `experiment/battle-hook-b11`, `d3572fd`). The hook replayed the report's shot, melee and rout formulas on all 318 battles with 0 differences, and found the report's draw order with no mismatch, so on these series a difference from the original is the port's (or its general's choices), not the report's formulas. The lab build sets `RandSeed` to its baked seed at `TBattleMap.StartBattle`, which is both the start and the resume path, and writes one `BATTLEnn.SAV` per half-round **after a side's moves and before its melee**. So each series replays from its start save and its seed with the Delphi generator of T123's replay implementation. The block layout is the decompile's (§1), and the research repository's `scripts/battle-block-check.py` reads it independently.

    **Each hooked trial's pairing is checked before it is replayed**, and a failed check is a fixture error, reported as such with the trial, never a port failure: the log's battle-start record carries the row's seed as `RandSeed`, its seed chain is unbroken (each record's seed-before is the previous record's seed-after), and its highest half-round counter equals the series' last `BATTLEnn` number. Because the save, the seed and the log come from one run, a passing check leaves no pairing to doubt.

  - **How a series is replayed.** Import the start save (T21's importer), set the attacker and defender and each side's control as the table says, seed the replay generator with the series' seed, and run T123's copy-in, T124's general and T123's half-rounds. At each point where the lab writes a save (the call to the half-round end), compare the port's battle block with the file's, all 9 header bytes, 40 slots and 168 cells. At the end, compare the post-battle strategic fields the write-back touches (both armies' units, money, supplies, unity, the news line). A resumed series starts from the imported block, reseeds, and runs T123's `Resume`. **For a series with a draw log**, the replay also records every draw the port takes through its seam and compares the sequence with the log's `Random` records in order, each draw's range and result, the half-round counter telling which half-round it falls in; the hook's boundary and marker records (battle start, flag clear, reseed, the shot, melee and rout entries) are not draws and are skipped, though the shot markers' actor and target may be used to name the first differing exchange.

Whether the original's box opened in a given series is not a fixture here, so it is not asserted per series.
  6. `git diff --stat origin/main...HEAD -- src/IC2.Engine/Battle src/IC2.Engine/Presentation` is empty (the golden master changes no rule), no save or game file is added to this repository (`git diff --stat origin/main...HEAD | grep -i "\.sav"` prints nothing), and `dotnet build IC2.sln` and `dotnet test IC2.sln` are green with the diff listing only Owns paths.
  7. **Every draw matches the original's log.** For every series with a `hooklog` (the B5 and `mix-rg` rows), the port's draws through its seam equal the log's `Random` records one for one, in order, range and result, from the first copy-in draw to the log's last `Random` record, which is the peace draw when the log has one and otherwise the last promotion draw (`0x4592BD`), with none extra and none missing: the port takes no draw after that record through the end of its write-back and peace test; the PR lists each series with its draw count and `PASS`. A failing series is reported with the first differing draw (its index, half-round counter, the original's site and range, the port's range) before any block comparison of that series is read.
- **Hazards**:
  - **The evidence is Wine-only, on a lab build.** A mismatch can be the port's, the lab patch's or Wine's. File it as a bug with the field and the half-round; do not change the port in this task, and do not loosen a check. The user decides what a confirmed mismatch means.
  - **Known unexplained points.** The sweep saw a resume raise the counter by 2 once (the code gives 1), and Gaul's light infantry reading 1 move (the slow advance explains it). If either fails, report it against the report, not the port.
  - **Who is computer-controlled.** Rome is a human nation under *Computer general*: no `+3`, no sort, no slow advance for it. Gaul is a computer nation in every series, `hi-hi-one` included: the sweep's "both sides on Computer general" means both sides are played by the general, not that Gaul's nation is human, so Gaul takes the `+3` (which enters its copy-in morale). Getting this wrong fails check 1 and 4 at the first file. The hook confirms it from the draws: in the 315 B5 battles, both armies saved at morale 65, Gaul's copy-in base was 68 and Rome's 65; and Rome, placed by the general, still draws its own placement `Random(5)` (two per battle).
  - **Importing a mid-battle save needs [#675](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/675).** A block save carries its 2,105 bytes after the trailer, and `SaveTurnState`, `SaveNewsLog` and `SavePendingOffer` all find the trailer from the file's end, so the resumed series' start (`gate2_a_BATTLE05.SAV`) and the crafted saves do not import today. This task owns only `SaveTurnState.cs`; if #675 has not merged when this task starts, stop and report it rather than editing the other two readers.
  - **The `gate2_a` start.** The probe staged its gate series from `1_rome_270_winter_11.sav` by walking Rome's army 0 to (86,28) itself; `FLD-RG_0743_rome_army0_at_86_28.SAV` is the sweep's rebuild of that state. If the first file of `gate2_a` fails, compare the two armies' records in FLD-RG with the probe's staged pre-attack save (`NB_pre_attack-*.SAV`, release `run-exp-battle-probe`) before suspecting the port.
  - **The hook's figures are the bot's.** The research repository promoted the hook report without re-reading its saves or buffers, and did not re-derive the B5 counts; a `hooklog` whose seed chain does not start at the series' seed, or a series whose hash differs from `SAVES.sha256` or `trials-b11.jsonl`, is reported against the evidence, not the port.
  - **Rout at morale 20.** The decompile's `Rout` draws when `morale > 19`; the hook report words the band `20 < morale ≤ 39`, and which the original applies at exactly 20 is open. T123 ports the decompile. A first difference at a rout test at morale 20 fails the series like any other difference: it is reported with the series, half-round and slot and investigated, with no cause assumed in advance.
  - **The flank draws are not predictable from the snapshots** (the hook): how many `Random(3)` a half-round takes depends on the general's moves inside it, so a draw-count difference there points at T124's pass order or flank eligibility, the choices the design names as T129's to check.
  - **Fixture volume.** The B5 and `mix-rg` series are about 4,800 `BATTLEnn` files of 134,254 bytes, some 650 MB, against the 585 saves of the probe and the first sweep. If the fixtures repository cannot hold them, the planner decides which series to take (the open question raised with the hook's evaluation); this task does not trim the table itself.
  - **The saves are the user's game files.** They stay in the private fixtures repository; nothing from them is committed here. Without them the tests skip explicitly, and the PR shows a local run.
  - **The 11-point list is the report's.** A check not covered by a fixture (two humans, a human-played side, `improved`) is named as uncovered in the PR, not invented.
