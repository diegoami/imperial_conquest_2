# T242 — The classic look draws the original's selection frame

The original-fidelity gate T196 left on #1010. Owner decision 2026-10-30: the **classic** asset pack reproduces the original's selection frame exactly, and the **improved** pack keeps T196's clear selection feedback. Evidence: [`2026-10-30-unit-map-selection-drawing1010.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/73f3d9b/docs/reports/2026-10-30-unit-map-selection-drawing1010.md) (research `73f3d9b`; five captures matched pixel for pixel).

How the original draws it (`TUnitMap_SelectUnit` → `FUN_004465a8`):
- **Who gets it:** any selected cell, whether a town, army or fleet, own or foreign.
- **What is drawn:** TerrainList image 12, a black rhombus outline with corners at the edge midpoints, 3–4 px thick on a 32 px tile, drawn with `SRCERASE`. Where the image is white, the tile and its marker are inverted (sea turns yellow); on the outline itself the pixels are black.
- **No blink:** there is no timer.

## Contract

- **Manual first** (CLAUDE.md rule 10). Read the original manual's sections and screenshots on selecting a unit or town through the operating-guide entry point, cite them, and record any difference from the research in the PR.
- **Classic pack:**
  - Draw the frame exactly as described: inside the rhombus, the selected cell's terrain and marker inverted; the outline black; no animation.
  - Apply it to a selected town as well, own or foreign, wherever the remake has a selected city, and to a selected army or fleet.
  - The rhombus mask's geometry comes from the research's description (the 32 px image), scaled with the tile as the map scales. Original-game image files never enter the repo; reconstruct the mask from the description.
- **Improved pack:** T196's selection feedback is unchanged.
- **Travel:** T196's guarantees for a selected army or fleet during strategic travel (the feedback stays on the travelling marker inside the one-tile footprint) hold in both packs.
- **No rule changes,** and no change to which entities can be selected.
