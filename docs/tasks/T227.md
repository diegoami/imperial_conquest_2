# T227 — Remove auto-recruit plans from player flows and execution

Owner design correction #1498 (2026-10-27) supersedes T210. Plans are confusing and are removed from both Settings and Recruit unit, rather than merely hidden while still issuing orders.

## Contract

- Remove the auto-recruit switch, saved-plan summaries and queue-target controls from Settings and Recruit unit. Remove the active auto-recruit planner and the End-turn invocation. No hidden legacy plan may enqueue a new order.
- Load valid old settings without losing unrelated preferences. Ignore/discard obsolete enabled/plans fields and omit them on subsequent writes. Retain only the compatibility shape needed by old input/test fixtures; no campaign/save schema changes.
- Preserve manual recruitment, already placed recruitment slots, training advancement, manual mobilisation and Average-or-better auto-mobilize. Existing travel/rally/support remain in their order around confirmed End turn, with one ordinary end command. Cancel remains inert.
- Supply/purse targets and reserve policy are a separate #1497 correction; do not change those rules in this removal.

## Owns

UserSettings obsolete-plan compatibility/normalization; SettingsScreen recruitment controls; RecruitUnitDialog plan controls/helpers; MainGameScreen RunAutoRecruit and confirmed-turn invocation; retired AutoRecruitPlanner and its linked tests/project entry; matching Settings/StrategyDialogs/EndTurn checks and UserSettings migration tests; T210 supersession note/task catalogue. Recruitment engine, save format, unit quality and other automation planners are read-only.

## Done when

1. Old settings containing enabled plans load with plans inactive/empty, preserve other options, and save without obsolete fields. No startup failure or campaign conversion is introduced.
2. Production Settings and Recruit unit contain no plan/switch/queue-target controls. Manual recruit/mobilize/training controls and quality labels remain; switching towns still works.
3. Actual confirmed End turn with a supplied legacy plan creates no recruit order or queue entry, preserves existing unready slots, still mobilizes Average troops and moves/supports units in order. Make more moves/Cancel does nothing.
4. Focused tests, solution/Godot builds, SettingsCheck/StrategyDialogsCheck/EndTurnBoxCheck and CI pass. No owner settings/game/private saves are modified.

Docs affected: this contract/task index, T210 policy supersession and any player instructions naming auto-recruit plans.
