#### T285 Immediate Classical observer launch and continuous showcase

Owner request, 2026-11-08, [#1824](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/1824), [contract](../design/v011-continuous-observer.md). Release: v0.11.0. Workflow: `docs/cloud-mode.md`.

**Scope:** `--observe` and `--observe-loop` desktop user arguments; immediate Classical Faithful all-AI startup; independent random watched-civilization selection and elimination reselection; campaign completion/reseed/restart after presentation drains; slow local camera-follow playback; transient loop option in observer controls. Ordinary startup, paused observer entry, human play and saved preferences retain their behavior. Runtime errors stop visibly.

**Owns:** new Godot-free launch/loop policy classes; `AppRoot`, `MainGameScreen.Observer*`, necessary strategic presentation hooks; dedicated engine/UI lifecycle tests and Godot check scene; test-project links, CI registration and operating-guide launch documentation. No combat/AI/ruleset/save-schema changes. Reference the private manual chapters 1–3/10 and their New Game/map screenshots before GUI work; this owner's observer convenience is the exception.

**Done when:**

1. Real startup with each flag bypasses menus, selects Classical Faithful, has no human seat and begins playing. No flag preserves the menu. Unknown/incompatible observer options report a useful error rather than starting another mode.
2. Loop follows one live playable civilization, switches only when eliminated at a safe presentation boundary, excludes barbarians, and never consumes campaign RNG for selection. Tests include a single survivor and no eligible survivors.
3. Completed campaigns restart only after movement/battle presentation drains and a brief ending; fresh campaign seeds differ. Pause prevents restart/advance; exit disposes the loop. Errors/stalls do not produce a silent restart cycle. Test completion and elimination separately.
4. Slow strategic/battle playback and local movement framing use the production observer/presentation pipeline. Transient defaults preserve stored preferences; turning the loop off restores normal controls. Existing ObserverModeCheck/ObserverPlaybackCheck and a real-loop control check pass.
5. Build/test solution and Godot, record exact-head CI and limitations; update launch documentation. Browser implementation is separate under #1797 and shares the observation policy where practical.
