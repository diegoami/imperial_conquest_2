#### T124 The tactical battle's computer general: placement, target choice and the three movement passes

- **Design milestone**: M21 (the tactical battle, v0.5.0 *Battles*, [#496](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/496)). It ports `game-design.md`'s Combat, "The tactical battle", paragraph "The computer general": the original's own AI for a battle, which plays every computer-controlled side and every side with *Computer general* on. **Labels**: `phase:2 lane:engine`
- **Branch**: `task/T124-tactical-general` · **Model/effort**: **Opus** / High. The AI is architecture-class under [§3.3](../build-process.md#33-model-selection), and this general decides every battle a human fights against the computer; its claims, focus counts and sorts are order-sensitive, and an order slip moves every draw after it · **Reviewer**: **very complex** under [§3.4](../build-process.md#34-why-the-reviewers-model-differs-from-the-implementers) criterion (a): Claude implements, so Sol **and** the Luna pair
- **Start after**: T123 · **Merge after**: T123. It may run beside T125 (disjoint Owns). It is **never in flight with** T121.
- **Owns**:
  - `src/IC2.Engine/Battle/Tactical/General/**` (new): the `ITacticalGeneral` implementation T123 calls;
  - `tests/IC2.Engine.Tests/Battle/Tactical/General/**` (new).
- **Scope**:
  - **Formula-shape constants stay in code** (added 2026-10-04, from T124's first attempt, PR 685; the rule T122's entry applies to the melee shape): the placement block's width 3 and first column 1, pass 2's shooter bonus `s div 4` and its odds test `2 × theirs div 3`, the score's `× 2` at the claim limit, and the adjacent engage's stop at 1 move. Each is one named constant citing the report section that states it. Every other value comes from `combat.tactical`. **The "HI + Ar" block:** the formations' `heavy_infantry` entry places archers with heavy infantry, as T122's data states (T122's Done-when 1). The archers' type is `Ruleset.ArcherUnitTypeId`. The block's heavy-infantry type is named by one cited constant, or by the formation table, never borrowed from an unrelated field such as `slowAdvanceExemptType`.
  - **Protects**: the decompiled computer general's decisions and draws: the `Form[Random(5)]` placement with its stacking, the `[1, 4, 3, 0, 2]` type order and slot order, the target preferences and scores, the claims and focus counts, engage, passes 2 and 3, the flank's `Random(3)`, and the clear-line test. Forbidden: a "smarter" or tidied general, a fixed negative-shots loop, a draw added or reordered, a constant not read from T122's `combat.tactical` (other than the cited formula-shape constants the Scope names), a type identity borrowed from an unrelated ruleset field, and any change to T123's rules or the strategic AI (`src/IC2.Engine/Ai/**`).
  - Every rule is **[confirmed: code, static, [`2026-10-04-decompiled-tactical-battle-rules.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-04-decompiled-tactical-battle-rules.md) §2 and §7]**, as `game-design.md` restates it:
    - **placement** (`FUN_004381A4`): clear the side's cells, `r = Random(5)`, the base row and step per side, four blocks of three columns from `placementFormations[r]`, slot order within a type, archers continuing a heavy-infantry block, rows held at the third so that a block of more than nine units stacks;
    - **movement** (`FUN_0043A31C`): `typeOrder`, then live units in slot order; pass 1 (target choice `FUN_00439E08`, score, claims; engage `FUN_0043A160`); pass 2 (`FUN_0043A544`, whose shooting loop does not check shots); pass 3 (`FUN_0043ABB4`: the threat sort, pass 2, the flank with its `Random(3)`, pass 2 again); the clear-line test (`FUN_00439F00`).
  - It acts only through T123's orders (move, shoot, set a target) and reads only T123's state, so every rule a unit obeys is T123's. The slow advance and the computer-only movement branches are T123's.
- **Done when**:
  1. **Placement tests** (report check 2), under scripted draws: for each `r = 0 … 4`, an army of each type lands in the design's columns and rows for both sides; heavy infantry and archers share a block; an army of ten or more heavy infantry and archers stacks units on occupied cells exactly as the design's counters give; one `Random(5)` per computer side, defender first.
  2. **Target-choice tests**: each type's preference order; the score `troops × quality × morale × M[theirs][mine] div 10000`, divided by `c + 1` below the claim limit and doubled at or above it; ties to the first; claims recorded and cleared at setup.
  3. **Engage and pass tests**: archers firing at distance below 3 until moves, shots or the target run out; the adjacent shoot-then-target case stopping at 1 move; the move toward a clear line; the box search in `dx`-outer, `dy`-inner order with a strict `<`; pass 2's shot target by `troops div s` with `s div 4` added for a shooter, its loop driving shots below 0, and its melee target with the `2 × theirs' div 3 < mine` condition; pass 3's threat sort (archers with shots by `−shotBound`) and the flank's `h`, its `Random(3)` flip, `v` with 0 as −1, and the two clamped tries.
  4. **The path** (report check 9): a scripted computer half-round of a mixed army logs each unit's destination, path and detours, and the test asserts them against values derived by hand in its comment.
  5. **A whole battle runs.** With this general on both sides and a scripted or replayed draw source, a battle between two mixed armies ends (one side empty) within a bounded number of half-rounds, and two runs give byte-identical traces.
  6. `git diff --stat origin/main...HEAD -- src/IC2.Engine/Battle/Tactical ':!src/IC2.Engine/Battle/Tactical/General' src/IC2.Engine/Ai src/IC2.Engine/Battle/InstantBattleResolver.cs` is empty, and `AiSoakTests` and `BattleDeterminismTests` pass unchanged.
  7. `dotnet build IC2.sln` and `dotnet test IC2.sln` are green, and the diff lists only Owns paths.
- **Hazards**:
  - **Port the general as it is.** The negative shots, the stacking, the `int` wrap in pass 2's `theirs − focus × theirs`, the threat sort's selection sort (not a stable library sort) and every tie rule are the original's. A library sort or a LINQ `OrderBy` changes tie order and is a bug here.
  - **If T123's orders cannot express a step**, stop and report; do not add a rule inside the general.
  - **The strategic AI is a different thing.** `src/IC2.Engine/Ai/**` decides whether to attack; this general fights. Neither changes the other.
  - **Done-when 5 is a liveness check, not a fidelity one.** Fidelity against the original is T129's.
