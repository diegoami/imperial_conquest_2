Classical-faithful uses F670's raw resupply pick. E84C accepts offers strictly less than 20 tiles away, retaining the first mercenary-pool slot on equality. These two tie domains are different.

Reachable resupply, reachable garrison and emergency moves remain in Improved only, unchanged. Faithful never substitutes a reachable city or emergency goal for the original destination. A raw helper return alone is insufficient: audit `AddMarchCandidate`'s single-walk progress/proposability filters, emergency calls in both proposal and post-command fallback, and the latter's default single-walk dispatch. Do not reproduce the original's undefined record-minus-one reads, add independent user toggles/save fields, or claim a complete global phase/scheduling rewrite.
