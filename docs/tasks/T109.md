- **Scope**: the four Strategy entries that T100 left disabled open the original's dialogs, from the menu and from the toolbar alike. Each commits through an engine verb on OK, and Cancel submits nothing.
  - **Taxation** (`TChangeTax`, titled "Change tax level"): a slider over T103's range, the integers 0 to 40 read from the ruleset's bounds, one step per arrow key and 5 per Page key **[Wine candidate: [`2026-10-02-unit-map-mouse-orders-and-tax-range.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-02-unit-map-mouse-orders-and-tax-range.md)]**, showing the current and the new rate and income, `income = taxBase × rate / 100` **[confirmed: `decompiled-fleet-tax-and-mercenary-formulas.md`]**, computed by the engine's `TaxIncome`. OK submits `set-tax`.
  - **Balance sheet** (`TBalanceSheet`): T104's record, read-only, OK only.
  - **Recruit unit** (`TArmyRecruits`, with Mobilize moved into it by the user's decision): pick one of the active nation's cities, a unit type and a size, with the initial and quarterly cost from `StandingRecruitmentCost`. Below that is the list of the city's units in training, with three buttons:
    - **Recruit unit**: `recruit-standing`;
    - **Mobilize**: `mobilize` the selected ready unit. The dialog first says where the regiment will appear: the army the engine picks next to the city, or a new army there (issue #520 item 5);
    - **Disband**: T108's `disband-slot`, on the selected unit.

    The troop box keeps bug #519's fix: from `standardBattalionSize / 5` to `standardBattalionSize` of the selected type, a default of a fifth, a step of 100 and 1,000 with the page keys, updated when the type changes (the user's decision of 2026-10-01, PR #550).
  - **Build fleet**: first the fleets under construction, as *"A fleet of N ships will be ready in W weeks at C."*, then the original's refusals, word for word: *"Only nations with coastal cities can build fleets."*, *"You do not have a free coastal city at this time."* and *"You cannot build a fleet at this time."* Otherwise `TBuildFleet`: 10 to 100 ships at `ships × 10` **[derived: code, `TPremierForm_BuildNewFleet`; costs confirmed: `decompiled-unit-map-orders-and-record-fields.md`]**. OK submits `order-fleet`.
  - **The context panel loses** the city panel's Recruit section and the army panel's "Mobilize first ready slot". It keeps the city's "In training here" list, which is information.

- **Hazards**:
  - **Which cities may recruit is [open]** (issue #515). Offer the cities `recruit-standing` accepts, and let the engine's own refusal speak. Don't add a rule in the UI.
  - **Whether `TBuildFleet` lets the player pick the city is [open]**: "a free coastal city" suggests the game picks one. Offer the free coastal cities `order-fleet` accepts, say so in a code comment, and file a research question if the review asks.
  - **The engine refuses recruitment and fortification on a short treasury, and the original doesn't** (bug #549). That is the engine's rule. Show its refusal; don't work around it.
  - If T108 is still waiting on its research read when everything else here is done, the main session decides through a plan PR whether Disband splits out. The implementer doesn't drop it.
