#### T203 Barbarians: design, then a scoped mechanic

- **Owner placement:** v0.9.0, delivery [#1683](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/1683), separated from the old Italy umbrella [#900](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/900).
- **This is a design task first.** Read [the design and implementation contract](../design/barbarians.md). Barbarians are a designed mechanic, not an original-game fidelity finding.

### Owner decisions, 2026-11-01

- Random spawning in remote areas, without fixed camps.
- Capture and retention of cities.
- Permanent hostility, without peace or trade.

### After the decisions

The linked contract separates approved rules from proposed tuning and unresolved gameplay choices. Do not implement the latter as settled decisions before the owner answers. Split implementation into faction lifecycle/hostility, deterministic spawning/movement/capture, and presentation/campaign acceptance. Each slice needs its own task contract, issue, owned paths and meaningful controls before coding.
