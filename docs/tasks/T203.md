#### T203 Barbarians: design, then a scoped mechanic

- **Release / priority**: 0.7 series candidate; placement is the user's decision (D6). Issue [900](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/900), "Barbarians: a new `[designed]` mechanic".
- **This is a design task first.** The original game has no barbarians, so their behaviour is the user's design, posed as a short decision document before any code.

### Questions for the user

1. **Seat.** Is the barbarian side a pseudo-nation within the 16, with no capital?
2. **Diplomacy.** Are they never at peace, so they never thaw, trade or ally and are never offered peace?
3. **Spawning.** Where and when do they appear: map edges, specific tiles, a seasonal chance, scaling with the year?
4. **Behaviour.** Do they raid the nearest city, and can they take and hold cities? What happens to a city they take?
5. **Victory and UI.** Are they excluded from victory counting, shown in the Nations menu, and shown on the map?

### After the decisions

Split into engine tasks: a nation flag and its validation, a spawn system with ruleset data, an AI profile, diplomacy exclusions and victory exclusion. Each gets its own contract, Owns and tests. Nothing is implemented under this entry until the decisions are recorded on #900.
