  - **The order** **[confirmed: [`decompiled-unit-map-orders-and-record-fields.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/decompiled-unit-map-orders-and-record-fields.md), `TUnitMap_SupplyArmy` `0x00446F50` opens `TAFSupply` `0x0043EDC0`]**. Unit map → Army → Supply army acts on the selected army. When a fleet carrying an army is selected, it acts on the army aboard **[derived: code, the prologue of each `TUnitMap_*` army order, as T111 states it]**. With no own army selected, the screen says *"Select one of your armies first."* and opens nothing **[designed wording]**.
  - **The providers** **[confirmed: code, `TAFSupply_FindProviders`, [`supply-capacity-rounding.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/supply-capacity-rounding.md) "Which path a purchase takes"]**: every city within one tile (Chebyshev distance ≤ `economy.commandAdjacencyRadiusTiles`, 1) whose owner is not at war with the army's nation, and every own fleet within one tile that is not under construction. `SupplyProviders.ForArmy(state, armyId, ruleset)` returns them in the state's order, cities first, each marked free (own city, own fleet) or paid (foreign city). It applies exactly the gates `BuySupplyCommandHandler` applies, and Done-when 1 proves the two agree. With no provider, the screen says *"No city or fleet of yours, or of a nation at peace with you, within one tile."* and opens nothing **[designed wording]**. The menu entry itself stays enabled: `GameCommandTable` has no per-selection enabled state today.
  - **The supply panel** lists the providers with their stock, as the original's window does ("Supplies at Antium: 330", **[Wine candidate: [`2026-10-02-fleet-orders-live.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-02-fleet-orders-live.md)]**, the fleet window; the army window is the same dialog), and shows the army's supply and its room. The steps are **10 and 100** tons **[confirmed: code, `supply-capacity-rounding.md`; the 10s and 100s arrows, Wine candidate above]**. The room is `troops div 100 − supply + 1` (`economy.armySupplyTonsPerTroops` 100 and `economy.supplyDialogArmyCapacityBonus` 1), and nothing floors it at 0 **[confirmed: code, `TAFSupply_ChangeSupply` `0x0043FC74` and `TAFSupply_ChangeBuyAmount` `0x0043FE4C`]**.
    - **A free provider** (own city or own fleet) **applies each press at once** **[confirmed: code, `TAFSupply_ChangeSupply`, "each `+` press applies straight to the army and the provider"]**. A press of `+10` or `+100` submits one `buy` of that step. The engine clamps it to the provider's stock and the room, so a press on an army past its room gives supply back, as the original does. No money moves.
    - **A paid provider** (a foreign city) **stages an amount**: `±10` and `±100` change it, floored at 0 and clamped to the city's stock, the room and, under `classical-faithful`'s per-unit purses, the army's money × 5 (`economy.supplyTonsPerTalent`, 5) **[confirmed: code, `TAFSupply_ChangeBuyAmount`]**; under `improved` the treasury pays and there is no money clamp (`SupplyPurchase`'s remarks, design-audit Q4). The panel shows the cost, `amount div 5` talents, paid to the city's owner **[confirmed: code, `TAFSupply_TransferSupply` `0x0043FF98`]**. **Transfer** submits one `buy` of the staged amount, and nothing is submitted until it is pressed **[confirmed: the same function]**.
  - **The money panel** **[confirmed: `TAFSupply_ChangeMoney`, [`decompiled-unit-map-orders-and-record-fields.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/decompiled-unit-map-orders-and-record-fields.md)]** moves talents between the army's purse and the national treasury, or an own fleet within one tile (the `via` fleet, picked in the panel when there is one), with T105's `transfer-money <army> <±amount> [via <fleet>]`. It shows the national balance and the army's money **[Wine candidate, the window above]**. Its steps are 10 and 100 talents **[Wine candidate, the window's 10s and 100s arrows]**, and each press submits one `transfer-money` at once **[designed: whether the original stages money is unread]**. The engine keeps its 1,000 ceiling (`economy.purseCapPerUnit`).
  - **The fleet provider reaches the session.** `BuySupplyCommand` already takes a `ProviderFleetId` (T46, #147), but `GameSession.HandleBuy` builds only the city form, and it reads only `ArmySupplyPurchased`, so a fleet purchase would throw there. `buy <army> fleet <fleet> <tons>` (5 tokens) becomes the fleet form: it dispatches `BuySupplyCommand` with `CityId: null` and the fleet id, and prints `<army> bought <n> tons of supply from fleet <fleet>, free.` from `ArmySupplyPurchasedFromFleet` **[designed wording]**. The 4-token city form and its usage line are unchanged; the `buy` help line names both forms.
  - **Feedback.** The dialog submits through the screen's existing submit path (so `MainGameScreen.CommandIssued` counts each command), shows the session's reply line for every command it submits inside the dialog, and refreshes its figures from the new state after each one. A refusal is therefore never silent.
  - **The city panel loses** its "Supply transfer (troop/money slider)" section. Its 0–50 range was a UI literal with no ruleset source (#697's code read; no `_provenance` names it), and Supply army replaces it.
  - **Design calls, each a default [designed]** that the reviewer and the user see in the PR and its screenshots, and that the user may change at the visual review: (1) the two messages' wording above; (2) the provider list as a list of rows, one selected, the first selected on opening; (3) the money panel's `via` choice as a picker that shows "Treasury" first, then each own fleet within one tile; (4) each money press applied at once; (5) the dialog closes with **Close**, Esc, or the overlay's existing close path, and nothing is pending when it closes (a staged paid amount not transferred is dropped).

- **Hazards**:
  - **The human visual review** posts screenshots of a windowed run: the dialog at an own city, at a foreign city with an amount staged, with a fleet as provider, the money panel with a fleet as `via`, and the city panel without the slider. "Looks right" is the user's call (Q-B), and it settles the design calls above; a change the user asks for there is rework of this task, not a new one.
  - **#697 may hide an engine defect.** If an adjacent own-city army's `buy` is refused or changes nothing in the session itself (not only in the slider), that is a bug in merged code: suspend, file it through the bug list, and do not work around it in the dialog ([build-process.md §4.6](../build-process.md#46-bugs-and-follow-ups)). A press that changes nothing because the army is at its room is the original's behaviour, and the reply line says so.
  - **A fleet purchase is free** in the engine, because no report says whether, or whom, the original pays when the provider is a fleet (`BuySupplyCommand`'s remarks, the `[open]` shared with T46 Done-when 8). Do not invent a payment.
  - **T111 and T112 build on this file.** T111's other Army dialogs stay T111's; T112 adds Supply fleet to `SupplyDialog.cs`. Keep the army half self-contained, and the model free of anything army-only that a fleet would need differently (the fleet's room is `ships × 8 − supply`, with no `+1`).
