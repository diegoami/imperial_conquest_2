### Scope

Correct manual treasury-to-army and treasury-to-fleet funding under the faithful per-unit-purse economy. A treasury balance below the requested positive amount, including zero or negative balances, does not reduce the signed purse-cap step. Debit the treasury by exactly the amount credited to the purse. Preserve the existing designed/central-treasury behavior as a negative control; select policy from the loaded rules, not a hard-coded world/ruleset ID.

Do not delete the common balance clamp indiscriminately. A fleet purse funding an army remains limited to the fleet's available money. Reverse transfers remain limited by their donor, and a receiving purse still has its cap. A positive arrow into an over-cap purse retains its signed pullback: the purse returns to its cap and the source receives the excess. Account totals must be conserved.

The canonical public cases include treasury -100, 0 and +50, target purse 100, and request +100. Treasury becomes -200, -100 and -50 respectively, while either army or fleet purse becomes 200. Controls include target purse at 1,000; purse 1,100 pulling back to 1,000; reverse transfer with donor 100 or 50; fleet provider 50 or 0; and reverse transfer into a fleet purse at 950. Use the loaded cap/step policy in production rather than copying these case constants into the helper.

**Caller distinction.** At intake, `SupplyDialogModel.SelectProvider` changes the provisions provider only. The separate money-source picker invokes `SelectVia`; money buttons use `SelectedViaFleetId`. Selecting a supplying fleet therefore does not implicitly select its purse. Prove that the ordinary Supply fleet provider selection still submits a treasury transfer. Do not remove explicit CLI/picker purse-to-purse support or call its seventh projected discrepancy an unintended default UI defect. Any additional fidelity restriction on that extension needs an explicit scope decision.

**Automatic funding is separate.** T207's confirmed-End-turn planner must still respect the configured nonnegative treasury reserve. Its existing budget tests are regression controls, not permission to fund automatically into debt. This task changes neither automatic policy nor recruitment Max behavior.
