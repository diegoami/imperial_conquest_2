- **Scope**: the evidence is the research repository's [`docs/reports/2026-09-29-nation-marker-colours.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-09-29-nation-marker-colours.md). The original draws every city, army and fleet marker as a **square filled with the owner's background colour**, with the glyph in the owner's **foreground colour**. Each nation has its own pair, all from the 16-colour Windows palette. Two pairs share a background (red: Carthage/Media; navy: Ptolemaic/Illyria) and differ by foreground. The backgrounds equal the research inspector's pre-T94 `OwnerColor` table (commit `ca541b1`).
  - **Model.** `NationDefinition` gains `string? GlyphColorHex = null` as its **last** positional parameter, so every existing construction (tests, toy world) compiles and loads unchanged. `colorHex` becomes the **background**.
  - **Data.** The classical world's 16 nations carry the report's backgrounds in `colorHex` and its foregrounds in `glyphColorHex`, with provenance `confirmed:` citing the report. The exporter emits exactly these, so a re-export reproduces the committed world. The toy world is unchanged: it has no `glyphColorHex`.
  - **Map (`GameMapView`).** When a pack texture is drawn:
    - fill the marker's rect with the owner's **background** colour;
    - draw the authored silhouette tinted with the owner's **foreground** colour;
    - when a nation has no `glyphColorHex`, fall back to white or black by the background's luminance;
    - keep the gold selection outline, the size tiers and the no-texture fallback shapes (which use the background colour).
  - **Inspector (`MapViewer`).** `OwnerColor` returns the report's backgrounds again, and its glyph colour uses the nation's foreground where it has one.
  - **`MarkerTint`** gains a Godot-free way to get the (background, foreground) pair for an owner, which the map uses and the tests pin.
