# T244 — The fleet table keeps dead slots until the original's sweep

Triage of #1606, owner decision 2026-10-30: a correction task, and the fleet sweep shares T240's switch. Evidence: [`2026-10-30-army-table-order-after-deaths117.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/325c94c/docs/reports/2026-10-30-army-table-order-after-deaths117.md), addendum 4 (research `325c94c`, conquest PR #264).

**The original:**
- A lost fleet's record stays in its slot, marked dead (owner −1), and a new fleet is appended after every slot.
- The sweep `FUN_0044adb0` swap-removes the army table's dead records (`FUN_0044abe0`) and then, in the same call, the fleet table's (`FUN_0044ad7c`), each from the last index down. So the fleet sweep runs exactly when T240's army sweep runs: when a human ends the turn and before each AI seat.
- In play, 31 End turns lose a fleet. The order was kept in **0 of 31**, and swap-remove reproduces **31 of 31** (storm30 seed 1: `[0,1,2,3,4] → [0,1,4,3]`). In emulation on the #187 RAM, two Carthaginian fleets merge onto the other one when their records are swapped.
- The army figures T240's provenance still quoted from research `150d95d` (18 of 19) are corrected to `325c94c`'s: survivors kept their order in 4 of 43 death turns and the sweep reproduces 42 of the 43.

**The faithful AI** reads the fleet table in order: the fleet merge `FUN_00450b30` takes the first valid survivor and partner, and the fleet order's `< 99` gate counts the whole table, `DAT_004a0326`, dead records included until the sweep.

**The remake** filters a lost fleet out of `state.Fleets` at once and keeps the survivors in order.

## Contract

- **One flag for both tables:** `armyTableCompaction` is renamed `unitTableCompaction`, values unchanged (`swapRemoveSweep` for `classical-faithful`, `inOrder` for the others), in the ruleset model, the four shipped rulesets, the docs and the tests. Its `_provenance` names #1606 and carries the corrected figures above.
- **Dead fleet slots** follow T240's design exactly: `GameState.DeadFleetSlots` beside `DeadArmySlots`, optional and empty by default, kept by a remake save and omitted from an original-SAV export; recorded centrally by the same before/after comparison (`ArmyTable.Record`, already called by the command dispatcher, `TurnCoordinator.RunPhases` and the tactical write-back); swept by `ArmyTableSweepSystem` right after the armies. No removal site is edited.
- **Links need no repointing:** a fleet's `CarriedArmyId` and an army's `AboardFleetId` are ids, not table indexes.
- **The `< 99` gate** (`AiFaithfulEconomy.OrderFleet`) counts the live fleets plus the dead fleet slots, which is the live count under `inOrder`.
- No rule other than fleet order and that count changes. The army-phase fleet loop (#1409) reads the list in order and inherits the swept order, but its decisions are not yet the original's.
