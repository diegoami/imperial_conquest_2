- **Scope**: the evidence for every rule below is `decompiled-unit-map-orders-and-record-fields.md` **[confirmed]** unless marked.
  - **Fleet** (acts on the selected own fleet):
    - **Supply fleet** (`TAFSupply`, against a city or another fleet): `buy-fleet-supply`, plus the money panel with T105's `transfer-money`, against the treasury or a co-located own fleet;
    - **Repair fleet**: a dialog for the points, costing `ships × points / 5`. Only at an own city and not while carrying an army; it zeroes the fleet's moves. Submits `repair-fleet`;
    - **Transfer ships** (`TFleetToFleet`): ships, supply and money, both ways, with T106's `AdjacentPartner.Fleet` as the partner. Submits `fleet-transfer`. With no own fleet at distance 1, it opens no dialog, submits nothing and shows the same no-partner message as Join fleets;
    - **Split fleet** (`TFleetToFleet`, titled *"Split fleet"*, the feature inventory rows UF04 and D04): needs 20 or more ships and no army aboard. Ships, supply and money are moved to the second fleet by hand, as in the dialog described below; the selected fleet keeps at least one ship (the engine's rule). **OK** submits **exactly one** `split-fleet <fleet> <new-fleet> <ships> [supply=<tons>] [money=<talents>]` ([T141](T141.md)'s form), each amount netted toward the new fleet and at most what the selected fleet holds; **Cancel** submits nothing;
    - **Join fleets**: no dialog. It takes the `AdjacentPartner.Fleet` partner and submits `join-fleets` (fewer than 101 ships together, neither carrying an army);
    - **Scuttle fleet**: `ConfirmPrompt` first, then `scuttle-fleet`.
    - **What the original's dialogs look like** **[Wine candidate: [`2026-10-02-fleet-orders-live.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-02-fleet-orders-live.md)]**: Supply fleet lists the providers ("Supplies at Antium: 330"), the fleet's supplies, the national balance and the fleet's money, with 10s and 100s arrows. Repair fleet shows the original state of repair, 1s and 10s arrows, the new state and the cost. Split fleet is a two-column table of the first and the second fleet's ships, supply and money, with 1s and 10s arrows for ships and 10s and 100s for supply and money, the down arrows moving to the second fleet; Transfer ships ("Fleet to fleet transfer") has the same layout. The scuttle prompt is *"Are you sure you want to scuttle this fleet ?"*. The refusals are *"The fleet can only be repaired at one of your cities."* and *"To scuttle a fleet it must be near one of your cities."*
  - **The original's refusal lines, in the order it tests them** **[derived: code; Wine candidate: one play per line, [`2026-10-05-refusal-texts-and-conditions.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/593fe15/docs/reports/2026-10-05-refusal-texts-and-conditions.md) rows R13, R14, R17, R18, R20–R24, research `593fe15`, a provisional report]**: Repair fleet, *"The fleet can only be repaired at one of your cities."* then *"A fleet cannot be repaired while it is carrying an army."*; Split fleet, *"You can not split a fleet containing less than 20 ships."* then *"You can not split a fleet carrying an army."*; Scuttle fleet, *"A fleet cannot be scuttled while it is carrying an army."* then *"To scuttle a fleet it must be near one of your cities."*; Fortify city, *"You cannot fortify a city which is under siege."*, *"This city cannot be fortified any further."*, *"This city is already being fortified."*. A dialog that refuses before it submits (as Split fleet does) shows these lines, the first failing test only; a refusal the engine returns is shown as the engine words it (its wording and order are bugs [#801](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/801) and [#802](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/802), not this task's).
  - **City → Fortify city** (`TFortifyCity`): 0 to (100 − current) points at `population(k) × points`. It is refused under siege or while already fortifying. Submits `order-city … fortify`.
  - **The command strip** (`UnitCommandStrip`), the original's Unit-map toolbar **[derived: code, `TUnitMap_AllButtonsOff`, `ArmyButtonsOn`, `FleetButtonsOn`, `CityButtonsOn`]**:
    - it shows the selected unit's group only: 7 army buttons, 6 fleet buttons or 1 city button, each the `ui.command.*` icon of its table row, plus Cancel selection **[designed]**, which the original's fleet strip also shows **[Wine candidate: [`2026-10-02-fleet-orders-live.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-02-fleet-orders-live.md)]**;
    - a fleet carrying an army shows the 6 fleet buttons, then the 7 army buttons;
    - a foreign unit, or no selection, shows nothing;
    - each button runs its table row, so the strip and the menu share one handler.
  - **The context panel keeps no command button.** With this task the panel is information only (the user's decision that it shows information only).
