- **Scope**:
  - **Show mercenaries → Light infantry, Heavy infantry, Archers, Light cavalry, Heavy cavalry, All mercenaries** highlight on the mini-map every mercenary offer of that type (every type for All), at the position T76 gives it, each type with its own symbol **[derived: code, `TAreaMap_ShowMercs`, bitmaps 4–8]**. The symbol is the type's `unit.<type>.icon` **[designed]**. Each entry is a check item, like the other Show entries (T110). The offers are not scoped by the viewed nation, because they belong to no nation.
  - **Army → Recruit mercenaries** (`TRecruitMercs`): it offers only the mercenaries of the **first city at distance exactly 1** from the selected army. With none, nothing happens **[confirmed: `decompiled-mercenary-offer-list-and-position.md`, `decompiled-unit-map-orders-and-record-fields.md`]**. A hire submits `hire-mercenary`. **The price is a gate, not a charge**: the original refuses the hire when the army's purse is below `(troops × price div 1000) × quality` (*"Your army has too little money to pay these mercenaries."*) and otherwise **takes nothing**, from the purse or the treasury; the first payment is the quarterly pay **[derived: code, `TRecruitMercs_RecruitMercUnit` @ 00441360; Wine candidate: two hires left the purse at 30 and the treasury at 2,270; [`2026-10-05-mercenary-hire-price-is-a-gate-not-a-charge.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/9ae8924/docs/reports/2026-10-05-mercenary-hire-price-is-a-gate-not-a-charge.md), research `9ae8924`]**. The engine deducts the price until [T143](T143.md) (bug #755) removes the charge; the dialog shows whatever the engine's outcome leaves.
  - **Three different numbers** in the original, all from the same quarterly price table **[derived: code, the same report]**: the hire **gate** `(troops × price div 1000) × quality`; the dialog's **"Quarterly cost" box** `(troops × price × quality) div 1000`; and the **actual quarterly pay** `((troops div 200) × price × quality) div 5`, the army panel's "Mercenary pay". They differ: Samnite LI 3,868 q8 gives 24 / 30 / 30, Etruscan HC 960 q9 gives 27 / 34 / 28. A purse of 30 hired the HC while the box showed 34. **The clone's quarterly cost column shows the original's displayed figure**, `(troops × price × quality) div 1000` (34 for the Etruscan horse), not the actual pay **[designed: the user's decision of 2026-10-05, relayed on PR #758]**. The hire's refusal is the engine's gate.
  - **The original's refusals, in order** **[derived: code, `TUnitMap_RecruitMercenaries` @ 00446FF4, the same report]**: no offer city at distance exactly 1 (nothing, no message); the 20th unit slot full (*"This army already has 20 units."*); the army's troops above 100,000 (*"This army cannot get any bigger."*); the city's owner at war (*"You cannot recruit from an enemy city."*); supplies under 15 per 10,000 troops (*"No mercenaries will join an army with so few supplies."*); with a fleet selected, `troops div 500` above its ships (*"Your fleet cannot carry any more troops."*). Only then does the dialog open; per hire it checks the purse gate, then 100,000 troops with the offer, then the fleet's space with the offer. The engine's rules are T76's; the dialog shows the engine's refusals and adds none of its own. On a fleet carrying an army, it acts on the army aboard, like every Army order.
  - **The 20-unit refusal, the landing slot and the recruitment table** **[confirmed: Wine candidate, [`2026-10-08-merc-hire-ignores-queue-r08-guards-slot-20.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-08-merc-hire-ignores-queue-r08-guards-slot-20.md), research `b72cfae`]**. The original's *"This army already has 20 units."* tests the army's slot 19 only, and the dialog does not open then; the hired unit lands at the army's last occupied slot + 1; a full recruitment table (40 slots) does not block a hire, and nothing is charged then either. On the clone's packed unit list these are the engine's 20-unit cap and its append, so the dialog adds nothing for them. **Inside the dialog, a hire that fills the army's 20th unit closes it** (`TRecruitMercs_RecruitMercUnit` :43686, `if (iVar3 == 0x13 …) TRecruitMercs_OK`) **[derived: code, the same report]**: the clone's dialog closes too.
  - **A right click on a city lists the mercenaries on offer there** (the 2026-10-05 amendment) **[Wine candidate: the feature inventory, row UM07, `FI_b1_25_city_right_click.png`, *"There are no mercenaries at Rome"*; derived: code, `TInformation_ShowCityUnits`'s literals *"Mercenaries at "* and *"There are no mercenaries at "*]**. The unit-list view of a city is headed *"Mercenaries at <city>"* and lists every live offer on that city's tile (T76's position), in slot order, with the columns the Recruit mercenaries dialog shows (type, troops, quality, quarterly cost); with none, it says *"There are no mercenaries at <city>"*. It applies to any city, whoever owns it, because the offers belong to no nation; the right click still selects nothing and disarms no order (T99). `MercenaryDialogModel` gives the list, read from the engine's offers, so the view and the dialog cannot disagree. The listing's layout is **[designed]**, settled at the human visual review.
  - **Protects** (for the amendment): the right click on an army or a fleet lists its units as today (T99, T111's quality); `MapClickRules` and `GameMapView`'s right-click routing are unchanged; the city panel a left click shows keeps its garrison line, now the one place the garrison is shown ([T140](T140.md) keeps it too).
- **Done when**:

  2. `MercenaryDialogModelTests`: the offers listed for an army are those of the first city at distance exactly 1, in the order the engine's adjacency rule (T76) uses. An army with no such city gets an empty list, and the dialog does not open. A hire that brings the army to 20 units reports the dialog closed, and an army already at 20 units does not open it. Each offer's quarterly cost is `(troops × price × quality) div 1000`: an HC of 960 troops at quality 9 shows **34** (not the pay, 28), and an LI of 3,868 at quality 8 shows 30.

A second hire, from a purse below that offer's gate, is refused with the engine's reason and changes nothing.

  6. **The city's right click** (the 2026-10-05 amendment). `MercenaryDialogModelTests`: the offers listed at a city are exactly the live offers on its tile, in slot order (a hired slot, troops `0xFFFF`, is not listed), and a city with none gets an empty list. In `MercenariesCheck`, a right click on a city with offers, through the map's real right-click path (`GameMapView`'s `UnitListRequested`), shows the heading *"Mercenaries at <city>"* and one line per offer on the laid-out `ContextPanel`; a right click on a city with none shows *"There are no mercenaries at <city>"*; neither changes the selection. Both fail on `origin/main` (the garrison is listed) and pass after.

  - **Test the hire with a priced offer.** The feature inventory's "free" hire (row UA02, `saves/merc-hire-free-0720.SAV`) was a priced offer whose dialog box reads 0 until a row is selected, and its purse was unchanged because the original charges nothing (the gate report above). Done-when 3 needs an offer whose gate is above zero, so the refused second hire shows the gate is real.
