#### T113 Mercenaries on the map: Show mercenaries and Recruit mercenaries

- **Design milestone**: M18 (the Godot UI). From the UI command audit's Show mercenaries and Recruit mercenaries rows ([`original-ui-command-audit.md`](../investigations/original-ui-command-audit.md) §1.5 and §1.6). Both wait on mercenary offers having a position, which is [T76](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/330). **Amended by the user's decision of 2026-10-05** ([#571](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/571#issuecomment-5994221165), from [#718](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/718)'s row UM07): *a city's right-click lists the mercenaries on offer there, as the original does*, not the garrison (T99's behaviour). **Labels**: `phase:3 lane:ui single-instance release:v0.5.0`

- **Scope**:
  - **Show mercenaries → Light infantry, Heavy infantry, Archers, Light cavalry, Heavy cavalry, All mercenaries** highlight on the mini-map every mercenary offer of that type (every type for All), at the position T76 gives it, each type with its own symbol **[derived: code, `TAreaMap_ShowMercs`, bitmaps 4–8]**. The symbol is the type's `unit.<type>.icon` **[designed]**. Each entry is a check item, like the other Show entries (T110). The offers are not scoped by the viewed nation, because they belong to no nation.
  - **Army → Recruit mercenaries** (`TRecruitMercs`): it offers only the mercenaries of the **first city at distance exactly 1** from the selected army. With none, nothing happens. The hire is paid from the army's purse, and submits `hire-mercenary` **[confirmed: `decompiled-mercenary-offer-list-and-position.md`, `decompiled-unit-map-orders-and-record-fields.md`]**. On a fleet carrying an army, it acts on the army aboard, like every Army order.
  - **A right click on a city lists the mercenaries on offer there** (the 2026-10-05 amendment) **[Wine candidate: the feature inventory, row UM07, `FI_b1_25_city_right_click.png`, *"There are no mercenaries at Rome"*; derived: code, `TInformation_ShowCityUnits`'s literals *"Mercenaries at "* and *"There are no mercenaries at "*]**. The unit-list view of a city is headed *"Mercenaries at <city>"* and lists every live offer on that city's tile (T76's position), in slot order, with the columns the Recruit mercenaries dialog shows (type, troops, quality, quarterly cost); with none, it says *"There are no mercenaries at <city>"*. It applies to any city, whoever owns it, because the offers belong to no nation; the right click still selects nothing and disarms no order (T99). `MercenaryDialogModel` gives the list, read from the engine's offers, so the view and the dialog cannot disagree. The listing's layout is **[designed]**, settled at the human visual review.
  - **Protects** (for the amendment): the right click on an army or a fleet lists its units as today (T99, T111's quality); `MapClickRules` and `GameMapView`'s right-click routing are unchanged; the city panel a left click shows keeps its garrison line, now the one place the garrison is shown ([T140](T140.md) keeps it too).
- **Done when**:
  1. `AreaMapHighlightsTests`: on a scripted state with offers of three types at known positions, each Show mercenaries entry marks exactly its type's offer tiles, and All marks all of them. It fails on `main` and passes after.
  2. `MercenaryDialogModelTests`: the offers listed for an army are those of the first city at distance exactly 1, in the order the engine's adjacency rule (T76) uses. An army with no such city gets an empty list, and the dialog does not open.
  3. The headless `MercenariesCheck` builds the real `MainGameScreen` on that state, hires one offer through the dialog, and asserts that the unit has joined the army and the army's purse has fallen by the hire cost.
  4. `GameCommandTableTests`, `MenuBarCheck`, `NationsAreaMapCheck`, `ArmyOrdersCheck` and `MapClickCheck` pass.
  5. `dotnet build IC2.sln` and `dotnet test IC2.sln` are green, and the diff lists only Owns paths.
  6. **The city's right click** (the 2026-10-05 amendment). `MercenaryDialogModelTests`: the offers listed at a city are exactly the live offers on its tile, in slot order (a hired slot, troops `0xFFFF`, is not listed), and a city with none gets an empty list. In `MercenariesCheck`, a right click on a city with offers, through the map's real right-click path (`GameMapView`'s `UnitListRequested`), shows the heading *"Mercenaries at <city>"* and one line per offer on the laid-out `ContextPanel`; a right click on a city with none shows *"There are no mercenaries at <city>"*; neither changes the selection. Both fail on `origin/main` (the garrison is listed) and pass after.
- **Hazards**:
  - **A new game has no offers** (bug #457), and the quarterly restock is T56. Test on a scripted state with offers, never on a new game.
  - T76 owns the adjacency rule. The dialog reads it from the engine and never re-implements it.
  - **Test the hire with a priced offer.** The one hire in the feature inventory [`2026-10-05-player-facing-feature-inventory.md`](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial-conquest-2-research/blob/main/docs/reports/2026-10-05-player-facing-feature-inventory.md) (row UA02, `saves/merc-hire-free-0720.SAV`, a provisional report) was of a free offer, and no price was deducted. Done-when 3's purse assertion needs an offer whose cost is above zero, or it passes on a hire that charges nothing.
