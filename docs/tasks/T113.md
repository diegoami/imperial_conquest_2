#### T113 Mercenaries on the map: Show mercenaries and Recruit mercenaries

- **Design milestone**: M18 (the Godot UI). From the UI command audit's Show mercenaries and Recruit mercenaries rows ([`original-ui-command-audit.md`](../investigations/original-ui-command-audit.md) §1.5 and §1.6). Both wait on mercenary offers having a position, which is [T76](https://github.com/diegoami/imperial_conquest_2/issues/330). **Labels**: `phase:3 lane:ui single-instance`
- **Branch**: `task/T113-mercenaries-on-the-map` · **Model/effort**: Sonnet / Medium · **Reviewer**: **Opus**/Medium **+ human visual review**
- **Start after**: T76 · **Merge after**: T76, T110, T112. It is **never in flight with** another task or fix under `godot/**`, except an engine task's one-line entry in `godot/UI/CommandVerbCatalog.cs`.
- **Owns**:
  - `godot/UI/AreaMapView.cs` (the mercenary highlights only);
  - `godot/UI/AreaMapHighlights.cs` (the mercenary sets only);
  - `godot/UI/Dialogs/RecruitMercenariesDialog.cs` and `MercenaryDialogModel.cs` (new; the model is Godot-free);
  - `godot/UI/MainGameScreen.cs` (opening the dialog only);
  - `godot/UI/GameCommandTable.cs` (the seven mercenary rows become wired);
  - `godot/Checks/MercenariesCheck.cs`, `godot/Checks/MercenariesCheck.tscn` (new);
  - `tests/IC2.Engine.Tests/Ui/AreaMapHighlightsTests.cs` (the mercenary cases), `tests/IC2.Engine.Tests/Ui/MercenaryDialogModelTests.cs` (new), and the one `<Compile Include>` line for `MercenaryDialogModel.cs` in `tests/IC2.Engine.Tests/IC2.Engine.Tests.csproj`.
- **Scope**:
  - **Show mercenaries → Light infantry, Heavy infantry, Archers, Light cavalry, Heavy cavalry, All mercenaries** highlight on the mini-map every mercenary offer of that type (every type for All), at the position T76 gives it, each type with its own symbol **[derived: code, `TAreaMap_ShowMercs`, bitmaps 4–8]**. The symbol is the type's `unit.<type>.icon` **[designed]**. Each entry is a check item, like the other Show entries (T110). The offers are not scoped by the viewed nation, because they belong to no nation.
  - **Army → Recruit mercenaries** (`TRecruitMercs`): it offers only the mercenaries of the **first city at distance exactly 1** from the selected army. With none, nothing happens. The hire is paid from the army's purse, and submits `hire-mercenary` **[confirmed: `decompiled-mercenary-offer-list-and-position.md`, `decompiled-unit-map-orders-and-record-fields.md`]**. On a fleet carrying an army, it acts on the army aboard, like every Army order.
- **Done when**:
  1. `AreaMapHighlightsTests`: on a scripted state with offers of three types at known positions, each Show mercenaries entry marks exactly its type's offer tiles, and All marks all of them. It fails on `main` and passes after.
  2. `MercenaryDialogModelTests`: the offers listed for an army are those of the first city at distance exactly 1, in the order the engine's adjacency rule (T76) uses. An army with no such city gets an empty list, and the dialog does not open.
  3. The headless `MercenariesCheck` builds the real `MainGameScreen` on that state, hires one offer through the dialog, and asserts that the unit has joined the army and the army's purse has fallen by the hire cost.
  4. `GameCommandTableTests`, `MenuBarCheck`, `NationsAreaMapCheck` and `ArmyOrdersCheck` pass.
  5. `dotnet build IC2.sln` and `dotnet test IC2.sln` are green, and the diff lists only Owns paths.
- **Hazards**:
  - **A new game has no offers** (bug #457), and the quarterly restock is T56. Test on a scripted state with offers, never on a new game.
  - T76 owns the adjacency rule. The dialog reads it from the engine and never re-implements it.
  - The click model and the Army orders' carried-army rule are **[derived: code]**, and an EXPLORE experiment in the original is confirming them (requested 2026-10-01). If it disagrees, that is a follow-up.
